# Multi-Modal Speech Enhancement

**Multi-modal speech enhancement** leverages multiple complementary sensor modalities — such as air-conducted (AC) microphones, bone-conducted (BC) vibration sensors, and inertial measurement units (IMUs) — to improve speech quality in challenging acoustic environments. By fusing signals from different physical domains, these systems can achieve robustness that single-modality approaches cannot match.
