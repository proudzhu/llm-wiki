# Tomohiro Nakatani

**Affiliation**: NTT Corporation, Japan
**Role**: Researcher / Research Supervisor
**Research Focus**: Source separation, dereverberation, speech enhancement, integration of model-based signal processing and deep learning

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Microphone Array Signal Processing and Deep Learning for Speech Enhancement" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2024) — [[sources/haeb-umbach-2024-microphone-array-deep-learning|Haeb-Umbach et al. 2024]].
- Co-author of the weighted prediction error (WPE) dereverberation method (Yoshioka, Nakatani, Miyoshi & Okuno 2011) — joint blind separation and dereverberation by joint optimization.
- Integrating DNN-based and spatial-clustering-based mask estimation for robust MVDR beamforming (ICASSP 2017) — the Class-2 hybrid combining DNN mask priors with EM refinement.
- Blind and neural-network-guided convolutional beamformer for joint denoising, dereverberation, and source separation (ICASSP 2021) — neural source model coupled with model-based convolutional beamforming.
