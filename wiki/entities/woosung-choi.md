# Woosung Choi

**Affiliation**: Queen Mary University of London (at time of Kim et al. 2021); KUIELab, Korea University (PhD, 2021)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Music source separation, latent source analysis, frequency-transformation blocks for spectrogram U-Nets.

## Key Contributions

- Co-first author of "KUIELab-MDX-Net: a two-stream neural network for music demixing" (MDX Workshop @ ISMIR 2021) — [[sources/kim-2021-kuielab-mdx-net|Kim et al. 2021]]
- First author of TFC-TDF-U-Net v1 (ISMIR 2020), the architecture KUIELab-MDX-Net's v2 efficiency modifications build upon; the TDF weight visualization in Kim et al. 2021 comes from his PhD thesis "Deep learning-based latent source analysis for source-aware audio manipulation" (Korea University, 2021)
- Co-author of LaSAFT-Net (ICASSP 2021), cited as the accuracy-focused system that exceeded the MDX Challenge time limit
