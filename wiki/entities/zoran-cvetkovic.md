# Zoran Cvetkovic

**Affiliation**: King's College London, London, U.K. (Reader in Signal Processing at the time of his 2012 paper)
**Role**: Researcher / Reader in Signal Processing
**Research Focus**: Signal processing in the broad sense — from theoretical aspects of signal analysis to applications in source coding, telecommunications, and audio and speech technology.

## Background

Received the Dipl.Ing.El. and Mag.El. degrees from the University of Belgrade, Belgrade, Yugoslavia, in 1989 and 1992, the M.Phil. degree from Columbia University, New York, in 1993, and the Ph.D. degree in electrical engineering from the University of California, Berkeley, in 1995. Held research positions at EPFL, Lausanne, Switzerland (1996), and Harvard University, Cambridge, MA (2002–2004). Member of Technical Staff at AT&T Shannon Laboratory from 1997 to 2002.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "On the Design and Implementation of Higher Order Differential Microphones" (IEEE TASLP 2012) — [[sources/desena-2012-higher-order-differential|De Sena, Hacihabiboglu & Cvetkovic 2012]] — the $(\alpha, \lambda)$ directivity design framework and the complex-root differential array structure.
