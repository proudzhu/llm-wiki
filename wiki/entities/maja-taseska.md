# Maja Taseska

**Affiliation**: International Audio Laboratories Erlangen; Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)
**Role**: Researcher (PhD, FAU, 2017)
**Research Focus**: Multi-microphone speech enhancement, informed spatial filtering, blind source separation, acoustic source tracking, distributed microphone arrays.

## Key Contributions

- PhD thesis "Informed Spatial Filters for Speech Enhancement" (FAU 2018) — developed the [[concepts/informed-spatial-filter|Informed Spatial Filter (ISF)]] paradigm as a unified framework for noise reduction, DOA-informed source extraction, acoustic spotforming, and sparsity-based BSS of static and moving sources — [[sources/taseska-2018-informed-spatial-filters|Taseska 2018]]
- CDR-based a priori speech absence probability for robust non-stationary noise PSD matrix estimation (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2017)
- DOA model-based signal detection for source extraction in the presence of competing talkers (EURASIP J. Adv. Signal Process. 2017)
- Spotforming: data-dependent spatial filtering with distributed arrays for position-selective sound acquisition (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2016)
- Blind source separation of moving sources using sparsity-based source detection and tracking (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2018)
- Recursive (RLS) implementations of informed spatial filters as informed GSCs (IWAENC 2016)
