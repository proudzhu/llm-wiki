# Rong Chao

**Affiliation**: National Taiwan University / Academia Sinica, Taiwan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, state-space models (Mamba) for speech processing, automatic speech recognition.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "An investigation of incorporating mamba for speech enhancement" (IEEE SLT 2024) — proposed [[concepts/semamba|SEMamba]], the first Mamba-based SE system, achieving SOTA PESQ 3.69 on VoiceBank-DEMAND with PCS — [[sources/chao-2024-mamba-speech-enhancement|Chao et al. 2024]]
- Co-author of "One Model, Many Latencies: Universal Speech Enhancement for Diverse Real-Time Applications" (arXiv preprint 2606.25621 (v3), 2026) — one-for-all real-time universal speech enhancement with selectable algorithmic and computational latency — [[sources/fu-2026-one-model-many-latencies|Fu et al. 2026]]
