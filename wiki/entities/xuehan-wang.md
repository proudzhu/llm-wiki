# Xuehan Wang

**Affiliation**: CIAIC and Shaanxi Provincial Key Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Microphone array signal processing, Kronecker product beamforming, adaptive beamforming.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Kronecker Product Adaptive Beamforming for Microphone Arrays" (APSIPA-ASC 2021) — introduced the sum-of-Kronecker-products beamformer representation applicable to arbitrary array geometries and the iterative Kronecker MVDR (KMVDR) beamformer — [[sources/wang-2021-kronecker-adaptive-beamforming|Wang et al. 2021]]
- Co-author of "Steering Study of Linear Differential Microphone Arrays" (IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing, vol. 29, pp. 158–170, 2021) — proved first-order LDMAs non-steerable, derived Nth-order steering conditions, and proposed null-constrained steerable LDMA design — [[sources/jin-2021-steering-study-ldma|Jin, Huang, Wang, Chen, Benesty & Cohen 2021]]
