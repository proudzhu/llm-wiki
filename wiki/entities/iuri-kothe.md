# Iuri Kothe

**Affiliation**: LPS (Signal Processing Laboratory), Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), Brazil (as of 2006)
**Role**: Researcher; M.S. in Electrical Engineering from COPPE/UFRJ (2006)
**Research Focus**: Digital audio processing.

## Biography

Born in São Paulo, Brazil, in 1980. Received the electrical engineering degree from Universidade Federal de Brasília (UnB), Brazil, in 2003, and the M.S. degree in Electrical Engineering from COPPE/UFRJ in 2006. AES (Audio Engineering Society) member.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "High-Selectivity Filter Banks for Spectral Analysis of Music Signals" (EURASIP J. Advances in Signal Processing 2007) — [[sources/diniz-2006-high-selectivity-filter-banks-spectral-analysis-music|Diniz et al. 2006]]
