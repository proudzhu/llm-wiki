# Pepe Gil-Cacho

**Affiliation**: Katholieke Universiteit Leuven, ESAT-SCD, Leuven, Belgium
**Role**: Researcher (PhD researcher at time of 2009 paper)
**Research Focus**: Acoustic howling suppression, adaptive notch filtering, signal processing for PA systems.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Regularized Adaptive Notch Filters for Acoustic Howling Suppression" (EUSIPCO 2009) — introduced the [[concepts/regularized-adaptive-notch-filter|RANF]] method: three parallel direct-form adaptive notch filters with signed regularization ($+\lambda$, $0$, $-\lambda$) whose coefficient convergence/divergence pattern is used as a howling detection criterion, combining the low delay/complexity of ANF-based NHS with a detection capability comparable to FFT-based methods — [[sources/gil-cacho-2009-regularized-adaptive-notch-filters|Gil-Cacho, van Waterschoot, Moonen & Jensen 2009]]
