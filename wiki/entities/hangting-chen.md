# Hangting Chen

**Affiliation**: Tencent AI Lab, Audio and Speech Signal Processing Oteam; previously Key Laboratory of Speech Acoustics & Content Understanding, Institute of Acoustics, CAS / University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (DPT-FSNet, ICASSP 2022)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Acoustic echo cancellation, noise suppression, low-complexity neural network design for speech enhancement, dual-path transformer architectures.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of [Ultra Dual-Path Compression for Joint Echo Cancellation and Noise Suppression](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2023-2302) (Interspeech 2023) — [[sources/chen-2023-ultra-dual-path-compression\|Chen et al. 2023]]
- Co-author of "DPT-FSNet: Dual-Path Transformer Based Full-Band and Sub-Band Fusion Network for Speech Enhancement" (ICASSP 2022) — frequency-domain dual-path transformer, SOTA on VoiceBank+DEMAND and DNS at 0.88M params, done at the Institute of Acoustics, CAS — [[sources/dang-2022-dpt-fsnet|Dang, Chen & Zhang 2022]]
