# Nirmal Kumar Rout

**Affiliation**: School of Electronics Engineering, Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) University, Bhubaneswar, India
**Role**: Associate Professor (at the time of his 2012 TIM paper: Ph.D. candidate at KIIT)
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive signal processing, soft computing, evolutionary computing.

## Background

Received the B.E. in electronics and telecommunication engineering from Sambalpur University (1991) and the M.Tech. in computer science from Utkal University (2001). Lecturer at Orissa Engineering College (1993–2002) and faculty member at ICFAI Institute of Science and Technology, Hyderabad (2002–2007), before joining KIIT University.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Particle swarm optimization based active noise control algorithm without secondary path identification" (IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas. 2012) — [[sources/rout-2012-pso-anc-without-secondary-path|Rout, Das & Panda 2012]], which introduced the [[concepts/conditional-reinitialized-pso|CRPSO]] algorithm for ANC robust to abrupt path changes.
- Co-authored the earlier conference study "Performance evaluation of particle swarm optimization based active noise control algorithm" (SEMCCO 2010), which trained PSO-based ANC using explicit primary/secondary path models — the precursor corrected by the 2012 journal paper's model-free online scheme.
