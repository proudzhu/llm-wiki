# Lei Guo

**Affiliation**: Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing, China
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Stochastic systems, adaptive control, system identification, feedback control theory

## Key Contributions

- Momentum LMS theory beyond stationarity: stability, tracking, and regret (arXiv 2026)
- Foundational work on stability of recursive stochastic tracking algorithms (SIAM J. Control Optim. 1994)
- Necessary and sufficient conditions for stability of LMS (IEEE TAC 1997)
- Exponential stability of general tracking algorithms (IEEE TAC 1995)
- Performance analysis of general tracking algorithms (IEEE TAC 1995)
