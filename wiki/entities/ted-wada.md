# Ted S. Wada

**Affiliation**: Center for Signal and Image Processing, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Robust acoustic echo cancellation based on independent component analysis and integrated residual echo enhancement.

## Key Contributions

- Developed the robust acoustic echo cancellation system (with [[entities/biing-hwang-juang|Fred Juang]]) based on error recovery nonlinearity (ERN) and batch adaptation that updates continuously during double talk without a double-talk detector — "Acoustic echo cancellation based on independent component analysis and integrated residual echo enhancement" (Proc. IEEE WASPAA 2009), cited as [1] in [[sources/wung-2011-residual-echo-suppression-system|Wung et al. 2011]].
- Co-author of "A system approach to residual echo suppression in robust hands-free teleconferencing" (Proc. IEEE ICASSP 2011) — [[sources/wung-2011-residual-echo-suppression-system|Wung et al. 2011]]
