# Hyeong-Seok Choi

**Affiliation**: Department of Intelligence and Information, Artificial Intelligence Institute, Seoul National University; Supertone Inc.
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning-based speech enhancement, phase-aware masking, lightweight real-time neural networks for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Phase-aware speech enhancement with deep complex U-net" (arXiv 2019) — foundational work on complex-valued U-Net for joint magnitude and phase estimation in speech enhancement (cited as reference [2] in TRU-Net).
- First author of "Real-Time Denoising and Dereverberation with Tiny Recurrent U-Net" (ICASSP 2021) — [[sources/choi-2021-trunet-real-time-speech-enhancement|Choi et al. 2021: TRU-Net]]
