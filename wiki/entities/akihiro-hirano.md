# Akihiro Hirano

**Affiliation**: NEC Corporation, Kawasaki, Japan (1989–1998); Faculty of Engineering, Kanazawa University (from 1998)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Digital signal processing — acoustic echo cancellers and adaptive microphone arrays.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "A Robust Adaptive Beamformer for Microphone Arrays with a Blocking Matrix Using Constrained Adaptive Filters" (IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, 1999) — third author on the [[concepts/gsc-beamformer|GSC]] with a [[concepts/coefficient-constrained-adaptive-filter|coefficient-constrained]] adaptive blocking matrix and a norm-constrained multiple-input canceller — [[sources/hoshuyama-1999-robust-adaptive-beamformer-ccaf|Hoshuyama, Sugiyama & Hirano 1999]]
- Co-author of "A real-time robust adaptive microphone array controlled by an SNR estimate" (ICASSP 1998) — the hardware real-time validation of the same robust beamformer design, cited as [19] in the 1999 journal paper
- Received the 1995 Academic Encouragement Award from the Institute of Electronics, Information, and Communication Engineers of Japan (IEICE)
