# Yujie Zhu

**Yujie Zhu** (朱瑜杰) is a master's student at the School of Electronic Information, Wuhan University, China (born 2003, Jiangxi).

**Role**: Graduate researcher
**Research Focus**: Microphone array signal processing, speech enhancement, dereverberation.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Low-Rank Robust Superdirective Beamforming Using Multidimensional Kronecker Products" (Journal of Signal Processing 2025) — [[sources/zhu-2025-kronecker-superdirective-beamforming|Zhu et al. 2025]]
