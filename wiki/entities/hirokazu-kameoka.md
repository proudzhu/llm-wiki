# Hirokazu Kameoka

**Affiliation**: NTT Corporation, Tokyo, Japan; SOKENDAI (Graduate University for Advanced Studies), Japan
**Role**: Senior Research Scientist, Researcher
**Research Focus**: Probabilistic models for audio source separation, NMF, variational autoencoders, speech enhancement.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "A review of blind source separation methods: two converging routes to ILRMA originating from ICA and NMF" (APSIPA Trans. Signal Inf. Process. 2019) — [[sources/sawada-2019-bss-ilrma-review|Sawada et al. 2019: BSS/ILRMA Review]].
- Co-developed the multichannel variational autoencoder (MVAE) framework integrating deep generative priors with ILRMA-style multichannel Gaussian likelihoods for BSS.
