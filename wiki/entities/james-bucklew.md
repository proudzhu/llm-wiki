# James A. Bucklew

**Affiliation**: Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Wisconsin–Madison
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Probability theory, stochastic processes, weak convergence, large deviations.

## Key Contributions

- Weak convergence and local stability of fixed step size recursive algorithms (IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 1993)
- Asymptotic analysis of momentum adaptive filtering algorithms (IEEE Trans. Signal Process. 1998)
- The covering problem and $\mu$-dependent adaptive algorithms (IEEE Trans. Signal Process. 1994)
