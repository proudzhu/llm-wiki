# Anthony Zander

**Affiliation**: School of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Australia
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, virtual sensing, moving virtual sensing algorithms, Kalman filtering for ANC.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored comprehensive review of virtual sensing algorithms for ANC (Algorithms, 2008)
- Developed remote moving microphone technique for tracking moving virtual locations
- Developed Kalman filtering moving virtual sensing method
- Moving zone of quiet for narrowband noise in ducts using virtual sensing
