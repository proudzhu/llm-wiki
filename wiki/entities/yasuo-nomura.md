# Yasuo Nomura

**Affiliation**: Department of Electronics, Faculty of Engineering, Kansai University, Suita, Osaka, Japan
**Role**: Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive signal processing, sound quality control.

## Key Contributions

- Co-developed (with Yoshinobu Kajikawa) the Kansai line of perturbation-based ANC: "Active noise control system without secondary path model" (IEICE Trans. J82-A 1999; ISCAS 2000), its frequency-domain extension (IEICE Trans. E84-A 2001), and the variable-perturbation FDSP ([[sources/kajikawa-2003-fdsp-variable-perturbation|Kajikawa & Nomura 2003, ICASSP]]) that scales perturbation magnitude with error power for ~75× larger step size
- Co-authored the Tokoro/Kajikawa/Nomura follow-ups improving convergence speed and stability of perturbation ANC (weighted control and frequency-domain variable perturbation, 2006–2007)
- Longtime collaborator acknowledged in [[sources/maeda-1999-anc-simultaneous-perturbation|Maeda & Yoshida 1999]], which pursued the parallel time-domain perturbation-ANC line at Kansai University
