# Kyogu Lee

**Affiliation**: Department of Intelligence and Information, Artificial Intelligence Institute, Seoul National University; Supertone Inc.
**Role**: Professor / Senior Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, speech separation, audio source separation, and machine learning applied to audio signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Senior author on "Phase-aware speech enhancement with deep complex U-net" (arXiv 2019) — DCU-Net, foundational complex-valued U-Net for speech enhancement.
- Senior author of "Real-Time Denoising and Dereverberation with Tiny Recurrent U-Net" (ICASSP 2021) — [[sources/choi-2021-trunet-real-time-speech-enhancement|Choi et al. 2021: TRU-Net]]
