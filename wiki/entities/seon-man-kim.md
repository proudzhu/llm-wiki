# Seon Man Kim

**Affiliation**: Institute of Sound and Vibration Research (ISVR), University of Southampton, U.K. (at time of 2014 publication); previously Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), Korea
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Audio and speech signal processing using multi-microphone techniques, non-negative matrix factorization, auditory image models.

## Background

Received a B.S. in mechanical design engineering from Chonbuk National University, Korea (2003), an M.S. in mechatronics engineering from GIST (2005), and a Ph.D. in information and communications engineering from GIST (2013). Worked as a researcher at the Samsung Reciprocating Compressor R&D Group, Gwangju, Korea (2005–2007).

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Direction-of-Arrival Based SNR Estimation for Dual-Microphone Speech Enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2014) — [[sources/kim-2014-doa-based-snr-estimation|Kim & Kim 2014]]
- Earlier work with H. K. Kim on target-to-non-target directional ratio estimation from dual-microphone phase differences (Interspeech 2013) and a hybrid probabilistic adaptation mode controller for GSC-based target-directional speech enhancement (ICASSP 2011) — both precursors to the 2014 TASLP paper.
