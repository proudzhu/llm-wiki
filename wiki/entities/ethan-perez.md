# Ethan Perez

**Affiliation**: MILA, Université de Montréal (at time of publication); Rice University
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Visual reasoning, neural network conditioning mechanisms, and language-model alignment/safety.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer" (AAAI 2018) — [[sources/perez-2018-film-visual-reasoning|Perez, Strub, de Vries, Dumoulin & Courville 2018]], which introduced [[concepts/film-layer|Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM)]], halving state-of-the-art error on the CLEVR visual reasoning benchmark.
