# Erwei Yin

**Affiliation**: Defense Innovation Institute, Academy of Military Sciences (AMS), Beijing, China
**Role**: Researcher, corresponding author
**Research Focus**: Multimodal human–machine interaction (speech, visual, EMG, neural signals), deep learning for speech enhancement and recognition, brain–computer interfaces.

## Key Contributions

- Corresponding author of "DBMIF: a deep balanced multimodal iterative fusion framework for air- and bone-conduction speech enhancement" (Applied Intelligence 2026) — [[sources/wu-2026-dbmif-multimodal-fusion|Wu et al. 2026]]: three-branch multimodal framework correcting AC/BC modality dominance via iterative attention fusion, bidirectional gating and a parameter-shared equilibrium bottleneck
- Co-author of AveSpeech, a comprehensive multimodal dataset integrating audio, visual and electromyographic signals for speech recognition (IEEE Trans. Human-Machine Systems 2025)
- Co-author of LipGen, viseme-guided lip video generation for visual speech recognition (ICASSP 2025)
