**Affiliation**: Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), Beijing; also affiliated with Department of Automation, Tsinghua University (from 2024)
**Role**: Associate Researcher / Associate Professor
**Research Focus**: Speech signal processing, speech recognition and synthesis, personalized voice generation and anti-spoofing, continual learning, multimodal fusion

- Member of the IEEE Signal Processing Society Speech and Language Processing Technical Committee (IEEE SLTC)
- Co-author on noise-robust speech recognition and speech separation works with Cunhang Fan, Jianhua Tao, Ruibo Fu, Zhao Lv
