# Timo Gerkmann

**Affiliation**: Universität Hamburg, Germany — Head of the Signal Processing Research Group; Deputy Head of the Department of Informatics
**Role**: Professor (Senior Member, IEEE)
**Research Focus**: Statistical signal processing and machine learning for speech and audio, applied to communication devices, hearing instruments, audiovisual media, and human–machine interfaces

**Editorial service**: Senior Area Editor of the IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing; Member of the IEEE Signal Processing Society Technical Committee on Audio and Acoustic Signal Processing

**Previous positions**: Technicolor Research & Innovation (Hanover, Germany); Carl-von-Ossietzky Universität Oldenburg (Germany); KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Stockholm, Sweden); Ruhr Universität Bochum (Germany); Siemens Corporate Research (Princeton, NJ, USA)

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Multi-channel Speech Separation Using Spatially Selective Deep Non-linear Filters" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2024) — [[sources/tesch-2024-spatially-selective-nonlinear-filters|Tesch & Gerkmann 2024]], the journal extension of his group's ICASSP 2023 SSF paper with a systematic SSF-vs-DS comparison and robustness/generalization experiments.
- Co-author of "Spatially selective deep non-linear filters for speaker extraction" (ICASSP 2023) [26] — introduced the one-hot DOA-conditioned SSF (Tesch & Gerkmann 2023).
- Co-author of "Insights into deep non-linear filters for improved multi-channel speech enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2023) [22] — [[sources/tesch-2023-insights-deep-nonlinear-filters|Tesch & Gerkmann 2023]]. Proposed the JNF (FT-JNF) architecture, the basis for the SSF and [[concepts/mcnet|McNet]].
- Co-author of "Nonlinear spatial filtering in multichannel speech enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2021) [21] — statistical MMSE analysis showing non-linear joint spatial and tempo-spectral filtering outperforms linear spatial filters in non-Gaussian interference; received the ITG VDE award 2022.
- Co-author of "Unbiased MMSE-based noise power estimation with low complexity and low tracking delay" (IEEE Trans. Audio, Speech, Language Proc. 2012) [44] — widely cited unbiased noise PSD estimator used in many subsequent DNN-based speech-enhancement and separation pipelines.
