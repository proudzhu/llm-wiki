# Lu Lu

## Overview

Researcher at the **School of Electronics and Information Engineering, Sichuan University**, Chengdu, China. Specialises in adaptive filtering, robust signal processing, and active noise control (both linear and nonlinear).

## Research Areas

- [[../concepts/active-noise-control|Active Noise Control]]
- [[../concepts/nonlinear-active-noise-control|Nonlinear Active Noise Control]]
- [[../concepts/volterra-filter|Volterra-based adaptive filters]]
- [[../concepts/maximum-correntropy-criterion|Maximum Correntropy Criterion]] and robust adaptive filtering
- [[../concepts/kernel-adaptive-filter|Kernel adaptive filters]]
