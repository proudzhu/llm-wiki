# Vincent Dumoulin

**Affiliation**: MILA, Université de Montréal (at time of publication); later Google Brain
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Generative models, image stylization, and learned representations.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer" (AAAI 2018) — [[sources/perez-2018-film-visual-reasoning|Perez, Strub, de Vries, Dumoulin & Courville 2018]]. His prior work on Conditional Instance Norm ("A Learned Representation for Artistic Style", ICLR 2017) is one of the Conditional Normalization methods that FiLM unifies and generalizes.
