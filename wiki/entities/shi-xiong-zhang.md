# Shi-Xiong Zhang

**Affiliation**: Tencent America, Bellevue, WA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech separation, audio-visual speech processing, multi-channel beamforming

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "ADL-MVDR: All Deep Learning MVDR Beamformer for Target Speech Separation" (ICASSP 2021) — [[sources/zhang-2021-adl-mvdr|Zhang et al. 2021]] — developed on the Tencent multi-modal multi-channel target speech separation platform.
- Co-author of "Audio-Visual Multi-Channel Integration and Recognition of Overlapped Speech" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2021).
- Co-author of "Multi-modal multi-channel system and corpus for cocktail party problems" (in preparation at the time of ADL-MVDR).
