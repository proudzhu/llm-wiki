# Muhammad Tahir Akhtar

**Affiliation**: Graduate School of Engineering, Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan; Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), Islamabad
**Role**: Researcher / Faculty Member
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive signal processing, online secondary path modeling. Known for developing variable step size LMS algorithms and two-filter ANC structures that reduce complexity while improving convergence.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed the VSS LMS algorithm for online secondary path modeling in ANC systems — uses an inverse step-size strategy (small-to-large) that exploits the decreasing nature of the disturbance signal (IEEE Trans. Audio, Speech, and Language Processing, 2006)
- Developed the two-adaptive-filter ANC structure with MFxLMS, eliminating the third filter used in prior methods
- Also proposed FxAFA-based ANC with better noise reduction but poor tracking properties (2005)
