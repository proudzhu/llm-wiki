# Patrick A. Naylor

**Affiliation**: Imperial College London, UK
**Role**: Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Speech dereverberation, room-acoustics parameter estimation, microphone array processing, acoustic SLAM.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Audio Signal Processing in the 21st Century" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2023) — TC-AASP 25-year retrospective — [[sources/richard-2023-audio-signal-processing-21st-century|Richard et al. 2023]]
- Editor of "Speech Dereverberation" (Springer, 2010, cited as [30] in the retrospective) — the dedicated, highly cited book summarizing a decade of dereverberation research
- Co-authored Acoustic SLAM for microphone-equipped devices exploring/interacting with sound sources (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2018, cited as [14])
- Co-authored "Theory and Applications of Spherical Microphone Array Processing" (Springer, 2017, cited as [15])
- Co-led the ACE challenge for blind room-acoustic-parameter estimation
