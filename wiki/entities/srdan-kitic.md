# Srđan Kitić

**Affiliation**: Orange Labs, Cesson-Sévigné, France
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Sound source localization; sparse and compressive approaches; audio signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "A survey of sound source localization with deep learning methods" (JASA 2022) — [[sources/grumiaux-2022-ssl-deep-learning-survey|Grumiaux et al. 2022]].
