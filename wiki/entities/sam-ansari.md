# Sam Ansari

**Affiliation**: Department of Electrical Engineering, University of Sharjah, United Arab Emirates
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Blind source separation, genetic-algorithm-optimized BSS, multiuser kurtosis, AI-based signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "A survey of artificial intelligence approaches in blind source separation" (Neurocomputing 2023) — [[sources/ansari-2023-ai-bss-survey|Ansari et al. 2023: AI BSS Survey]] — proposes a three-way taxonomy (Classical ML / DL / Evolutionary) of AI-based BSS methods.
- Co-authored "Blind source separation based on genetic algorithm-optimized multiuser kurtosis" (TSP 2023) — applies GA optimization to multiuser kurtosis for BSS.
