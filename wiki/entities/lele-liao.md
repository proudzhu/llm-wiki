# Lele Liao

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Modern Acoustics, Nanjing University; NJU-Horizon Intelligent Audio Lab, Horizon Robotics, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Independent vector extraction/analysis, blind source extraction, closely spaced sources, low-SNR speech extraction.

## Key Contributions

- Co-first author of "Speech extraction under extremely low SNR conditions" (Applied Acoustics 2024) — convergence-region analysis of the OGIVE cost function on real speech and natural-gradient OGIVE variants — [[sources/ruan-2024-speech-extraction-low-snr|Ruan et al. 2024]]
- First author of "Efficient independent vector extraction of dominant source (L)" (J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 2022) — IVE of the dominant source
- First author of "Improvement of independent vector analysis for closely spaced sources" (Applied Acoustics 2023) — IVA robustness for closely spaced sources, cited as the basis for the ≥45° incident-angle setup in [[sources/ruan-2024-speech-extraction-low-snr|Ruan et al. 2024]]
