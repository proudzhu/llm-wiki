# Mina Mounir

**Affiliation**: KU Leuven, Leuven, Belgium
**Role**: Researcher (PhD, KU Leuven, 2020)
**Research Focus**: Acoustic event detection, howling detection, spectral sparsity measures, feature and evaluation design for audio signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Conceived the [[concepts/ninosp2-transposed|NINOS²-T]] howling detection feature, implemented the algorithm, evaluated the dataset, and led the writing of "Robust and early howling detection based on a sparsity measure" (EURASIP J. Audio Speech Music Process. 2025) — [[sources/mounir-2025-robust-early-howling-detection-sparsity|Mounir, Bernardi & van Waterschoot 2025]]
- Previously developed the NINOS² spectral sparsity feature for guitar note onset detection (EUSIPCO 2016), the basis from which NINOS²-T is transposed
- Authored the PhD thesis "Acoustic event detection: Feature, evaluation and dataset design" (KU Leuven, 2020), which formalized the feature/evaluation/dataset design methodology later applied to howling detection
