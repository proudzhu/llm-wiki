# Xinye Yan

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Modern Acoustics, Institute of Acoustics, Nanjing University (2014)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multi-channel speech enhancement for dual-microphone Bluetooth headsets; coherence-based and spatial-pre-separation noise reduction algorithms; speech-distortion-constrained optimal filtering.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "基于双传声器的蓝牙耳机降噪算法" ("Two microphone based noise suppression algorithms for Bluetooth headsets", 应用声学 2014) — taxonomy of coherence-based vs spatial-pre-separation algorithms, SD-constrained optimal filter analysis, and ATF-GSC implementation for BT headsets — [[sources/yan-2014-dual-mic-bt-noise-reduction|Yan et al. 2014]]
