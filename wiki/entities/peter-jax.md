# Peter Jax

**Affiliation**: RWTH Aachen University, Institute for Theoretical Information Technology
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Signal processing, active noise control, and robust controller design. His group at RWTH Aachen works on feedback ANC systems with rigorous robust stability guarantees, including uncertainty modeling and IMC-based optimization.

## Key Contributions

- Supervising research on data-driven uncertainty models (elliptic, convex hull) for robust feedback ANC in headphones
- Co-author on the multi-disk uncertainty model (IWAENC 2022) and the extended journal version (JAES 2024)
- Co-author on DOA dependency investigation of ANC headphones, showing feedforward ANC is DOA-dependent while feedback ANC is not (INTERNOISE 2018)
