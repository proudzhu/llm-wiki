# Jingdong Chen

**Jingdong Chen** (陈景东) is a professor at the Center of Intelligent Acoustics and Immersive Communications (CIAIC), Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, China.

## Research Areas

- Microphone array signal processing
- Speech enhancement and noise reduction
- Beamforming and spatial filtering
- Active noise control

## Notable Contributions

- Co-author of *Microphone Arrays* (Springer, 2023) with Benesty, Huang, and Pan
- Co-author of *Fundamentals of Signal Enhancement and Array Signal Processing* (Wiley-IEEE Press, 2018)
- Pioneering work on differential beamforming with high directivity factors
- SCM reconstruction method for multi-source speech enhancement (Liu et al. 2026)

## Affiliations

- Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), Xi'an, China
