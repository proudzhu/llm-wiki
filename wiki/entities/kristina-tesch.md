# Kristina Tesch

**Affiliation**: Signal Processing Group, Universität Hamburg, Germany (since 2019; doctoral researcher)
**Role**: Researcher (Student Member, IEEE; doctoral candidate)
**Research Focus**: Digital signal processing and machine learning algorithms for speech and audio, with a focus on multichannel speech enhancement and speech separation

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Multi-channel Speech Separation Using Spatially Selective Deep Non-linear Filters" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2024) — [[sources/tesch-2024-spatially-selective-nonlinear-filters|Tesch & Gerkmann 2024]], extending her ICASSP 2023 conference paper with a systematic SSF-vs-DS comparison, blind DoA estimation strategies, and robustness/generalization experiments.
- Lead author of "Spatially selective deep non-linear filters for speaker extraction" (ICASSP 2023) [26] — introduced the one-hot DOA-conditioned SSF via F-LSTM cell-state initialization that this 2024 journal paper extends.
- Lead author of "Insights into deep non-linear filters for improved multi-channel speech enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2023) [22] — proposed the JNF (FT-JNF) architecture with F-LSTM + T-LSTM stacking, on which the SSF and [[concepts/mcnet|McNet]] are built.
- Co-author of "Nonlinear spatial filtering in multichannel speech enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2021) [21] — established via statistical MMSE analysis that non-linear joint spatial and tempo-spectral filtering outperforms linear spatial filters in non-Gaussian interference; received the ITG VDE award 2022 for this publication.
- Co-author of "On the role of spatial, spectral, and temporal processing for DNN-based non-linear multi-channel speech enhancement" (Interspeech 2022) [27]
