# Rainer Martin

**Affiliation**: Institute of Communication Systems and Data Processing (IND), Aachen University of Technology (RWTH Aachen), Germany; now Institute of Communication Acoustics (IKA), Ruhr-Universität Bochum, Germany
**Role**: Senior Research Engineer / Professor
**Research Focus**: Acoustic signal processing, noise reduction, acoustic echo cancellation, frame erasure concealment in packet networks, robust feedback active noise control

## Education

- **Dr.-Ing.** (1996) — Aachen University of Technology (RWTH Aachen), Germany
- **M.S.E.E.** (1989) — Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, USA
- **Dipl.-Ing.** (1988) — Aachen University of Technology (RWTH Aachen), Germany

## Career

- **1996–present**: Senior Research Engineer / Professor, Institute of Communication Systems and Data Processing (IND), RWTH Aachen; later Chair of Communication Acoustics, Institute of Communication Acoustics (IKA), Ruhr-Universität Bochum
- **1998–1999**: Visiting Researcher, AT&T Speech and Image Processing Services Research Lab, Florham Park, NJ, USA

## Key Contributions

- **Noise PSD estimation based on minimum statistics** (IEEE T-SAP 2001) — seminal work on noise power spectral density estimation without a voice activity detector, deriving optimal time-varying smoothing parameters and bias compensation for minimum tracking
- **Spectral subtraction based on minimum statistics** (EUSIPCO 1994) — early work establishing the minimum statistics approach for speech enhancement
- Co-author of "A Psychoacoustic Approach to Combined Acoustic Echo Cancellation and Noise Reduction" (IEEE Transactions on Speech and Audio Processing, vol. 10, no. 5, pp. 245–256, 2002) — joint residual echo and noise reduction via a psychoacoustically motivated postfilter weighting rule — [[sources/gustafsson-2002-psychoacoustic-aec-nr|Gustafsson, Martin, Jax & Vary 2002]]
- Co-author of "Laguerre Filter-based Robust Feedback Active Noise Control" (EUSIPCO 2025) — Laguerre-filter-based IMC control filter design for feedback ANC (order-17 Laguerre matches order-255 FIR) — [[sources/klatt-2025-laguerre-feedback-anc|Klatt & Martin 2025]]
