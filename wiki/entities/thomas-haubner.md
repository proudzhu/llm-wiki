# Thomas Haubner

**Affiliation**: Multimedia Communications and Signal Processing, Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Blind source separation, Bayesian/majorize-minimize optimization for multichannel speech processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Spatially guided independent vector analysis" (ICASSP 2020) — MAP derivation of IVA with a DOA-uncertainty-aware spatial prior over the demixing matrices, solved with AuxIVA-style MM update rules — [[sources/brendel-2020-spatially-guided-iva|Brendel, Haubner & Kellermann 2020]]
