# Changyan Zheng

**Affiliation**: Defense Innovation Institute, Academy of Military Sciences (AMS), Beijing, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Bone-conducted and throat-microphone speech enhancement, deep recurrent and attention-based spectral restoration, multimodal fusion.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "DBMIF: a deep balanced multimodal iterative fusion framework for air- and bone-conduction speech enhancement" (Applied Intelligence 2026) — [[sources/wu-2026-dbmif-multimodal-fusion|Wu et al. 2026]]: three-branch iterative AC/BC fusion with bidirectional gating and a fixed-point equilibrium bottleneck
- Earlier work on throat-microphone speech enhancement with deep BLSTM recurrent networks (ICCC 2018)
- Attention-based bone-conducted spectral restoration with spectro-temporal structure constraints (IEICE Trans. Fundamentals 2019)
