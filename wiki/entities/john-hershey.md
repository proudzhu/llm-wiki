# John Hershey

**Affiliation**: Google Research (at the time of the 2016 paper: Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), Cambridge, MA, USA)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech separation and enhancement, robust ASR, audio-visual machine listening and scene understanding, deep learning for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Improved MVDR Beamforming Using Single-Channel Mask Prediction Networks" (Interspeech 2016) — founding mask-based beamforming paper: single-channel LSTM masks drive the noise SCM of a statistics-only MVDR — [[sources/erdogan-2016-improved-mvdr-mask-prediction|Erdogan, Hershey, Watanabe, Mandel & Le Roux 2016]]
- Known for deep clustering for speaker-independent speech separation (Hershey et al., Interspeech 2016) and the subsequent permutation-invariant training line of work.
- Known for audio-visual speech enhancement and machine listening (AudioSet, "Looking to Listen").
