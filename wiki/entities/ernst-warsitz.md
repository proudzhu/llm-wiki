# Ernst Warsitz

**Affiliation**: Department of Communications Engineering, University of Paderborn, Germany (Research Staff Member since 2001)
**Role**: Researcher (Student Member, IEEE at the time of the 2007 paper)
**Research Focus**: Statistical signal processing, microphone array beamforming, acoustic scene analysis

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Blind Acoustic Beamforming Based on Generalized Eigenvalue Decomposition" (IEEE TASLP 2007) — [[sources/warsitz-2007-blind-gev-beamforming|Warsitz & Haeb-Umbach 2007]]; the origin of the GEV (max-SNR) beamformer with Blind Analytical Normalization (BAN), later the classical kernel of neural mask-based beamforming. Also contributed the BSN post-filter and the single-stage gradient-ascent generalized-eigenvector tracking algorithm.
- Co-author of "Acoustic filter-and-sum beamforming by adaptive principal component analysis" (ICASSP 2005) — the PCA-based precursor of the 2007 GEV beamformer.
- Co-author of "Adaptive filter-and-sum beamforming in spatially correlated noise" (IWAENC 2005) and "Controlling Speech Distortion in Adaptive Frequency-Domain Principal Eigenvector Beamforming" (IWAENC 2006).
- Co-author of "Adaptive beamforming combined with particle filtering for acoustic source localization" (ICSLP 2004) — the DoA-estimation byproduct of response-derived pdfs.
