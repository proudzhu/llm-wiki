# Dennis R. Morgan

## Overview

Senior Member, IEEE. Affiliated with **Bell Laboratories, Lucent Technologies**, Murray Hill, NJ. Known for foundational contributions to adaptive signal processing algorithms, particularly the analysis and derivation of the [[concepts/filtered-x-lms-algorithm|Filtered-x LMS Algorithm]].

## Research Areas

- Adaptive signal processing
- Digital signal processing
- [[concepts/active-noise-control|Active Noise Control]]
- Speech processing

## Notable Contributions

- **FXLMS stability analysis** (1980) — Showed that the FXLMS algorithm can tolerate ~90° phase error between S(z) and Ŝ(z) under slow adaptation
- [[sources/kuo-1999-active-noise-control-tutorial-review|Kuo 1999: Active Noise Control Tutorial Review]] — Co-author with [[sen-m-kuo|Sen M. Kuo]]
- Derived the FXLMS algorithm as the solution to the secondary-path compensation problem
