# Ji Lin

**Affiliation**: MIT (MIT HAN Lab)
**Role**: Researcher / PhD candidate
**Research Focus**: Efficient deep learning, TinyML on microcontrollers, neural architecture search, model compression.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "MCUNet: Tiny Deep Learning on IoT Devices" (NeurIPS 2020) — [[sources/lin-2020-mcunet\|Lin et al. 2020: MCUNet]]; co-designed TinyNAS and TinyEngine to achieve the first >70% ImageNet accuracy on a commercial microcontroller.
- Co-author of runtime neural pruning (NeurIPS 2017).
