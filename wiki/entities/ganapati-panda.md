# Ganapati Panda

**Affiliation**: School of Electrical Sciences, IIT Bhubaneswar, India
**Role**: Deputy Director and Professor (at the time of the 2012 TIM paper); previously Director of NIT Jamshedpur and founder head of the School of Electrical Sciences at IIT Bhubaneswar
**Research Focus**: Digital signal processing, digital communication, soft computing, intelligent instrumentation, evolutionary computing, computational finance, sensor networks, distributed signal processing.

## Background

Ph.D. in electronics and communication engineering from IIT Kharagpur (1981); postdoctoral researcher at the University of Edinburgh (1984–1986). Founder head of the Electronics and Communication Engineering Department at NIT Rourkela. 38 years in teaching and research, 21 Ph.D. students guided, and 300+ refereed publications. Fellow of the National Academy of Engineering, India (2000), the National Academy of Sciences, India (2003), IETE, and the Institution of Engineers.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of the **filtered-s LMS algorithm** for nonlinear ANC (with D. P. Das, IEEE Trans. Speech Audio Process. 2004).
- Co-author of "Particle swarm optimization based active noise control algorithm without secondary path identification" (IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas. 2012) — [[sources/rout-2012-pso-anc-without-secondary-path|Rout, Das & Panda 2012]].
- Co-author of the comprehensive learning PSO for IIR system identification (Majhi & Panda 2009), a related evolutionary-computation contribution cited by the 2012 paper.
