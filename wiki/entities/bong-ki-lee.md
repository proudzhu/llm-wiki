# Bong-Ki Lee

**Affiliation**: Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering, Hanyang University, Seoul, Korea
**Role**: Researcher (Ph.D. candidate at Hanyang University at the time of the 2016 paper)
**Research Focus**: Speech coding, speech enhancement, and machine learning applied to speech signal processing.

**Background**: B.S. in electrical and communication engineering, Hanyang University (Ansan, 2010); M.S. in electronics and computer engineering, Hanyang University (Seoul, 2012); Ph.D. studies at Hanyang University since 2012.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Packet Loss Concealment Based on Deep Neural Networks for Digital Speech Transmission" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2016) — the first DNN-regression approach to packet loss concealment, using two DNNs to estimate log-power spectra and phases of missing frames — [[sources/lee-2016-dnn-plc|Lee & Chang 2016]]
