# Shinji Watanabe

**Affiliation**: Carnegie Mellon University, Language Technologies Institute (at the time of the 2016 paper: Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), Cambridge, MA, USA)
**Role**: Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Automatic speech recognition, multichannel speech processing and neural beamforming for ASR, end-to-end speech recognition, machine translation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Improved MVDR Beamforming Using Single-Channel Mask Prediction Networks" (Interspeech 2016) — founding mask-based beamforming paper: single-channel LSTM masks drive the noise SCM of a statistics-only MVDR — [[sources/erdogan-2016-improved-mvdr-mask-prediction|Erdogan, Hershey, Watanabe, Mandel & Le Roux 2016]]
- Known for multichannel end-to-end ASR (the original neural beamforming inside end-to-end recognition, Watanabe et al., ICASSP 2017) and the hybrid CTC/attention architecture.
- Co-organizer of the CHiME challenge series; creator of the ESPnet speech processing toolkit.
