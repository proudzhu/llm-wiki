# Justin Chan

**Affiliation**: Paul G. Allen School, University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA
**Affiliation (2026)**: Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Low-cost health sensing with smartphones and hearables, machine learning for accessible healthcare and audio.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Semantic Hearing: Programming Acoustic Scenes with Binaural Hearables" (UIST 2023) — [[sources/veluri-2023-semantic-hearing|Veluri et al. 2023]].
- Co-author of Waveformer, "Real-Time Target Sound Extraction" (IEEE ICASSP 2023).
- Smartphone-based hearing screening: detecting middle-ear fluid with smartphones (Science Translational Medicine 2019) and an off-the-shelf otoacoustic-emission probe for hearing screening via smartphone (Nature Biomedical Engineering 2022).
- Co-supervised (with Swarun Kumar, corresponding author) "Active Noise Cancellation on Open-Ear Smart Glasses" (arXiv preprint 2604.05519, 2026) — the first working ANC system on open-ear smart glasses without an in-ear error microphone — [[sources/yuan-2026-open-ear-anc-smart-glasses|Yuan, Liu & Xiao 2026]]
