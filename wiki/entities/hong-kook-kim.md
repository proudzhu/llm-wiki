# Hong Kook Kim

**Affiliation**: School of Information and Communications, Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), Korea; visiting professor at City University of New York (from July 2014)
**Role**: Professor (IEEE Senior Member, M'99–SM'01)
**Research Focus**: Large vocabulary speech recognition, audio coding and speech/audio source separation, embedded algorithms and solutions for speech and audio processing for handheld devices.

## Background

Received a B.S. in control and instrumentation engineering from Seoul National University, Korea (1988), and M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in electrical engineering from KAIST, Korea (1990, 1994). Senior researcher at Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology (SAIT), Korea (1990–1998); senior technical staff member at the Voice Enabled Services Research Lab, AT&T Labs-Research, Florham Park, NJ (1998–2003); professor at GIST since August 2003. Affiliate member of the IEEE Speech and Language Technical Committee; editorial committee member and area editor of *Digital Signal Processing* since 2012.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Direction-of-Arrival Based SNR Estimation for Dual-Microphone Speech Enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2014) — [[sources/kim-2014-doa-based-snr-estimation|Kim & Kim 2014]]
- Earlier work with S. M. Kim on dual-microphone phase-difference-based speech enhancement: target-to-non-target directional ratio estimation (Interspeech 2013) and hybrid probabilistic adaptation mode control for GSC-based target-directional speech enhancement (ICASSP 2011).
- Statistical model-based noise reduction for car interior speech recognition (ETRI Journal 2010) — car-telematics application context for the dual-microphone work.
