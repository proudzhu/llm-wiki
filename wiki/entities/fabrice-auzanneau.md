# Fabrice Auzanneau

**Fabrice Auzanneau** is a researcher at CEA, List (Université Paris-Saclay), France.

## Research Areas

- Distributed binaural speech enhancement
- Speech enhancement for hearing aids
- Neural-network quantization for hybrid SE systems

## Notable Contributions

- RT-Tango: real-time distributed binaural speech enhancement for low-power hearing aid devices (arXiv 2026, co-author)
- Quantized TANGO / MN-TANGO: low-precision inference for a hybrid distributed binaural SE system (arXiv 2026, co-author)
- Multichannel Speech Enhancement Under Low-Latency Constraints: Balancing Quality And Computational Cost (EEAI 2025, co-author)
