# Sina Miran

**Affiliation**: Starkey Hearing Technologies (now Apple Inc.)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Acoustic feedback cancellation in hearing aids, Bayesian filtering for auditory attention tracking, multi-modal signal processing combining audio and inertial sensors.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed IMU-based step-size control for AFC in hearing aids, demonstrating that head movement acceleration reliably predicts feedback path changes (ICASSP 2026)
- Developed real-time tracking of selective auditory attention from M/EEG using Bayesian filtering (Frontiers in Neuroscience, 2018)
