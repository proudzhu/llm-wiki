# Konrad Kowalczyk

**Affiliation**: University of Erlangen-Nuremberg, Erlangen, Germany (2015 era); now Warsaw University of Technology, Poland
**Role**: Researcher / Academic
**Research Focus**: Spatial audio and acoustic signal processing — microphone-array noise reduction, room-acoustics modeling, acoustic parameter estimation; more recently robust ASR and audio-source separation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Residual Noise Control Using a Parametric Multichannel Wiener Filter" (ICASSP 2015) — the generalized [[concepts/parametric-multi-channel-wiener-filter|PMWF]] with residual noise control — [[sources/braun-2015-residual-noise-control|Braun, Kowalczyk & Habets 2015]]
- Earlier work on reflection-coefficient estimation via pseudospectrum matching and virtual-microphone signal generation in noisy environments (EUSIPCO 2013, IEEE/ACM TASLP 2014), with the University of Erlangen-Nuremberg (LMS) group
