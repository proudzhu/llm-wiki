# Unto K. Laine

**Affiliation**: Helsinki University of Technology, Laboratory of Acoustics and Audio Signal Processing, Espoo, Finland

**Role**: Researcher / AES Member

**Research Focus**: Frequency warping theory, FAM/FAMlet functions, wavelet transforms, nonuniform filter banks, auditory modeling.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Frequency-Warped Signal Processing for Audio Applications" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2000) — [[sources/harma-2000-frequency-warped-signal-processing|Härmä et al. 2000]]
- Developed a general theory of frequency warping in terms of FAM and FAMlet functions (1992, 1994)
- Introduced critically sampled perfect-reconstruction nonuniform filter banks based on block-recursive FAMlet transforms (EUROSPEECH'97)
- Co-designed Bark-FAMlet filter banks (NAM'96)
