# DeLiang Wang

**Affiliation**: Ohio State University, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Computational auditory scene analysis, speech separation and enhancement, deep learning for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Supervised speech separation survey — comprehensive overview of DNN-based separation (IEEE TASLP 2018)
- First introduction of deep learning to speech separation via DNN-based subband IBM estimation (2012–2013)
- Two-stage DNN architecture achieving first substantial speech intelligibility improvements for hearing-impaired listeners in noise
- Large-scale training with 10,000 noises for noise-independent speech enhancement
- Attention-based AC-BC fusion with complex-domain CRN and CycleGAN semi-supervised framework (IEEE TASLP 2022)
- CRN for real-time monaural speech enhancement with Ke Tan (Interspeech 2018)
