# Yuki Mitsufuji

**Affiliation**: R&D Center, Sony Corporation, Tokyo, Japan (at the time of the 2020 JASA paper); later Sony Group Corporation / Kyoto University
**Role**: Researcher (Sony)
**Research Focus**: Music source separation, deep learning for audio, audio signal processing, active noise control

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Coherence-Based Performance Analysis on Noise Reduction in Multichannel Active Noise Control Systems" (JASA 2020) — [[sources/zhang-2020-coherence-mcanc-noise-reduction|Zhang 2020]]: algorithm-independent maximum-NR analysis for multichannel ANC via a three-term residual-energy decomposition
- Co-organizer of the ISMIR Music Demixing (MDX) Challenge (2021) — the compute-constrained music separation benchmark evaluated in [[sources/kim-2021-kuielab-mdx-net|Kim et al. 2021]]; co-authored D3Net, one of the compared baseline architectures
