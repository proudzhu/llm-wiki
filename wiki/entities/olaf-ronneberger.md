# Olaf Ronneberger

**Affiliation**: Computer Science Department and BIOSS Centre for Biological Signalling Studies, University of Freiburg, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Biomedical image analysis, convolutional networks for dense prediction, microscopy image processing.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation" (MICCAI 2015) — introduced the U-Net encoder–decoder with skip connections, the overlap-tile strategy, boundary-weighted loss and elastic deformation augmentation, which won the ISBI 2012 EM segmentation and ISBI 2015 cell-tracking challenges — [[sources/ronneberger-2015-u-net|Ronneberger 2015]]
