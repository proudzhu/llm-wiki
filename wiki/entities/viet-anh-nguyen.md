# Viet-Anh Nguyen

**Affiliation**: Crystalsound Team, NamiTech JSC, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
**Role**: Researcher (industry speech-processing team)
**Research Focus**: Real-time neural packet loss concealment, speech enhancement, and bandwidth extension for full-band telecommunication audio.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Improving performance of real-time full-band blind packet-loss concealment with predictive network" (ICASSP 2023) — the full-band recurrent network (FRN), a blind, frame-causal PLC model at 48 kHz whose predictor branch reuses the previous output; outperforms the informed Interspeech 2022 PLC-challenge top performer tPLCnet — [[sources/nguyen-2023-predictive-plc|Nguyen, Nguyen & Khong 2023]]
- Co-authored "TUNet: A block-online bandwidth extension model based on transformers and self-supervised pretraining" (ICASSP 2022) — same co-author group, cited by the FRN paper as prior work
