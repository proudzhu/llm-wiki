# Shiming Zhang

**Affiliation**: Saitama University, Japan (Doctoral Dissertation, 2022)
**Supervisor**: Professor Tetsuya Shimamura

**Research Focus**: Statistical signal processing for bone-conducted speech analysis and synthesis, including robust pitch extraction and AC-to-BC speech conversion.

**Key Contributions**:
1. **WACF-CEP**: Dual-modal pitch extraction combining AC weighted auto-correlation with BC cepstrum, achieving lowest gross pitch error across 5 noise types with 30x faster processing than BaNa.
2. **LS-IIR synthesis**: First-order IIR filter for real-time AC-to-BC speech conversion, validated by LAR distance (0.37→0.18) and listening tests (20/20 correct for male speakers).
3. **Systematic SNR quantification**: ~10 dB BC SNR gain across white, babble, train, factory, and car interior noise with 8 speakers.
