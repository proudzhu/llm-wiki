# Toon van Waterschoot

**Affiliation**: KU Leuven, Leuven, Belgium
**Role**: Professor / Supervisor
**Research Focus**: Acoustic feedback control, acoustic signal processing, speech enhancement, active noise control, sound reproduction.

## Key Contributions

- Supervised the NINOS²-T howling detection research and provided the code for the baseline howling detection features in "Robust and early howling detection based on a sparsity measure" (EURASIP J. Audio Speech Music Process. 2025) — [[sources/mounir-2025-robust-early-howling-detection-sparsity|Mounir, Bernardi & van Waterschoot 2025]]
- Co-authored the comprehensive survey "Fifty years of acoustic feedback control: state of the art and future challenges" (Proc. IEEE, 2011) — the foundational reference for the acoustic feedback problem, NHS, and HD features
- Co-authored "Comparative evaluation of howling detection criteria in notch-filter-based howling suppression" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2010) — the reference survey of state-of-the-art HD features that this paper builds upon and benchmarks against
- Acquired funding for the NINOS²-T research (KU Leuven Internal Funds; ERC Consolidator Grant SONORA, no. 773268)
