# Jacob Benesty

**Jacob Benesty** is a professor at INRS-EMT (Institut National de la Recherche Scientifique - Énergie, Matériaux et Télécommunications), University of Quebec, Montreal, Canada.

## Research Areas

- Microphone array signal processing
- Speech enhancement and noise reduction
- Adaptive filtering and echo cancellation
- Source localization and beamforming

## Notable Contributions

- Co-author of *Microphone Arrays* (Springer, 2023)
- Co-author of *Fundamentals of Signal Enhancement and Array Signal Processing* (Wiley-IEEE Press, 2018)
- Foundational work on multichannel Wiener filter theory
- Differential beamforming with high directivity factors
- Dimensionality reduction for STFT-domain beamforming

## Affiliations

- INRS-EMT, University of Quebec, Montreal, QC, Canada
