# Antonio Bonafonte

**Affiliation**: Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC), Barcelona, Spain (TALP Research Center), at the time of SEGAN
**Role**: Professor / Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech synthesis and recognition (text-to-speech, voice conversion), statistical and neural spoken-language processing; co-author on the early neural speech-enhancement line.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of **SEGAN** — the first GAN-based, waveform-level speech enhancement system, whose single shared parametrization covered 28 speakers and 40 noise conditions — [[sources/pascual-2017-segan|Pascual, Bonafonte & Serrà 2017]]
