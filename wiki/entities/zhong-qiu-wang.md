# Zhong-Qiu Wang

**Affiliation**: Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen, China (previously Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories, MERL, and The Ohio State University)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech separation, speech enhancement, cross-talk reduction, multi-channel signal processing, robust ASR.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed CTRnet for cross-talk reduction via blind deconvolution trained on real-recorded close-talk and far-field mixtures (IJCAI 2026 / arXiv 2026)
- Proposed PuLSS framework using CTRnet-estimated pseudo-labels for far-field speech separation; first neural method to outperform GSS on CHiME-6 (arXiv 2026)
- First-authored "On the compensation between magnitude and phase in speech separation" (IEEE Signal Processing Letters 2021), naming and analyzing the [[concepts/magnitude-phase-compensation-effect|magnitude-phase compensation effect]] — [[sources/wang-2021-magnitude-phase-compensation|Wang, Wichern & Le Roux 2021]]
- Developed the complex spectral mapping framework for single- and multi-channel speech enhancement and robust ASR (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2020–2021), with the DenseUNet-TCN architecture
