# Willem Alexander Klatt

**Affiliation**: Institute of Communication Acoustics, Ruhr-Universität Bochum, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Robust feedback active noise control, filter design under uncertainty, convex optimization for control filter synthesis, Laguerre filter-based control.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Laguerre Filter-based Robust Feedback Active Noise Control" (EUSIPCO 2025) — replaces the FIR control filter in IMC-based feedback ANC with a Laguerre network, preserving convexity while matching order-255 FIR performance with order 17 — [[sources/klatt-2025-laguerre-feedback-anc|Klatt & Martin 2025]]
- First author of "Control Filter Design with Convex-set-based Uncertainty Model for Robust Feedback Active Noise Control" (EURASIP Journal on Advances in Signal Processing, 2025) — dynamic frequency-dependent convex-set uncertainty regions computed during filter optimization, replacing conservative disk models
- Co-author of "Filter Synthesis for Robust Feedback Active Noise Control Using Non-Uniformly Discretized Fourier Spectra" (EUSIPCO 2024, with M. Bürger, R. Martin, H. Puder) — non-uniform unit-circle discretization that allocates optimization resources to the frequency range where feedback ANC is physically feasible
