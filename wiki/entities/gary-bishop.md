# Gary Bishop

**Affiliation**: University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Department of Computer Science
**Role**: Professor

## Research Focus

Gary Bishop is a computer science researcher at UNC-Chapel Hill working on computer vision, human-computer interaction, and assistive technology. He co-authored the widely cited "An Introduction to the Kalman Filter" technical report with Greg Welch. His broader research includes accessible computing and interactive 3D graphics.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "An Introduction to the Kalman Filter" — one of the most referenced Kalman filter tutorials
- Research in accessible computing and interactive 3D graphics
