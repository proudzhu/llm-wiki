# Simon Doclo

**Affiliation**: Department of Medical Physics and Acoustics and Cluster of Excellence "Hearing4all.connects", Carl von Ossietzky Universität Oldenburg, Germany
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Multi-microphone speech enhancement, beamforming for hearing aids and hearables, active noise control, dereverberation, and binaural signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Long-standing research program on multi-microphone speech enhancement and noise reduction for assistive listening devices
- Co-author on the soft-constrained spatially selective ANC framework and its robust extension under secondary path variations
