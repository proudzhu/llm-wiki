# Piero Iared Rivera Benois

## Overview

PhD researcher at the **Department of Signal Processing and Communications**, Helmut-Schmidt-Universität / University of the Federal Armed Forces Hamburg, Germany. Originally from Santiago de Chile.

## Research Areas

- Active noise control for headphones
- Hybrid and pseudo-cascaded control structures
- Adaptive filtering (Modified Normalized FxLMS)
- FPGA-based real-time ANC implementation

## Notable Work

- [[sources/benois-2020-hybrid-pseudo-cascaded-anc-headphones|Benois 2020: Hybrid and Pseudo-Cascaded ANC for Headphones]] — PhD dissertation on combining FF + MVC + IMC into novel ANC control structures
