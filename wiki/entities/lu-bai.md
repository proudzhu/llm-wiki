# Lu Bai

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Modern Acoustics, Institute of Acoustics, Nanjing University; NJU-Horizon Intelligent Audio Laboratory, Nanjing Institute of Advanced Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing, China
**Role**: Researcher (PhD-track, `smail.nju.edu.cn` address)
**Research Focus**: Active noise control (ANC), deep-learning-based ANC controllers, streaming inference for real-time ANC.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Feedback-guided DNN-based controller fusion for robust fixed-parameter active noise control" (arXiv 2026) — hybrid WaveNet + feedback-guided mixture-of-experts of FIR experts, robust to acoustic-path mismatch without online adaptation — [[sources/bai-2026-feedback-guided-anc|Bai et al. 2026]]
- Lead author of "WaveNet-Volterra neural network for active noise control: a fully causal approach" (Mech. Syst. Signal Process. 2025, vol. 241, 113486) — fully causal WaveNet-VNN controller combining WaveNet temporal modeling with Volterra neural network nonlinear modeling for ANC
- Lead author of "An adaptive deep neural network for active road noise control" (J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 2026, vol. 159(4), pp. 3674–3685) — adaptive DNN for active road noise control
