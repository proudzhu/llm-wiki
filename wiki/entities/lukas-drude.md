# Lukas Drude

**Affiliation**: University of Paderborn, Department of Communications Engineering, Paderborn, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Neural mask estimation for beamforming, hybrid model-based/data-driven speech enhancement

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Neural Network Based Spectral Mask Estimation for Acoustic Beamforming" (ICASSP 2016) — [[sources/heymann-2016-neural-spectral-mask-beamforming|Heymann, Drude & Haeb-Umbach 2016]]; the neural mask + GEV/MVDR beamforming front-end that originated the CHiME-challenge-winning systems.
- Co-author of the LSTM-supported GEV beamformer front-end for the 3rd CHiME challenge (ASRU 2015).
