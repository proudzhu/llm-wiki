# Mike Davies

**Affiliation**: Neuromorphic Computing Lab, Intel Labs
**Role**: Researcher / Lab leadership
**Research Focus**: Neuromorphic computing architecture, Loihi chip family, spiking neural networks.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "The Intel Neuromorphic DNS Challenge" (arXiv 2023) — [[sources/timcheck-2023-intel-neuromorphic-dns-challenge|Timcheck et al. 2023]].
- Lead author of "Advancing neuromorphic computing with Loihi: A survey of results and outlook" (Proceedings of the IEEE 2021) — the foundational Loihi 1 survey cited as reference [1] in the N-DNS Challenge paper, establishing the Loihi architecture's compute model that the challenge targets for extension via Loihi 2.
