# Luiz W. P. Biscainho

**Affiliation**: DEL/Poli and PEE/COPPE, Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), Brazil
**Role**: Associate Professor
**Research Focus**: Digital signal processing, particularly audio processing and adaptive systems.

## Biography

Born in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, in 1962. Received the electrical engineering degree (magna cum laude) from EE (now Poli) at UFRJ in 1985, and the M.S. and D.S. degrees in electrical engineering from COPPE/UFRJ in 1990 and 2000, respectively. Member of IEEE, AES (Audio Engineering Society), and SBrT (Brazilian Telecommunications Society).

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "High-Selectivity Filter Banks for Spectral Analysis of Music Signals" (EURASIP J. Advances in Signal Processing 2007) — [[sources/diniz-2006-high-selectivity-filter-banks-spectral-analysis-music|Diniz et al. 2006]]
