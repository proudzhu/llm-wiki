# Rodrigo C. de Lamare

## Overview

Researcher at **CETUC, Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio)**, Brazil. Works on adaptive signal processing, communications, distributed estimation, robust filtering, and active noise control.

## Research Areas

- Adaptive filtering and parameter estimation
- Distributed and cooperative signal processing
- Reduced-rank adaptive filtering, beamforming, MIMO systems
- Sparse adaptive algorithms
- [[../concepts/active-noise-control|Active Noise Control]]
- [[../concepts/nonlinear-active-noise-control|Nonlinear Active Noise Control]]
- Wireless and acoustic sensor networks

## Key Contributions

- Sparsity-aware pseudo affine projection algorithm for ANC (2014)
- Co-author of two-part comprehensive ANC surveys (Part I — Linear Systems, Part II — Nonlinear Systems, 2021)
