# Liang Wenfeng (梁文锋)

**Role**: Founder, [[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]]
**Location**: China
**Background**: Self-described "ordinary university graduate" (not from a top-tier school); cites Jack Welch (former GE CEO) on vision-driven organization as a key early influence. Founded DeepSeek ~2024 — two years before the 2026 investor exchange meeting.

## Key Contributions

- Founded [[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]] (~2024) — Chinese AI research lab known for open-weight frontier LLMs
- Led DeepSeek through the Spring Festival 2025 viral moment (DeepSeek-R1) without pivoting to a C-end monetization strategy
- Articulated a distinctive **AGI staircase roadmap** (language models → CoT → Agent → Continuous learning → Self-iterating singularity → Embodied AI) — see [[concepts/agi-roadmap-staircase|AGI Roadmap Staircase]]
- Established DeepSeek's pricing benchmark: "ten-month payback / 6× profit margin" over hardware cost — see [[concepts/restraint-as-strategy|Restraint as Strategy]]
- Authorized open-sourcing of DeepSeek's strongest models as a deliberate strategic choice (not forced), articulated in the 2026 investor meeting

## Public Statements

The investor exchange meeting recorded 2026-05-20 (transcribed 2026-07-16) is the most extensive first-person strategic statement from Liang in the public record. See [[sources/liang-wenfeng-investor-exchange-meeting|Liang Wenfeng 2026: Investor Exchange Meeting]] for the full content.

Notable positions expressed:

- Open-source of frontier models is non-negotiable and "original intent, not forced"
- The US–China AI gap is primarily a **resource (compute) gap, not a talent gap**
- AGI is reachable only via continuous learning, then self-iteration — not via world models or video generation
- Restraint is "a strategy, not a sacrifice" — the company that takes less profit wins the larger game
- The only non-negotiable core interest is **team stability**
