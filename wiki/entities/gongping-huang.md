# Gongping Huang

**Gongping Huang** (黄公平) is a researcher at the School of Electronic Information, Wuhan University, China.

## Research Areas

- Microphone array signal processing
- Differential beamforming
- Speech enhancement

## Notable Contributions

- Robust differential beamforming with high directivity factors
- Dimensionality reduction for STFT-domain beamforming
- Advances in microphone array processing (ICASSP 2025 overview)
- SCM reconstruction method for multi-source speech enhancement (Liu et al. 2026)
