# Sven Nordholm

**Affiliation**: The University of Western Australia / Western Australian Telecommunications Research Institute (WATRI), Crawley, Australia (at time of 2004 publication); WATRI is a joint venture between The University of Western Australia and Curtin University of Technology
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Microphone array signal processing, subband beamforming, speech enhancement, hands-free communication.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author (with N. Grbić) of "Soft constrained subband beamforming for handsfree speech enhancement" (ICASSP 2002) — a subband beamforming approach to hands-free enhancement cited as reference [2] in [[sources/low-2004-hybrid-bss-anc|Low & Nordholm 2004]]
- Co-author of "A hybrid speech enhancement system employing blind source separation and adaptive noise cancellation" (NORSIG 2004) — [[sources/low-2004-hybrid-bss-anc|Low & Nordholm 2004]]
- Earlier work on improved spatial filters for blocking matrices (Claesson & Nordholm 1992), in the lineage of robust adaptive beamforming surveyed by [[sources/hoshuyama-1999-robust-adaptive-beamformer-ccaf|Hoshuyama, Sugiyama & Hirano 1999]]
