# Enzo De Sena

**Affiliation**: King's College London, London, U.K. (Ph.D. in electronic engineering, at the time of his 2012 paper)
**Role**: Researcher (audio signal processing)
**Research Focus**: Multichannel audio systems, psychoacoustics of spatial hearing, room acoustics simulation, and microphone array processing.

## Background

Received the B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees (cum laude) in telecommunications engineering from the Università degli Studi di Napoli "Federico II", Napoli, Italy, in 2007 and 2009, respectively.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "On the Design and Implementation of Higher Order Differential Microphones" (IEEE TASLP 2012) — [[sources/desena-2012-higher-order-differential|De Sena, Hacihabiboglu & Cvetkovic 2012]] — the $(\alpha, \lambda)$ directivity design framework and the complex-root differential array structure.
