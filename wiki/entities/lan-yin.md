# Lan Yin

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Noise and Vibration Research, Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

**Role**: Researcher in active noise control

## Contributions

- Proposed FRM-SFANC algorithm for selective fixed-filter ANC in headphones based on frequency response matching, providing a computationally efficient alternative to CNN-based filter selection
- Formulated the SFANC problem as a hidden Markov model, deriving theoretical justification for filter selection based on reference signal likelihood
