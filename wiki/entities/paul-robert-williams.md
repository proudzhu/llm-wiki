# Paul Robert Williams

**Affiliation**: Harman International Industries Limited (Chester, GB); resident of Stevenage (GB)
**Role**: Inventor
**Research Focus**: Automatic acoustic-feedback detection and suppression for sound-reinforcement systems; digital signal processing for live-sound and PA applications.

## Key Contributions

- Inventor of "System for elimination of acoustic feedback" (U.S. Patent 8,634,575 B2, granted 2014; divisional of application Ser. No. 09/658,538 filed Sep. 9, 2000) — [[sources/williams-2014-acoustic-feedback-elimination|Williams 2014]]. The patent introduces [[concepts/ballistics-based-howling-detection|ballistics-based howling detection]] (asymmetric per-FFT-bin attack/release with frequency-dependent time constants) and [[concepts/trial-and-verify-notch-insertion|trial-and-verify notch insertion]] as a concrete [[concepts/notch-filter-based-howling-suppression|NHS]] implementation for PA systems.
