# Christopher Montgomery

**Affiliation**: Amazon Web Services, Palo Alto, CA, USA (formerly Xiph.Org / Mozilla)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Open-source audio codecs, neural audio coding, packet loss concealment.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Real-Time Packet Loss Concealment With Mixed Generative and Predictive Model" (INTERSPEECH 2022, pp. 570–574) — 2nd place, Interspeech 2022 Audio Deep PLC Challenge — [[sources/valin-2022-real-time-plc|Valin et al. 2022]]
- Founder of the Xiph.Org Foundation and lead architect of open-source multimedia codecs including Vorbis, Theora, and Opus
- Co-author of the Opus audio codec specification (IETF RFC 6716, 2012), together with [[entities/jean-marc-valin|Jean-Marc Valin]], Koen Vos, and [[entities/timothy-b-terriberry|Timothy B. Terriberry]]
