# Joan Serrà

**Affiliation**: Telefónica Research, Barcelona, Spain, at the time of SEGAN
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Audio and music signal processing, generative models for audio, deep learning for music information retrieval and speech.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of **SEGAN** — the first application of generative adversarial networks to speech enhancement, operating directly on the 16 kHz waveform with a convolutional encoder–decoder generator and a least-squares adversarial objective with an $L_1$ anchor — [[sources/pascual-2017-segan|Pascual, Bonafonte & Serrà 2017]]
