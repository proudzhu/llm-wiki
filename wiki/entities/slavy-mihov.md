# Slavy Mihov

**Affiliation**: Technical University of Sofia, Sofia 1000, Bulgaria
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Mathematical optimization for microphone-array signal processing; constrained beamformer design and parameter optimization for speech-enhancement systems on small devices.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Sound Capture System and Spatial Filter for Small Devices" (2008) — contributed the steepest-gradient-descent optimization of the beamformer weights (front-back-difference maximization) and the eight offline-tunable parameters (adaptation time constants and feature gains) of the probability-based spatial filter — [[sources/tashev-2008-sound-capture-spatial-filter|Tashev et al. 2008]]
- Co-authored "Enhanced Sound Capture System for Small Devices" (ICEST 2008, Niš, Serbia) — companion paper cited as ref. [5] in the 2008 paper
