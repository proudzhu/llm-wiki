# Katerina Zmolikova

**Affiliation**: Brno University of Technology, Faculty of Information Technology, Czech Republic (at time of 2023 overview); previously NTT Communication Science Laboratories, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Target speech extraction, speaker-aware beamforming, multi-modal speech processing

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Neural Target Speech Extraction: An Overview" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2023) — [[sources/zmolikova-2023-neural-target-speech-extraction-overview|Zmolikova et al. 2023]], a comprehensive review unifying audio, visual, and spatial clue TSE under a single neural framework.
- Speaker-aware neural network based beamformer for speaker extraction in speech mixtures (Interspeech 2017, [30])
- Learning speaker representation for neural network based multichannel speaker extraction (ASRU 2017, [41])
- PhD thesis "Neural target speech extraction" (Brno University of Technology, 2022) [52]
