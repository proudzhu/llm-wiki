# Kelan Kuang

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Noise and Vibration Research, Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, bone-conducted speech processing, multi-modal fusion, deep learning for acoustics

## Key Contributions

- Lightweight speech enhancement network fusing bone- and air-conducted speech (JASA 2024)
- DenGCAN backbone — densely gated convolutional attention network with AG skip-connections and sConformer bottleneck
- sConformer (squeezed Conformer) for efficient temporal dependency modeling, co-authored in GCAN (JASA 2023)
- First author of "Low-Complexity BC-Aided Speech Enhancement Leveraging Sub-Band and Full-Band Architecture" (Applied Acoustics 2025) — low-complexity BC-aided speech enhancement with sub-band ERB gains + full-band deep filtering, 40.3 MMACs/s — [[sources/kuang-2025-low-complexity-bc-speech-enhancement|Kuang, Yang, Wang & Yang 2025]]
- Co-author of "Multi-Modal Deep-Filtering-Based Speech Enhancement via Bone-Conducted Speech" (ICSPCC 2025) — Multi-modal deep-filtering network (MDFNet) fusing BC and AC speech inside the deep-filtering coefficients, with ERB-gain high-frequency branch and ASR-best CER at low SNR — [[sources/wang-2025-multi-modal-deep-filtering-bc|Wang, Kuang, Li, Yang, Bai & Liu 2025]]
