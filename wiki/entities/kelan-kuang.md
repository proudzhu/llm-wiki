**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Noise and Vibration Research, Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, bone-conducted speech processing, multi-modal fusion, deep learning for acoustics

- DenGCAN backbone — densely gated convolutional attention network with AG skip-connections and sConformer bottleneck
