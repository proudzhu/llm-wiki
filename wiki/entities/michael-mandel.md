# Michael Mandel

**Affiliation**: Brooklyn College, City University of New York (CUNY), New York, NY, USA
**Role**: Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Spatial audio and source separation, binaural hearing, machine listening, deep learning for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Improved MVDR Beamforming Using Single-Channel Mask Prediction Networks" (Interspeech 2016) — founding mask-based beamforming paper: single-channel LSTM masks drive the noise SCM of a statistics-only MVDR — [[sources/erdogan-2016-improved-mvdr-mask-prediction|Erdogan, Hershey, Watanabe, Mandel & Le Roux 2016]]
- Known for ideal ratio mask estimation for binaural speech separation (Mandel et al., ICASSP 2010) and model-based EM approaches to source separation.
