# Aki Härmä

**Affiliation**: Helsinki University of Technology, Laboratory of Acoustics and Audio Signal Processing, Espoo, Finland (at time of publication); later Maastricht University

**Role**: Researcher / AES Member

**Research Focus**: Frequency-warped signal processing, audio coding, warped linear prediction, adaptive filtering, speech and audio DSP.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Frequency-Warped Signal Processing for Audio Applications" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2000) — [[sources/harma-2000-frequency-warped-signal-processing|Härmä et al. 2000]] — a tutorial surveying frequency-warping methodology and its audio applications
- Developed implementation techniques for recursive filters with delay-free loops (ICASSP 1998; Signal Processing 2000), enabling directly realizable WIIR structures
- Designed the WLP-based audio codec evaluated in the listening tests of the 2000 tutorial
- Co-developed a free MATLAB toolbox for frequency-warped signal processing
