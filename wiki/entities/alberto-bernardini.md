# Alberto Bernardini

**Affiliation**: Dipartimento di Elettronica, Informazione e Bioingegneria (DEIB), Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
**Role**: Researcher (Member, IEEE)
**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, physics-based sound synthesis and virtual analog modeling, deep learning for audio, networked music performance.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Hybrid Packet Loss Concealment for Real-Time Networked Music Applications" (IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing 2024) — co-developed **PARCnet**, which casts music PLC as learning the residual of an online autoregressive predictor — [[sources/mezza-2024-hybrid-plc|Mezza et al. 2024]]
