# Noboru Harada

**Affiliation**: NTT Inc., Tokyo, Japan (Senior Distinguished Researcher); Visiting Professor, Hokkaido University, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: High-efficiency speech/audio coding, lossless compression, acoustic event and anomaly-sound detection, and acoustic device design for open-ear headphones.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Designing an Open-Back Enclosure to Mitigate Sound Leakage for Open-Ear Headphones" (IEEE Access 2026) — [[sources/chiba-2026-open-back-enclosure-leakage|Chiba, Kako & Harada 2026]]: a single-driver enclosure with a back air cavity and four side-openings that emits opposite-phase (dipole) radiation to cut open-ear sound leakage by 7.6 dB on average over 1–7 kHz.
- Co-author of adaptive post-filtering controlled by pitch frequency for CELP-based speech coders (Asilomar 2014, with Chiba, Kamamoto, Moriya, Miyabe, Yamada & Makino).
- Long-running NTT research line on speech/audio coding, lossless compression, and acoustic event detection, including anomaly-sound detection.
