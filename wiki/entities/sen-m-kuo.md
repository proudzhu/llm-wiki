# Sen M. Kuo

## Overview

Professor of Electrical Engineering at **Northern Illinois University**, DeKalb, IL. Widely recognized authority on [[concepts/active-noise-control|Active Noise Control]] and author of the seminal textbook "Active Noise Control Systems: Algorithms and DSP Implementations" (Wiley, 1996).

## Research Areas

- [[concepts/active-noise-control|Active Noise Control]]
- Adaptive signal processing
- Digital signal processing (DSP) applications
- [[concepts/filtered-x-lms-algorithm|Filtered-x LMS Algorithm]]

## Notable Work

- [[sources/kuo-1999-active-noise-control-tutorial-review|Kuo 1999: Active Noise Control Tutorial Review]] — One of the most cited ANC tutorial papers (with [[dennis-r-morgan|Dennis R. Morgan]])
- "Active Noise Control Systems: Algorithms and DSP Implementations" (textbook)
- First author of "Active Noise Control System for Headphone Applications" (IEEE Trans. Control Systems Technology, 2006) — designed and implemented the adaptive feedback ANC headphone with error-microphone placement optimization and music-based secondary-path identification, outperforming a Bose QC2 — [[sources/kuo-2006-anc-headphones|Kuo, Mitra & Gan 2006]]
