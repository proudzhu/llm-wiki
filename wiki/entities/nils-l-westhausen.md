# Nils L. Westhausen

**Affiliation**: University of Oldenburg, Department of Medical Physics and Acoustics, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Real-time neural speech enhancement, packet loss concealment, low-complexity deep-learning models for communication audio.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "tPLCnet: Real-time Deep Packet Loss Concealment in the Time Domain Using a Short Temporal Context" (Interspeech 2022) — [[sources/westhausen-2022-tplcnet|Westhausen & Meyer 2022]] — a seq2one BGRU model for on-demand PLC that ranked 3rd (tied) in the Interspeech 2022 Audio Deep PLC Challenge.
- Co-authored the Dual-signal Transformation LSTM Network (DTLN) for real-time noise suppression (Interspeech 2020) and its application to acoustic echo cancellation (ICASSP 2021) — a widely used fully-data-driven AEC/SE baseline (see [[concepts/dtln|DTLN]]).
