# Haoxin Ruan

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Modern Acoustics, Nanjing University; NJU-Horizon Intelligent Audio Lab, Horizon Robotics, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Blind source extraction, speech enhancement under extremely low SNR, independent vector extraction, natural-gradient optimization.

## Key Contributions

- Co-first author of "Speech extraction under extremely low SNR conditions" (Applied Acoustics 2024) — convergence-region analysis of the OGIVE cost function on real speech and natural-gradient OGIVE variants (OGIVEa_NG/OGIVEw_NG) — [[sources/ruan-2024-speech-extraction-low-snr|Ruan et al. 2024]]
- Released the paper's audio samples at <https://github.com/hxruan-cpp/lowSNR-audio-samples>
