# Justin Salamon

**Affiliation**: Adobe Research (previously New York University, Music and Audio Research Lab)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Sound event detection, environmental audio analysis, bioacoustics, PCEN, audio frontends.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Per-Channel Energy Normalization: Why and How" (IEEE SPL 2019) — [[sources/lostanlen-2019-pcen-why-and-how|Lostanlen et al. 2019]]
- Deep convolutional neural networks and data augmentation for environmental sound classification (IEEE SPL 2017)
- Fusing shallow and deep learning for bioacoustic bird species classification (ICASSP 2017)
- Co-developed BirdVox flight-call detection datasets and benchmarks
