# Greg Welch

**Affiliation**: University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Department of Computer Science
**Role**: Professor

## Research Focus

Greg Welch is a computer science researcher at UNC-Chapel Hill whose work spans tracking systems, augmented reality, and Kalman filtering. He co-authored the widely cited technical report "An Introduction to the Kalman Filter" (TR 95-041), which has become the standard accessible tutorial on the subject. His research emphasizes practical applications of state estimation in computer vision and 3D tracking.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "An Introduction to the Kalman Filter" — one of the most referenced Kalman filter tutorials
- Research in optoelectronic tracking and 3D user interfaces
