# Danielle Moreau

**Affiliation**: School of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide, Australia
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Virtual sensing algorithms for active noise control, stochastically optimal tonal diffuse field virtual sensing, active headrest systems.

## Key Contributions

- First comprehensive review of virtual sensing algorithms for ANC (Algorithms, 2008) — systematically categorized and compared 9 virtual sensing methods
- Developed stochastically optimal tonal diffuse field virtual sensing technique for pure-tone diffuse sound fields
- Active noise control with virtual acoustic sensors in diffuse field environments
