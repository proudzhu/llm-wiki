# Ziling Huang

**Affiliation**: Shanghai Normal University, Shanghai, China; MiLM Plus, Xiaomi Inc., Beijing, China
**Role**: Researcher (first author, † primary contributor)
**Research Focus**: Target speech extraction (TSE), embedding-free personalized speech enhancement, noise-robust multi-speaker extraction.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Lightweight Speech Enhancement Guided Target Speech Extraction in Noisy Multi-Speaker Scenarios" (arXiv 2026) — proposed LGTSE and D-LGTSE frameworks integrating [[concepts/gtcrn|GTCRN]] as a lightweight denoiser for noise-agnostic enrollment guidance and distortion-aware training; [[sources/huang-2026-lightweight-speech-enhancement-guided-target-speech-extraction|Huang et al. 2026]].
- Previously proposed [[concepts/sef-pnet|SEF-PNet]], the embedding-free personalized speech enhancement backbone (ICASSP 2025) that serves as the baseline for LGTSE/D-LGTSE.
