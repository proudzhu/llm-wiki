# G. W. Stewart

**G. W. (Pete) Stewart** is a numerical analyst and matrix computations researcher at the **University of Maryland, College Park**. He is a leading authority on [[concepts/singular-value-decomposition|singular value decomposition]] and matrix perturbation theory.

## Contributions

- Author of the historical survey [[sources/stewart-1993-early-history-svd|On the Early History of the Singular Value Decomposition]] (SIAM Review, 1993), which traces the SVD's origins through the work of [[entities/eugenio-beltrami|Beltrami]], [[entities/camille-jordan|Jordan]], [[entities/james-joseph-sylvester|Sylvester]], [[entities/erhard-schmidt|Schmidt]], and [[entities/hermann-weyl|Weyl]].
- Contributor to the CS decomposition (Stewart 1977) — a tool used in perturbation theory for invariant subspaces.
- Author of textbooks on matrix computations and numerical linear algebra.
