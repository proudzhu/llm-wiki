# Harm de Vries

**Affiliation**: MILA, Université de Montréal (at time of publication)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multimodal language-vision learning, visual question answering, and neural conditioning.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer" (AAAI 2018) — [[sources/perez-2018-film-visual-reasoning|Perez, Strub, de Vries, Dumoulin & Courville 2018]], which introduced [[concepts/film-layer|Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM)]]. His earlier work on modulating early visual processing by language (Conditional Batch Norm for VQA, NIPS 2017) is one of the Conditional Normalization methods FiLM generalizes.
