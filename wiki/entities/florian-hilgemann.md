# Florian Hilgemann

**Affiliation**: RWTH Aachen University, Institute for Theoretical Information Technology
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Robust feedback active noise control in headphones, data-driven uncertainty modeling, and controller optimization. His work focuses on reducing conservatism in conventional uncertainty models to improve ANC performance while maintaining robust stability guarantees.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed elliptic and convex hull uncertainty models for feedback ANC that achieve 10–18 dB more attenuation than conventional disk models
- Previously introduced the multi-disk uncertainty model (IWAENC 2022) as an intermediate step toward less conservative designs
