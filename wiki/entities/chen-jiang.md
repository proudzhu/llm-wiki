# Chen Jiang

**Affiliation**: School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Jiaotong University, Beijing, China
**Role**: Researcher (first author)
**Research Focus**: Lightweight speech enhancement, state-space models, depthwise separable convolutions, multi-scale feature learning.

## Key Contributions

- Co-first authored "Lightweight Speech Enhancement with State-Space Model and Depthwise Separable Convolution" (*Digital Signal Processing*, 2026) — [[sources/jiang-2026-lightweight-speech-enhancement-ssm-dsc|Jiang, Gao, Wang, Zou & Liu 2026]]. Led writing of the original draft, methodology, investigation, and resources. The paper introduces [[concepts/lights4|lightS4]] (a diagonal-constrained S4 variant), the [[concepts/auditory-inspired-spectral-compressor|Auditory-Inspired Spectral Compressor (AISC)]], and a [[concepts/classifier-loss|Classifier Loss]] for human-voice interference suppression, achieving PESQ 3.32 / 0.50 G MACs on VoiceBank+DEMAND.
