# Mattes Ohlenbusch

**Affiliation**: University of Oldenburg (Germany); work on PAS-SE done during an internship at Bose Corporation

**Role**: Researcher

**Research Focus**: Own-voice speech enhancement for hearables with in-ear microphones; auxiliary-sensor speech enhancement; personalization.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "PAS-SE: personalized auxiliary-sensor speech enhancement for voice pickup in hearables" (ICASSP 2026) — [[sources/ohlenbusch-2026-pas-se|Ohlenbusch et al. 2026]]
- Co-author of "Modeling of speech-dependent own voice transfer characteristics for hearables with an in-ear microphone" (Acta Acustica 2024)
- Co-author of "Multi-Microphone Noise Data Augmentation for DNN-based Own Voice Reconstruction for Hearables in Noisy Environments" (ICASSP 2024) — source of the AS-SE training configuration B
- Co-author of "Low-complexity own voice reconstruction for hearables with an in-ear microphone" (ICASSP 2025)
