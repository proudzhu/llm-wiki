# Jason Wung

**Affiliation**: Center for Signal and Image Processing, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Acoustic echo cancellation, residual echo suppression, and psychoacoustic postfiltering for hands-free teleconferencing.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "A system approach to residual echo suppression in robust hands-free teleconferencing" (Proc. IEEE ICASSP 2011) — proposed a system-level residual echo estimate exploiting both the LSA nonlinear echo estimate and the linear AEC echo estimate — [[sources/wung-2011-residual-echo-suppression-system|Wung et al. 2011]]
