# Brian McFee

**Affiliation**: New York University (Music and Audio Research Lab)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Music information retrieval, audio analysis, digital signal processing, open-source audio software (librosa).

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Per-Channel Energy Normalization: Why and How" (IEEE SPL 2019) — [[sources/lostanlen-2019-pcen-why-and-how|Lostanlen et al. 2019]]; released the open-source PCEN implementation in librosa v0.6.1
- Lead maintainer of librosa, the standard Python library for audio and music analysis
