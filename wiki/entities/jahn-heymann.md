# Jahn Heymann

**Affiliation**: University of Paderborn, Department of Communications Engineering, Paderborn, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Neural-network-based speech enhancement and beamforming front-ends for robust ASR, distant-microphone speech recognition

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Neural Network Based Spectral Mask Estimation for Acoustic Beamforming" (ICASSP 2016) — [[sources/heymann-2016-neural-spectral-mask-beamforming|Heymann, Drude & Haeb-Umbach 2016]]; the neural mask + GEV/MVDR beamforming front-end that originated the CHiME-challenge-winning systems, with joint-frequency mask estimation, clean-speech-only training, and the MVDR-vs-GEV condition-number analysis.
- First author of the LSTM-supported GEV beamformer front-end for the 3rd CHiME challenge (ASRU 2015), where neural-network mask estimation was first used in this beamforming pipeline.
