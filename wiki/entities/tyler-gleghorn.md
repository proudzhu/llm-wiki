# Tyler Gleghorn

**Affiliation**: Microsoft Corporation, One Microsoft Way, Redmond, WA 98052, USA
**Role**: Researcher / Hardware Engineer
**Research Focus**: Acoustic hardware design and measurement for microphone-array prototypes in mobile devices; anechoic-chamber directivity characterization.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Sound Capture System and Spatial Filter for Small Devices" (2008) — built the cell-phone mock-up with two unidirectional microphones placed back-to-back at 9.6 mm spacing; led the anechoic-chamber directivity measurements (36 rotations of $10^\circ$ using chirp signals from a reference loudspeaker) used to derive the beamformer weights — [[sources/tashev-2008-sound-capture-spatial-filter|Tashev et al. 2008]]
- Co-authored "Enhanced Sound Capture System for Small Devices" (ICEST 2008, Niš, Serbia) — companion paper cited as ref. [5] in the 2008 paper
