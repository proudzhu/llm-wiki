# Yihao Li

**Affiliation**: Lab of Intelligent Information Processing, Army Engineering University, Nanjing, China
**Role**: Researcher (PhD-level author at time of publication)
**Research Focus**: Monaural speech enhancement, complex-valued neural networks, training-loss design for scale preservation.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Scale-aware dual-branch complex convolutional recurrent network for monaural speech enhancement" (Computer Speech & Language 2024) — [[sources/li-2024-dbccrn|Li et al. 2024]]: proposed [[concepts/dbccrn|DBCCRN]], the [[concepts/scale-aware-snr|SA-SNR]] loss, and the [[concepts/scale-error|ScaleError]] metric; led methodology, software, and experiments (CRediT). Maintains the [demo page](https://liyihao17.github.io/DualBranchComplexCRN/).
