# Matthias Blau

**Affiliation**: Institut für Hörtechnik und Audiologie, Jade-Hochschule, Oldenburg, Germany; Cluster of Excellence "Hearing4all.connects"
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Audiology, hearing technology, individual ear-canal acoustics, transfer-function characterisation of hearing devices, and active noise control in hearables.

## Key Contributions

- Research on individualised ear-canal modelling and transfer-function databases for hearables (Hearpiece database, Denk & Kollmeier 2021 collaboration line)
- Co-author on the robust soft-constrained spatially selective ANC framework with focus on real-world acoustic and individual variability
