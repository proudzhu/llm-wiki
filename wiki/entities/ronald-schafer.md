# Ronald W. Schafer

**Affiliation**: Hewlett-Packard Laboratories, Palo Alto, CA, USA (formerly Georgia Institute of Technology)
**Role**: Senior Researcher
**Research Focus**: Digital signal processing theory and applications; co-author of the classic Oppenheim & Schafer DSP textbook. Applied DSP to acoustic echo cancellation and speech enhancement.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "A system approach to residual echo suppression in robust hands-free teleconferencing" (Proc. IEEE ICASSP 2011) — [[sources/wung-2011-residual-echo-suppression-system|Wung et al. 2011]]
