# Sumit Bam Shrestha

**Affiliation**: Neuromorphic Computing Lab, Intel Labs
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Spiking neural network training algorithms, surrogate gradient methods (SLAYER), neuromorphic audio processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "The Intel Neuromorphic DNS Challenge" (arXiv 2023) — [[sources/timcheck-2023-intel-neuromorphic-dns-challenge|Timcheck et al. 2023]]. Co-developed the SDNN baseline solution trained with Lava-dl (an extended SLAYER backpropagation tool with surrogate gradients).
- Originator of SLAYER (Shrestha & Orchard, 2018), a widely-adopted surrogate-gradient backpropagation algorithm for spiking neural networks, used in the Lava-dl training framework that underpins the N-DNS Challenge baseline.
