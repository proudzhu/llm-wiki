# Filipe C. C. B. Diniz

**Affiliation**: LPS-PEE/COPPE, Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), Brazil (as of 2006); also affiliated with Petrobras (Brazilian oil company)
**Role**: Researcher; D.S. student at PEE/COPPE/UFRJ (as of 2006)
**Research Focus**: Digital signal processing, particularly audio and speech processing and denoising techniques.

## Biography

Born in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, in 1979. Received the Electrical Engineering degree from EE (now Poli) at UFRJ in 2003 and the M.S. degree in electrical engineering from COPPE/UFRJ in 2005. IEEE member.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "High-Selectivity Filter Banks for Spectral Analysis of Music Signals" (EURASIP J. Advances in Signal Processing 2007) — [[sources/diniz-2006-high-selectivity-filter-banks-spectral-analysis-music|Diniz et al. 2006]]
