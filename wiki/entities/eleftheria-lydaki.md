# Eleftheria Lydaki

**Affiliation**: Eriksholm Research Centre, Snekkersten, Denmark; Department of Electronic Systems, Aalborg University, Denmark
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning for hearing aid feedback cancellation, acoustic feedback control, speech and music signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed Deep Feedback Cancellation (DFC), a compact DNN for direct feedback-path IR estimation in hearing aids (IEEE TASLP 2026)
- Demonstrated that predicting the IR rather than the clean output enables 10x smaller models with superior performance
- Developed NESD loss with temporal smoothing for resolving convergence/steady-state trade-off
