# Eric Grinstein

**Affiliation**: Imperial College London, U.K. (work done during research internship at Meta)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multi-channel speech enhancement, sound source localization, hybrid DSP/DNN systems, beamforming.

## Key Contributions

- First author of NeuralPMWF — a tiny neural network (164.9k params) fully controlling the parametric multi-channel Wiener filter, with SPP-driven dynamic distortion control ([[sources/grinstein-2025-tiny-param-mwf|Grinstein et al. 2025]])
- First author of "Steered Response Power for Sound Source Localization — a Tutorial Review" (EURASIP Journal on Audio, Speech, and Music Processing 2024(1):59, 2024) — the tutorial review of 200+ SRP papers and variants, introducing the X-SRP modular framework and Python library — [[sources/grinstein-2024-srp-tutorial-review|Grinstein, Tengan, Çakmak et al. 2024]]
