# Hakan Erdogan

**Affiliation**: Microsoft AI and Research, Redmond, WA, USA (at the time of this 2016 paper: Sabanci University, Istanbul, Turkey)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement and robust ASR, neural-network-based mask estimation, mask-based beamforming

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Exploring Practical Aspects of Neural Mask-Based Beamforming for Far-Field Speech Recognition" (ICASSP 2018) — practical evaluation of neural mask-based beamforming for far-field ASR (two arrays, two acoustic models, feature-level LogMel MSE loss, online variants) — [[sources/boeddeker-2018-neural-mask-beamforming|Boeddeker, Erdogan, Yoshioka & Haeb-Umbach 2018]]
- Known for the phase-sensitive mask for speech enhancement and robust ASR (Erdogan et al., ICASSP 2015) — an improvement over ideal ratio masks that accounts for phase errors between noisy and clean spectrograms.
- First author of "Improved MVDR Beamforming Using Single-Channel Mask Prediction Networks" (Interspeech 2016) — founding mask-based beamforming paper: single-channel LSTM masks drive the noise SCM of a statistics-only MVDR; single (max-combined) mask plus minimum-floor post-mask is the best configuration — [[sources/erdogan-2016-improved-mvdr-mask-prediction|Erdogan, Hershey, Watanabe, Mandel & Le Roux 2016]]
