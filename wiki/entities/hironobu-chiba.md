# Hironobu Chiba

**Affiliation**: NTT Inc., Tokyo, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Post-filtering for speech coding and playback intelligibility; low-complexity speech enhancement; acoustic design and active noise control for open-ear headphones.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Wideband active noise control for open-ear headphones via low-delay acoustic design with a coaxial loudspeaker" (Applied Acoustics 2027) — [[sources/chiba-2027-wideband-anc-coaxial-loudspeaker|Chiba et al. 2027]]: a design-oriented secondary-path delay budget (electronic + acoustic + mechanical terms) that allocates control bands between a coaxial woofer/tweeter pair for wideband open-ear feedforward ANC (8.01 dB over 100–2000 Hz vs 5.94 dB single-driver).
- Co-author of "Low-frequency harmonic control for speech intelligibility in open-ear headphones" (ICASSP 2026) — [[sources/watanabe-2026-low-frequency-harmonic-control|Watanabe et al. 2026]].
- Developer of adaptive post-filtering controlled by pitch frequency for CELP-based speech coders (Asilomar 2014, with Kamamoto, Moriya, Harada, Miyabe, Yamada & Makino) — a post-filter whose pitch enhancement band and gain vary per frame with the pitch period, improving perceptual sound quality; the foundation on which LFHC builds.
