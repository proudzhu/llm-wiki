# Hironobu Chiba

**Affiliation**: NTT Inc., Tokyo, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Post-filtering for speech coding and playback intelligibility; low-complexity speech enhancement.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Low-frequency harmonic control for speech intelligibility in open-ear headphones" (ICASSP 2026) — [[sources/watanabe-2026-low-frequency-harmonic-control|Watanabe et al. 2026]].
- Developer of adaptive post-filtering controlled by pitch frequency for CELP-based speech coders (Asilomar 2014, with Kamamoto, Moriya, Harada, Miyabe, Yamada & Makino) — a post-filter whose pitch enhancement band and gain vary per frame with the pitch period, improving perceptual sound quality; the foundation on which LFHC builds.
