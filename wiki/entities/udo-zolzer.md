# Udo Zölzer

**Udo Zölzer** is a Professor at the Helmut Schmidt University in Hamburg, Germany. He is a prominent figure in the field of **Digital Audio Effects** and audio signal processing.

## Research Contributions

Professor Zölzer is well-known for his textbook *"Digital Audio Effects" (DAFX)*, which is a standard reference in the industry. His research covers a wide range of topics, including:
- Audio effects algorithms (filtering, reverb, dynamics).
- Adaptive signal processing.
- **Active Noise Control**: He was the PhD supervisor for **[[piero-iared-rivera-benois|Piero Iared Rivera Benois]]**, whose research focused on hybrid ANC for headphones.
