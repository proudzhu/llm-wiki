# Yi Luo

**Affiliation**: Tencent AI Lab, Audio and Speech Signal Processing Oteam
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Time-frequency modeling for speech enhancement, dual-path transformer architectures, joint AEC and noise suppression.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored [Ultra Dual-Path Compression for Joint Echo Cancellation and Noise Suppression](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2023-2302) (Interspeech 2023) — [[sources/chen-2023-ultra-dual-path-compression\|Chen et al. 2023]]
- First author of "Music Source Separation with Band-Split RNN" (arXiv 2209.15174, 2022; IEEE/ACM TASLP 2023) — proposed [[concepts/band-split-rnn|BSRNN]], a frequency-domain MSS model with instrument-specific band splitting and interleaved band/sequence BLSTM modeling that outperformed all MDX Challenge 2021 top systems on vocals, drums, and other — [[sources/luo-2022-band-split-rnn|Luo & Yu 2022]]
