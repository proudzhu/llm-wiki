# Raymond de Callafon

**Affiliation**: Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, University of California, San Diego (UCSD), USA
**Role**: Full Professor; Director of the System Identification and Control Laboratory
**Research Focus**: Experiment-based approximation modeling, control-relevant system identification, recursive/adaptive control

## Biography

Received his M.Sc. (1992) and Ph.D. (1998) in Mechanical Engineering from Delft University of Technology, the Netherlands. Joined the Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Department at UCSD in 1997, where he is currently a Full Professor. Directs the System Identification and Control Laboratory and is an affiliated faculty member of the Center for Magnetic Recording Research. Recipient of the 2010 INSIC Technical Achievement award for the design and application of adaptive servo technology.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Detection of secondary-path irregularities in active noise control headphones" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2014) — [[sources/guldenschuh-2014-secondary-path-irregularities|Guldenschuh & de Callafon 2014]]
- Earlier work on active noise control of a cooling fan in a short duct with robust constraint checking before filter updates (NoiseCon/NCAD 2008, with Kinney & Villalta) — a direct antecedent of the 2014 paper's constraint-checking approach
- Research program on control-relevant system identification and adaptive control applied to servo systems and acoustics
