# Aurelio Uncini

**Affiliation**: Department of Information Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunications (DIET), Sapienza University of Rome, Italy
**Role**: Professor / Researcher
**Research Focus**: Adaptive signal processing, neural networks for audio processing, acoustic echo cancellation, intelligent audio systems.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored the Physics-Informed NLMS (PI-NLMS) algorithm for acoustic echo cancellation — [[sources/scarpiniti-2027-physics-informed-adaptive-filtering-aec|Scarpiniti, Comminiello & Uncini 2027]]
- Author of *Fundamentals of Adaptive Signal Processing* (Springer, 2015) — a standard reference in the field.
