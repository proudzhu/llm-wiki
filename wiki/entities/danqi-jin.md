# Danqi Jin

**Danqi Jin** (金丹琦) is a researcher working on microphone array signal processing and speech enhancement, collaborating with the [[entities/gongping-huang|Gongping Huang]] group.

**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Microphone array beamforming, speech enhancement.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Robust Fusion of Differential Beamformers" (IEEE Signal Processing Letters 2025) — statistics-free online fusion of a null-constrained DMA bank via instantaneous minimum-variance selection — [[sources/zhao-2025-robust-fusion-differential-beamformers|Zhao, Luo, Jin, Jin & Huang 2025]]
