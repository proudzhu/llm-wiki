# Yegui Xiao

**Affiliation**: Dept. of Information Science, Prefectural University of Hiroshima, Hiroshima, 734-8558, Japan
**Role**: Professor / Senior Researcher (corresponding author)
**Research Focus**: Adaptive filtering for active noise control; cascade adaptive notch filter banks (CANFB); online secondary- and feedback-path modeling; narrowband ANC; statistical analysis of adaptive systems.

## Key Contributions

- Corresponding author of the robust FFANC with simultaneous OSPM and OFBPM (*Signal Processing*, 2027) — originated the second supporting filter concept and supervised the steady-state analysis.
- Co-author, "Properties of FXLMS-based narrowband active noise control with online secondary-path modeling" (*IEEE Trans. Signal Processing*, 2009) — fundamental FXLMS-OSPM interaction analysis.
- Co-author, "An efficient filter bank structure for adaptive notch filtering and applications" (*IEEE/ACM TASLP*, 2021) — CANFB architecture used in narrowband ANC systems.
- Co-author on multiple earlier OSPM/OFBPM narrowband ANC systems (ICSV 2019; APCCAS 2019) that the 2027 paper extends.
