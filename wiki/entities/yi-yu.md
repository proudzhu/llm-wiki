# Yi Yu

## Overview

Researcher at the **School of Information Engineering, Southwest University of Science and Technology (SWUST)**, Mianyang, China. Affiliated with the Robot Technology Used for Special Environment Key Laboratory of Sichuan Province. Works on adaptive filtering, distributed signal processing, and active noise control (linear and nonlinear).

## Research Areas

- Adaptive filtering algorithms
- Distributed and diffusion adaptive filtering
