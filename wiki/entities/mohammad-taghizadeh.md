# Mohammad J. Taghizadeh

**Affiliation**: Huawei European Research Center, Munich, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Microphone array processing, ad hoc array calibration, diffuse field modeling, and multi-channel speech enhancement for mobile devices.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Multi-channel noise reduction for hands-free voice communication on mobile phones" (ICASSP 2017) — [[sources/jin-2017-multichannel-noise-reduction-mobile|Jin et al. 2017]] — proposed an adaptive-coherence noise estimation method with adaptive split-frequency selection and globally MMSE-optimal multi-channel variance estimation, validated on a 3-microphone Huawei Mate 8.
- Prior work on enhanced diffuse field models for ad hoc microphone array calibration (Taghizadeh, Garner & Bourlard, Signal Processing 2014, ref [19] in the Jin et al. 2017 paper) — used to ensure diffuseness of the replayed noise field in the experimental evaluation.
