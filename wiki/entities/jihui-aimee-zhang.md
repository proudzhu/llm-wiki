# Jihui Aimee Zhang

**Affiliation**: Audio and Acoustic Signal Processing Group, The Australian National University, Canberra, Australia (at the time of the 2020 JASA paper)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, multichannel ANC performance analysis, coherence-based evaluation, spatial noise field control

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Coherence-Based Performance Analysis on Noise Reduction in Multichannel Active Noise Control Systems" (JASA 2020) — [[sources/zhang-2020-coherence-mcanc-noise-reduction|Zhang 2020]]: algorithm-independent three-term decomposition of the multichannel ANC residual energy (signal coherence / filter / system null space), with a closed-form optimal filter that generalizes the single-channel coherence limit of Kuo & Morgan to arbitrary MIMO systems
- Co-author of "Multichannel Active Noise Control for Spatially Sparse Noise Fields" (JASA-EL 2016)
- Co-author of "Spatial Noise Cancellation Inside Cars: Performance Analysis and Experimental Results" (IEEE WASPAA 2015)
- Co-author of "Evaluation of Spatial Active Noise Cancellation Performance Using Spherical Harmonic Analysis" (IWAENC 2016)
