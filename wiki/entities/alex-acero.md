# Alex Acero

**Affiliation**: Microsoft (Redmond, WA, USA)
**Role**: Researcher / Research Manager
**Research Focus**: Speech recognition, speech enhancement, and microphone-array processing; long-running contributions to hands-free speech-capture systems for mobile and consumer devices.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Sound Capture System and Spatial Filter for Small Devices" (2008) — the back-to-back unidirectional microphone-array geometry with a probability-based non-linear spatial filter — [[sources/tashev-2008-sound-capture-spatial-filter|Tashev et al. 2008]]
- Co-authored "Microphone Array for Headset with Spatial Noise Suppressor" (IWAENC 2005) — earlier spatial-noise-suppressor work, cited as ref. [3] in the 2008 paper
- Co-authored "Microphone Array Post-Processor Using Instantaneous Direction of Arrival" (IWAENC 2006) — direct probability-estimation approach to spatial post-filtering, cited as ref. [4] in the 2008 paper
