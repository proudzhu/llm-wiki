# Mehrez Souden

**Mehrez Souden** is a researcher in microphone array signal processing. He received the Diplôme d'Ingénieur in signals and systems from the École Polytechnique de Tunisie (2004) and the M.Sc. in telecommunications from INRS-EMT, University of Quebec (2006), and pursued his Ph.D. in telecommunications engineering at INRS-EMT.

**Affiliation**: INRS-EMT, University of Quebec, Montreal, QC, Canada
**Role**: Researcher (Ph.D. candidate at time of the PMWF paper)
**Research Focus**: Microphone array processing — noise reduction and source localization.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "On Optimal Frequency-Domain Multichannel Linear Filtering for Noise Reduction" (IEEE TASLP 2010) — the foundational [[concepts/parametric-multi-channel-wiener-filter|PMWF]] paper, which unified the [[concepts/mvdr-beamformer|MVDR]], GSC, and multi-channel Wiener filters in one statistics-only frequency-domain framework and derived closed-form distortion/noise-reduction measures — [[sources/souden-2010-pmwf|Souden, Benesty & Affes 2010]]
- First author of "An Integrated Solution for Online Multichannel Noise Tracking and Reduction" (IEEE TASLP 2011) — the practical implementation of the [[concepts/multi-channel-speech-presence-probability|MC-SPP]] with a multivariate a priori SAP estimator, [[concepts/multichannel-mcra|multichannel MCRA]] noise PSD matrix tracking, and an SPP-driven modified multichannel Wiener filter — [[sources/souden-2011-online-noise-tracking|Souden, Chen, Benesty & Affes 2011]]
