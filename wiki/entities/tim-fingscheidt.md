# Tim Fingscheidt

**Affiliation**: Institute for Communications Technology, Technische Universität Braunschweig, Germany (Professor)
**Role**: Professor / Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech and audio signal processing, speech enhancement, acoustic echo control, deep learning for speech communications, hands-free telecommunication.

## Key Contributions

- Efficient high-performance Bark-scale neural network for residual echo and noise suppression (ICASSP 2024) — [[sources/seidel-2024-bark-scale-nn-residual-suppression\|Seidel, Mowlaee & Fingscheidt 2024]]
- Y²-Net FCRN for acoustic echo and noise suppression (Interspeech 2021)
- AEC in A Netshell: On target and topology choices for FCRN AEC (ICASSP 2021)
- Bandwidth-scalable fully mask-based deep FCRN acoustic echo cancellation and postfiltering (IWAENC 2022)
- Efficient deep acoustic echo suppression with condition-aware training (WASPAA 2023)
- Co-authored hands-free system with low-delay subband acoustic echo control and noise reduction (ICASSP 2008)
