# Shyamnath Gollakota

**Affiliation**: Paul G. Allen School, University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Wireless systems, mobile sensing, and machine learning for hearables and health applications.

## Key Contributions

- Senior author of "Semantic Hearing: Programming Acoustic Scenes with Binaural Hearables" (UIST 2023) — [[sources/veluri-2023-semantic-hearing|Veluri et al. 2023]]; leads the UW group behind semantic hearing and Waveformer.
- Co-author of Waveformer, "Real-Time Target Sound Extraction" (IEEE ICASSP 2023).
- Co-author of ClearBuds: wireless binaural earbuds for learning-based speech enhancement (ACM MobiSys 2022) and Hybrid Neural Networks for On-Device Directional Hearing (AAAI 2022).
- Smartphone-based health sensing, including middle-ear fluid detection (Science Translational Medicine 2019) and smartphone otoacoustic-emission hearing screening (Nature Biomedical Engineering 2022).
