# Bandhav Veluri

**Affiliation**: Paul G. Allen School, University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Real-time target sound extraction, binaural hearing, on-device neural audio for hearables.

## Key Contributions

- Co-first author of "Semantic Hearing: Programming Acoustic Scenes with Binaural Hearables" (UIST 2023) — [[sources/veluri-2023-semantic-hearing|Veluri et al. 2023]]; designed the first binaural target sound extraction neural network and its real-time smartphone implementation.
- First author of Waveformer, "Real-Time Target Sound Extraction" (IEEE ICASSP 2023) — the single-channel streaming transformer architecture that the semantic-hearing network extends to binaural input.
