# Gal Itzhak

**Affiliation**: Technion — Israel Institute of Technology, Haifa, Israel (Ph.D. 2021, Visiting Lecturer since 2023); Carl von Ossietzky University of Oldenburg, Germany (Research Fellow since 2023); Palo Alto Networks (Principal Data Scientist, then Data Science Research Manager, since 2021)
**Role**: Researcher, Data Science Manager, Visiting Lecturer
**Research Focus**: Array signal processing, speech enhancement, microphone arrays, machine learning, cyber security.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "STFT-Domain Least-Distortion Region-of-Interest Beamforming" (IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing, 2025) — introduced the LD-MWNG and LD-MDF ROI beamformers with the eigenvector-truncation design parameter $K$ — [[sources/itzhak-2025-stft-roi-beamforming|Itzhak & Cohen 2025]]
- Sustained research line on region-of-interest and robust array processing: rectangular-array differential/constant-beamwidth beamforming (EUSIPCO 2021, Speech Communication 2022, IWAENC 2022, WASPAA 2023), Kronecker-product beamforming with sparse concentric circular arrays (IEEE OJSP 2024), joint array-geometry-and-beamformer optimization with sparse circular sector arrays (IWAENC 2024, with Simon Doclo), and robust beamforming for multispeaker audio conferencing under DOA uncertainty (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2025).
- Earlier work on quadratic approaches for single-channel noise reduction (EURASIP JASMP 2020, with Benesty and Cohen).

## Background

Received the B.Sc. degree in electrical engineering and physics (2012) and the Ph.D. degree (direct track) in electrical engineering (2021) from the Technion. Algorithm Engineer, Researcher, and Consultant with the Ministry of Defense (2012–2018, digital communication and high-speed logic design); Senior Researcher and Advisor in the private sector (2018–2021, statistical and ML models for wireless-device fingerprinting).
