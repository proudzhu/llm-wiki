# Jonathan Timcheck

**Affiliation**: Neuromorphic Computing Lab, Intel Labs
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Neuromorphic computing, neuromorphic audio processing, challenge problem design.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "The Intel Neuromorphic DNS Challenge" (arXiv 2023) — [[sources/timcheck-2023-intel-neuromorphic-dns-challenge|Timcheck et al. 2023]], defining the Intel N-DNS Challenge benchmark for real-time SNN-based audio denoising on Loihi 2, including the SDNN baseline solution and the power/latency/resource evaluation methodology.
