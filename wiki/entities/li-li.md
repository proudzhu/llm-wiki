# Li Li

**Affiliation**: NTT Communication Science Laboratories, Nippon Telegraph and Telephone Corporation, Japan (as of 2022, per Goto et al. 2022); previously University of Tsukuba, Japan and Microsoft Corporation, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multichannel speech enhancement, blind source separation, independent vector analysis.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Geometrically Constrained Independent Vector Analysis for Directional Speech Enhancement" (ICASSP 2020) — [[sources/li-2020-geometrically-constrained-iva|Li & Koishida 2020]]
- First author of "Online Directional Speech Enhancement Using Geometrically Constrained IVA" (Interspeech 2020) — real-time online extension of GCAV-IVA via autoregressive auxiliary variables; real-time dual-mic directional speech enhancement — [[sources/li-2020-online-gciva|Li, Koishida & Makino 2020]]
- Co-author of "Accelerating Online GC-IVA with Iterative Source Steering" (APSIPA ASC 2022) — online GC-AuxIVA-ISS, an inverse-free online geometrically constrained IVA with MUSIC-based interference DOA estimation — [[sources/goto-2022-iss-gciva|Goto, Ueda, Li, Yamada & Makino 2022]]
