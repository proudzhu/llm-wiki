# Ariel Frank

**Affiliation**: Andrew and Erna Viterbi Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Technion — Israel Institute of Technology, Haifa, Israel

**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, beamforming, wearable audio systems, low-latency spatial filtering for smart glasses.

**Key Contribution**: Developed unified formulation for time-domain and STFT-domain region-of-interest (LDMG) beamformers, demonstrating time-domain superiority for smart-glasses audio front-ends in terms of latency and performance.
