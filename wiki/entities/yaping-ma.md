# Yaping Ma

**Affiliation**: Institute of Automation, Jiangnan University, Wuxi, 214122, China
**Role**: Researcher (Ph.D. candidate / junior researcher as of 2027)
**Research Focus**: Active noise control (ANC) with online secondary- and feedback-path modeling; supporting-filter architectures; auxiliary-noise scaling strategies; robustness to time-varying paths.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed a robust FFANC system with simultaneous OSPM and OFBPM, introducing a second supporting filter $H_2(z)$ and a global AWGN scaling scheme driven by the SF output — achieves near-ideal-benchmark NRP with no divisions/square roots (*Signal Processing*, 2027).
- Co-author, "A robust feedback active noise control system with online secondary path modeling" (*IEEE Signal Processing Letters*, 2022) — refreshment scheme for abrupt IIR path changes.
- Co-author, "A new strategy for online secondary-path modeling of narrowband active noise control" (*IEEE/ACM TASLP*, 2017) — auxiliary-noise scaling for narrowband ANC.
