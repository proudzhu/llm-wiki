# Yu Tsao

**Affiliation**: Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan
**Role**: Researcher / Research Fellow
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, speech recognition, speaker recognition, deep learning for speech and audio processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "An investigation of incorporating mamba for speech enhancement" (IEEE SLT 2024) — [[concepts/semamba|SEMamba]], the first Mamba-based SE system — [[sources/chao-2024-mamba-speech-enhancement|Chao et al. 2024]]
- Co-author of "One Model, Many Latencies: Universal Speech Enhancement for Diverse Real-Time Applications" (arXiv preprint 2606.25621 (v3), 2026) — one-for-all real-time universal speech enhancement with selectable algorithmic and computational latency — [[sources/fu-2026-one-model-many-latencies|Fu et al. 2026]]
- Co-author of "MetricGAN: Generative Adversarial Networks based Black-box Metric Scores Optimization for Speech Enhancement" (ICML 2019) — the discriminator-as-metric-surrogate formulation that trains a generator directly against PESQ/STOI without any knowledge of the metrics' internals — [[sources/fu-2019-metricgan|Fu, Liao, Tsao & Lin 2019]]
- Co-author of "Boosting Objective Scores of a Speech Enhancement Model by MetricGAN Post-processing" (APSIPA ASC 2020) — senior author of the DNS 2020 system applying MetricGAN as a post-hoc fine-tuning stage for a causal Transformer enhancer — [[sources/fu-2020-metricgan-post-processing|Fu et al. 2020]]
