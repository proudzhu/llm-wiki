# Juan Pablo Bello

**Affiliation**: New York University (Music and Audio Research Lab; SONYC project lead)
**Role**: Professor of Music Technology and Computer Science
**Research Focus**: Urban noise monitoring (SONYC), sound event detection, music information retrieval, acoustic sensing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Per-Channel Energy Normalization: Why and How" (IEEE SPL 2019) — [[sources/lostanlen-2019-pcen-why-and-how|Lostanlen et al. 2019]]
- Principal investigator of SONYC (Sounds of New York City) — a system for monitoring, analysis, and mitigation of urban noise pollution
- Deep convolutional neural networks and data augmentation for environmental sound classification (IEEE SPL 2017)
