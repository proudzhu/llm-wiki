# Michael Klingbeil

**Affiliation**: Amazon Web Services, Palo Alto, CA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech coding, audio signal processing, neural audio redundancy coding.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Real-Time Packet Loss Concealment With Mixed Generative and Predictive Model" (INTERSPEECH 2022, pp. 570–574) — 2nd place, Interspeech 2022 Audio Deep PLC Challenge — [[sources/valin-2022-real-time-plc|Valin et al. 2022]]
- Co-authored "DRED: Deep REDundancy Coding of Speech Using a Rate-Distortion-Optimized Variational Autoencoder" (IEEE J. Sel. Top. Signal Process. 2024) — with Valin, Büthe, Mustafa — a neural redundancy coding extension of the PLC work
- Earlier work on "Software for Spectral Analysis, Editing, and Synthesis" (ICMC 2005)
