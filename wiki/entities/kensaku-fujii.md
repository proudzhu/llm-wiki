# Kensaku Fujii

**Affiliation**: Department of Computer Engineering, University of Hyogo, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive signal processing, simultaneous equations method.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed the simultaneous equations method for active noise control systems (1999), eliminating the need for explicit secondary path modeling
- Experimental verification of the simultaneous equations method in practical ANC systems (2006)
- Frequency-domain processing techniques for ANC (2003)
- Methods for updating secondary path and feedback control filter coefficients (2001, 2005)
- Hybrid ANC systems based on the simultaneous equations method
