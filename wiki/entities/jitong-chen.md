# Jitong Chen

**Affiliation**: Ohio State University, Department of Computer Science and Engineering
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Supervised speech separation, acoustic features for T-F masking, large-scale training for noise generalization, speaker generalization with LSTM.

## Key Contributions

- Supervised speech separation survey with DeLiang Wang (IEEE TASLP 2018)
- Multi-Resolution Cochleagram (MRCG) feature for classification-based speech separation at low SNRs
- Large-scale training study demonstrating noise-independent speech enhancement with 10,000 noises
- LSTM for speaker generalization in supervised speech separation
