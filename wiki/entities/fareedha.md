# Fareedha

**Affiliation**: National Institute of Technology, Warangal, Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning-based active noise cancellation, integrating neural secondary path estimation with adaptive control. Her work focuses on end-to-end frameworks that replace iterative adaptation with data-driven real-time prediction.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed DeepSPE + ANC-Net end-to-end framework for joint secondary path estimation and adaptive ANC control (ICASSP 2026)
- Previously developed DFANC-EKF combining Extended Kalman Filtering with CNN-based feature extraction for dynamic fixed-filter ANC (ICASSP 2025)
