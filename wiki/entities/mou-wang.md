# Mou Wang

**Affiliation**: Laboratory of Noise and Audio Research, Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing; OPPO, Beijing (ICSPCC 2025 work)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, bone-conducted speech processing, time-domain multi-modal fusion, deep learning for acoustics

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Multi-modal speech enhancement with bone-conducted speech in time domain" (Applied Acoustics 2022) — an end-to-end multi-modal involution neural network estimating clean AC speech from recorded BC and AC speech
- Co-authored "Low-complexity bone conduction-aided speech enhancement leveraging sub-band and full-band architecture" (Applied Acoustics 2025) — [[sources/kuang-2025-low-complexity-bc-speech-enhancement|Kuang, Yang, Wang & Yang 2025]]
- Co-author of "DBMIF — A Deep Balanced Multimodal Iterative Fusion Framework for AC/BC Speech Enhancement" (Applied Intelligence 2026) — three-branch iterative AC/BC fusion with DIAF, CBGI and a parameter-shared DBI equilibrium bottleneck — [[sources/wu-2026-dbmif-multimodal-fusion|Wu, Zheng, Zhang, Zhang, Zheng, Yang, Yan & Yin 2026]]
- Co-author of "Multi-Modal Deep-Filtering-Based Speech Enhancement via Bone-Conducted Speech" (ICSPCC 2025) — Multi-modal deep-filtering network (MDFNet) fusing BC and AC speech inside the deep-filtering coefficients, with ERB-gain high-frequency branch and ASR-best CER at low SNR — [[sources/wang-2025-multi-modal-deep-filtering-bc|Wang, Kuang, Li, Yang, Bai & Liu 2025]]
