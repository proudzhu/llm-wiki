# Dahan Wang

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Modern Acoustics, Nanjing University, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, deep learning for audio processing.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Adaptive Convolution for CNN-based Speech Enhancement Models" (IEEE TASLPRO 2025) — introduced [[concepts/adaptive-convolution|adaptive convolution]] (frame-wise causal dynamic convolution) and the ultra-lightweight [[concepts/adaptcrn|AdaptCRN]] (135K params, 41 MMACs/s, PESQ 2.98 on VCTK-DEMAND) — [[sources/wang-2025-adaptive-convolution-cnn-speech-enhancement|Wang et al. 2025]]
- Co-author of CoFi-Lite (IEEE SPL 2026) — ultra-lightweight speech enhancement with decoupled coarse/fine paths, 12.87M MACs/s — [[sources/yang-2026-cofi-lite-ultra-lightweight-speech-enhancement|Yang et al. 2026]]
- Co-author of HALO (arXiv 2026) — half-frame-rate adaptive learnable operator for lightweight STFT-based speech enhancement
