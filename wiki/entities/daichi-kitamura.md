# Daichi Kitamura

**Affiliation**: National Institute of Technology, Kagawa College, Takamatsu, Japan (formerly SOKENDAI / University of Tokyo)
**Role**: Associate Professor, Researcher
**Research Focus**: Independent low-rank matrix analysis, nonnegative matrix factorization, blind source separation, music source separation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "A review of blind source separation methods: two converging routes to ILRMA originating from ICA and NMF" (APSIPA Trans. Signal Inf. Process. 2019) — [[sources/sawada-2019-bss-ilrma-review|Sawada et al. 2019: BSS/ILRMA Review]]. Provides the canonical dual derivation of ILRMA from the IVA and MNMF sides.
- Principal author of the original ILRMA formulation (Kitamura et al., WASPAA 2015; IEEE TASLP 2018) — the method unifying IVA's spatial model with NMF's spectrogram model that is the convergence point of the ICA and NMF routes.
