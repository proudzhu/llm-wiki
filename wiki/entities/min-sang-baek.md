# Min-Sang Baek

**Affiliation**: Hanyang University, Seoul, Korea (School of Electronic Engineering)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning for sound source localization and direction-of-arrival estimation, geometry-invariant neural networks, curriculum learning for spatial audio.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "DNN-based Geometry-Invariant DOA Estimation With Microphone Positional Encoding and Complexity Gradual Training" (IEEE TASLPRO 2025) — introduced **Microphone Positional Encoding (MPE)**, the **GI-DOAEnet** architecture, and **Complexity Gradual Training (CGT)** — [[sources/baek-2025-geometry-invariant-doa-estimation|Baek, Chang & Cohen 2025]]
- First author of "Deeply Supervised Curriculum Learning for Deep Neural Network-Based Sound Source Localization" (Interspeech 2023) — the DSCL training scheme on which CGT builds; also the origin of the CRNN single-array DOA baseline used in the 2025 comparison
