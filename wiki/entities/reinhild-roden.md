# Reinhild Roden

**Affiliation**: Institut für Hörtechnik und Audiologie, Jade-Hochschule, Oldenburg, Germany; Cluster of Excellence "Hearing4all.connects"
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Hearables, transfer-function characterisation of in-ear devices, and active noise control in closed-fitting earpieces.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author on the robust soft-constrained spatially selective ANC framework, contributing to secondary-path measurement and real-time platform integration
- Practical experience with KEMAR-based measurement of hearable secondary paths
