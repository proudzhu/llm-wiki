# Vincent Lostanlen

**Affiliation**: CNRS researcher, Nantes (previously New York University / Cornell Lab of Ornithology)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Time-frequency analysis, scattering transforms, audio frontends, bioacoustics, PCEN.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Per-Channel Energy Normalization: Why and How" (IEEE SPL 2019) — [[sources/lostanlen-2019-pcen-why-and-how|Lostanlen et al. 2019]]; asymptotic analysis of PCEN's temporal integration, AGC, and DRC components
- Pioneer of time-frequency scattering transforms (joint work with Joakim Andén and Stéphane Mallat)
- Developed BirdVox flight-call detection datasets and benchmarks for bioacoustic monitoring
