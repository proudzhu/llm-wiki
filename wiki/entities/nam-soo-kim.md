# Nam Soo Kim

**Affiliation**: Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and INMC, Seoul National University, Seoul, South Korea
**Role**: Professor (senior author)
**Research Focus**: Speech signal processing, neural speech enhancement, statistical speech and speaker modeling.

## Key Contributions

- Senior author of "FastEnhancer: Speed-Optimized Streaming Neural Speech Enhancement" (ICASSP 2026) — [[sources/ahn-2026-fastenhancer|Ahn, Han, Woo & Kim 2026]] — proposed the [[concepts/rnnformer|RNNFormer]] dual-path block (time GRU + frequency MHSA) and established that architectural simplicity, not params/MACs reduction, drives real-world inference speed.
