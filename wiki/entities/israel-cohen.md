# Israel Cohen

**Affiliation**: Andrew and Erna Viterbi Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Technion — Israel Institute of Technology, Haifa, Israel

**Research Focus**: Speech and audio signal processing, beamforming, speech enhancement, noise reduction, microphone array processing, wearable audio systems.

**Key Contribution**: Co-developed unified formulation for time-domain and STFT-domain region-of-interest (LDMG) beamformers for smart glasses, establishing time-domain superiority for low-latency wearable audio front-ends. Israel Cohen is a well-known researcher in the speech processing community with extensive contributions to speech enhancement and spatial filtering.
