**Affiliation**: Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, USA
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Wireless and mobile sensing systems, wearable computing, and audio applications — co-supervised the open-ear smart glasses ANC system (corresponding author).

## Key Contributions

- Co-supervised (with Justin Chan) "Active noise cancellation on open-ear smart glasses" (arXiv preprint 2026) — the first working ANC system on open-ear wearables without an in-ear error microphone — [[sources/yuan-2026-open-ear-anc-smart-glasses|Yuan, Liu & Xiao 2026]]
