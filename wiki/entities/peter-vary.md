# Peter Vary

**Affiliation**: Institute of Communication Systems, RWTH Aachen University (Professor and Head since 1988)
**Role**: Professor (Life Fellow, IEEE), formerly Philips Communication Industries (PKI), Nuremberg (1980–1988)
**Research Focus**: Wireless communications, speech coding, joint source-channel coding, error concealment, speech enhancement for mobile phones and digital hearing aids, active noise and occlusion cancellation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Occlusion Effect Cancellation in Headphones and Hearing Devices—The Sister of Active Noise Cancellation" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2022) — [[sources/liebich-2022-occlusion-effect-cancellation|Liebich & Vary 2022]]
- Co-authored "Active Cancellation of the Occlusion Effect in Hearing Aids by Time Invariant Robust Feedback" (ITG Speech Communication 2016)
- Fellow of the IEEE Signal Processing Society, the EURASIP Signal Processing Society, and the German ITG.
