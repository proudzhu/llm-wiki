# Walter Kellermann

**Affiliation**: Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), Chair of Multimedia Communications and Signal Processing
**Role**: Professor, Fellow of the IEEE
**Research Focus**: Acoustic signal processing, multichannel speech enhancement, dereverberation, spatial audio, beamforming.

## Key Contributions

- CDR-based dereverberation using spatial coherence between omnidirectional microphones (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2015) — proposed novel unbiased CDR estimators and DOA-independent dereverberation system
- Co-author of fundamental works in dereverberation, spatial coherence modeling, and robust acoustic signal processing
