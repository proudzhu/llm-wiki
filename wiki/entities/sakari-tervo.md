# Sakari Tervo

**Affiliation**: Helsinki University of Technology (TKK), Department of Media Technology, Finland (at the time of the 2009 EUSIPCO paper; TKK became part of Aalto University in 2010)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Sound source localization and direction estimation from [[concepts/sound-intensity-vector|sound intensity vectors]], room acoustics measurement and analysis, spatial audio.

## Key Contributions

- Authored "Direction Estimation Based on Sound Intensity Vectors" (EUSIPCO 2009) — [[sources/tervo-2009-sound-intensity-direction|Tervo 2009]] — an empirical comparison of five intensity-vector DOA estimators (CME, MCA, CMD, VMM, WGM) on real concert-hall data, finding mixture-model methods (von Mises in particular) more accurate and more noise-robust than simple circular averaging.
- Doctoral research supervised by Dr. Tapio Lokki, funded by the Academy of Finland (project 119092), the European Research Council (FP7 grant 203636), and the Helsinki Graduate School in Computer Science and Engineering.
