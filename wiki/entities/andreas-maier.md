# Andreas Maier

**Affiliation**: Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Pattern Recognition Lab, Erlangen, Germany
**Role**: Professor, Head of Pattern Recognition Lab
**Research Focus**: Pattern recognition, speech processing, medical image processing, deep learning.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of [[sources/schroter-2022-deepfilternet|DeepFilterNet]] — low-complexity speech enhancement framework (ICASSP 2022)
- Co-author of DeepFilterNet2 and CLCNet
- Supervisor of multiple works on deep learning for speech enhancement and hearing aid applications
