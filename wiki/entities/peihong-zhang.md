# Peihong Zhang

## Overview

Peihong Zhang is a researcher affiliated with the **School of Advanced Technology** at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) in Suzhou, China. Their research focuses on acoustic signal processing, deep learning applications for audio classification, and bio-acoustic analysis such as heart sound segmentation.

## Research Areas

- Acoustic Signal Processing
- Acoustic Scene Classification (ASC) & Curriculum Learning
- Heart Sound Segmentation & Cardiovascular Analysis
- Topological Signal Analysis

## Notable Work

- [[sources/cai-2024-tf-sepnet|Cai, Zhang & Li 2024: TF-SepNet]] — Co-author, proposing parallel 1D kernels to process time and frequency features separately.
