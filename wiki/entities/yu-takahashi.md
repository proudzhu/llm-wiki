# Yu Takahashi

**Affiliation**: Yamaha Corporation, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, microphone-array signal processing, blind source separation, acoustic signal processing for human–avatar/robot communication.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Real-Time Speech Extraction Based on Rank-Constrained Spatial Covariance Matrix Estimation and Spatially Regularized Independent Low-Rank Matrix Analysis With Fast Demixing Matrix Estimation" (IEEE Access, 2025) — [[sources/ishikawa-2025-real-time-speech-extraction|Ishikawa et al. 2025: Real-Time RCSCME-based Speech Extraction]]. Contributed to the experimental design and the application scenario of real-time speech extraction for human–avatar communication on edge devices (NVIDIA Jetson AGX).
