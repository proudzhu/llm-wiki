# Andrew Farnsworth

**Affiliation**: Cornell Lab of Ornithology
**Role**: Research Associate
**Research Focus**: Avian bioacoustics, migration monitoring, bird flight-call detection, acoustic sensor networks.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Per-Channel Energy Normalization: Why and How" (IEEE SPL 2019) — [[sources/lostanlen-2019-pcen-why-and-how|Lostanlen et al. 2019]]
- Co-developed the BirdVox project for avian flight-call detection and monitoring
- Bioacoustic bird species classification research (ICASSP 2017)
