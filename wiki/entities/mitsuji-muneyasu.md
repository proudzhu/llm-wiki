# Mitsuji Muneyasu

**Affiliation**: Faculty of Engineering, Kansai University, Japan
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive signal processing, digital filters.

## Key Contributions

- Co-proposer of the simultaneous equations method for ANC (1999)
- Hybrid ANC systems based on simultaneous equations method (2001)
- Experimental verification of the simultaneous equations method (2006)
- Methods for updating feedback control filter coefficients (2005)
