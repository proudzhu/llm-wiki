# Peter Plantinga

**Affiliation**: The Ohio State University, Columbus, OH, USA (at time of publication)
**Role**: Researcher; SpeechBrain toolkit developer
**Research Focus**: Robust speech recognition, speech enhancement, open-source speech-processing toolkits.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "MetricGAN+: An Improved Version of MetricGAN for Speech Enhancement" (Interspeech 2021) — the SpeechBrain-released MetricGAN+ recipe (noisy-speech discriminator targets, experience replay, learnable sigmoid mask; PESQ 3.15 on VoiceBank+DEMAND) — [[sources/fu-2021-metricgan-plus|Fu et al. 2021]]
- Co-authored "Spectral feature mapping with mimic loss for robust speech recognition" (ICASSP 2018), cited by MetricGAN+ as evidence that signal-level losses do not track WER
