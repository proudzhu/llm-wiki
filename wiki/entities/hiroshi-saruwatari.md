# Hiroshi Saruwatari

**Affiliation**: The University of Tokyo, Japan
**Role**: Professor, Researcher
**Research Focus**: Blind source separation, speech enhancement, microphone arrays, independent component analysis.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored Distributed FastMNMF with block-diagonal SCMs for distributed microphone arrays (arXiv 2026)
- Co-authored auxiliary-function-based decentralized independent vector analysis for distributed microphone arrays (APSIPA ASC 2025)
- Co-authored "A review of blind source separation methods: two converging routes to ILRMA originating from ICA and NMF" (APSIPA Trans. Signal Inf. Process. 2019) — [[sources/sawada-2019-bss-ilrma-review|Sawada et al. 2019: BSS/ILRMA Review]]. Provided the unifying survey of determined BSS along the ICA and NMF routes converging at ILRMA.
- Co-authored "Real-Time Speech Extraction Based on Rank-Constrained Spatial Covariance Matrix Estimation and Spatially Regularized Independent Low-Rank Matrix Analysis With Fast Demixing Matrix Estimation" (IEEE Access, 2025) — [[sources/ishikawa-2025-real-time-speech-extraction|Ishikawa et al. 2025: Real-Time RCSCME-based Speech Extraction]]. Contributed to the real-time blockwise batch framework, the SR-ILRMA / NSR-ILRMA regularizers, and the FastVCD / FastIP demixing-update algorithms.
