# Stefan Liebich

**Affiliation**: RWTH Aachen University, Institute of Communication Systems
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise cancellation for headphones, occlusion effect cancellation, own-voice perception with occluded ears, direction-of-arrival dependency of ANC systems, device-specific head-related transfer functions. Co-founder of Elevear GmbH (technology licensing for headphones and hearing aids).

## Key Contributions

- First-author investigation of DOA dependency of ANC in in-ear headphones using DHRTF measurements (INTERNOISE 2018)
- Derived analytical bounds on ANC attenuation as a function of magnitude and phase deviation
- First author of "Occlusion Effect Cancellation in Headphones and Hearing Devices—The Sister of Active Noise Cancellation" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2022) — joint OEC/ANC treatment with correction-filter decoupling, adaptive factor α, and 23-participant listening test — [[sources/liebich-2022-occlusion-effect-cancellation|Liebich & Vary 2022]]
