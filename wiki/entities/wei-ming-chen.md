# Wei-Ming Chen

**Affiliation**: MIT & National Taiwan University
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Efficient deep learning, TinyML, neural architecture search and inference engines for microcontrollers.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "MCUNet: Tiny Deep Learning on IoT Devices" (NeurIPS 2020) — [[sources/lin-2020-mcunet\|Lin et al. 2020: MCUNet]]; contributed to the TinyNAS architecture search and TinyEngine inference library co-design.
- Co-author of "MCUNetV2: Memory-Efficient Patch-based Inference for Tiny Deep Learning" (arXiv 2021) — [[sources/lin-2021-mcunetv2\|Lin et al. 2021: MCUNetV2]]; contributed to patch-based inference scheduling, receptive field redistribution, and the joint architecture + inference-scheduling NAS.
- Co-author of "Tiny Machine Learning: Progress and Futures" (IEEE Circuits and Systems Magazine 2023) — [[sources/lin-2023-tinyml-progress-futures\|Lin et al. 2023: TinyML — Progress and Futures]]; contributed to the survey of TinyML inference/training literature and the MCUNet-family narrative.
