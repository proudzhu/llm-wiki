# Matthias Rath

**Affiliation**: Institute for Advanced Procrastination, Berlin, Germany
**Role**: Independent researcher / software developer
**Research Focus**: Realtime audio signal processing, digital audio workstations, audio plugin architectures, mathematical foundations of audio DSP.

## Key Contributions

- Derivation of minimum delay formula for realtime block size adaptation (Linux Audio Conference 2026) with Matthias Geier, showing the minimum latency is $\Delta = b_\text{plugin} - \gcd(b_\text{host}, b_\text{plugin})$ — a result connecting elementary number theory (Bézout's identity) to practical audio buffer sizing
