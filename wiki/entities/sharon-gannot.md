# Sharon Gannot

**Affiliation**: Faculty of Engineering, Bar-Ilan University, Ramat-Gan, Israel
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, microphone array signal processing, beamforming, relative transfer function estimation, deep learning for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Linearly constrained deep beamformer for multi-speaker scenarios (arXiv 2026)
- Interpretable binaural deep beamforming guided by time-varying RTF (arXiv 2026)
- Signal enhancement using beamforming and nonstationarity with applications to speech (IEEE Trans. Signal Process. 2001)
- Consolidated perspective on multimicrophone speech enhancement and source separation (IEEE/ACM Trans. ASLP 2017)
- Multichannel eigenspace beamforming in reverberant noisy environment with multiple interfering speech signals (IEEE Trans. ASLP 2009)
- Co-authored "Audio Signal Processing in the 21st Century" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2023) — TC-AASP 25-year retrospective; the enhancement/beamforming sections draw on his RTF/MVDR/MWF work and consolidated multichannel-separation perspective (cited as [42], [43]) — [[sources/richard-2023-audio-signal-processing-21st-century|Richard et al. 2023]]
