# Debi Prasad Das

**Affiliation**: Process Engineering and Instrumentation Cell, CSIR-Institute of Minerals and Materials Technology, Bhubaneswar, India
**Role**: Scientist (CSIR)
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive signal processing, computationally efficient and hardware-suitable algorithms, microcontroller-based system development, image processing for the mineral industry.

## Background

Ph.D. in electronics and instrumentation engineering from NIT Rourkela (2004). Postdoctoral researcher at IIT Kharagpur (2003–2004), scientist at CSIR-CEERI Pilani (2005–2008), and visiting postdoctoral researcher in active noise control at the University of Adelaide (2010–2011). Recipient of the Orissa Young Scientists Award (2004), CSIR Young Scientist Award (2009), and the BOYSCAST Fellowship (2009–2010).

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of the **filtered-s LMS algorithm** for active mitigation of nonlinear noise processes (Das & Panda, IEEE Trans. Speech Audio Process. 2004; multichannel extension 2006) — a foundational nonlinear ANC algorithm.
- Co-author of "Particle swarm optimization based active noise control algorithm without secondary path identification" (IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas. 2012) — [[sources/rout-2012-pso-anc-without-secondary-path|Rout, Das & Panda 2012]].
