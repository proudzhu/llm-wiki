# Ziyue Zhao

**Affiliation**: GN Advanced Science, Ballerup, Denmark
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, acoustic echo control, voice quality enhancement for hands-free communication and speakerphones.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "DiffVQE: hybrid diffusion voice quality enhancement under acoustic echo and noise" (arXiv 2026) — [[sources/lugo-2026-diffvqe|Lugo, Seidel, Mowlaee, Zhao & Fingscheidt 2026]]. Jointly developed the first fully reproducible diffusion-based AEC system; single-step hybrid discriminative/generative design beating DeepVQE on most metrics at ~10% of its compute.
