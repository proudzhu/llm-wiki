# Stefan Gustafsson

**Affiliation**: Institute of Communication Systems and Data Processing (IND), RWTH Aachen University, Germany (at the time of his doctoral work); KPN Research, Leidschendam, The Netherlands (since 1999)
**Role**: Researcher / Scientific Advisor
**Research Focus**: Combined acoustic echo cancellation and noise reduction for hands-free telephony, psychoacoustically motivated speech enhancement, and (later) radio network planning and optimization for UMTS.

## Education

- **Dr.-Ing.** (1999) — Electrical engineering and information technology, Aachen University of Technology (RWTH), Germany; doctoral thesis on combined acoustic echo cancellation and noise reduction for hands-free telephony
- **M.Sc.** (1995) — Applied physics and electrical engineering, University of Linköping, Sweden

## Key Contributions

- First author of "A Psychoacoustic Approach to Combined Acoustic Echo Cancellation and Noise Reduction" (IEEE Trans. Speech and Audio Processing, 2002) — [[sources/gustafsson-2002-psychoacoustic-aec-nr|Gustafsson, Martin, Jax & Vary 2002]] — the original derivation of the $\tilde H^{IND}$ masking-threshold postfilter weighting rule for joint residual echo and noise attenuation
- Developed the equivalent-transfer-function residual echo PSD estimator and its conservative combination with the coherence method (with R. Martin, late 1990s)
- Co-proposed the psychoacoustically motivated noise-reduction weighting rule that preserves background noise characteristics (ICASSP 1998, with P. Jax and P. Vary)
