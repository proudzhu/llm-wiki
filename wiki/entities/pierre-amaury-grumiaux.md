# Pierre-Amaury Grumiaux

**Affiliation**: Nantes Université, École Centrale Nantes, CNRS, LS2N, Nantes, France; also Orange Labs, Cesson-Sévigné, France and Univ. Grenoble Alpes, GIPSA-lab, France
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning-based sound source localization; microphone array signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "A survey of sound source localization with deep learning methods" (JASA 2022) — [[sources/grumiaux-2022-ssl-deep-learning-survey|Grumiaux et al. 2022]], a 156-paper taxonomy of DL-based SSL.
- Co-authored CRNN and attention-based multi-source SSL systems with intensity features and joint source counting (Grumiaux et al. 2020, 2021a, 2021b, cited in the survey).
