# Hüseyin Hacihabiboglu

**Affiliation**: Informatics Institute, Middle East Technical University (METU), Ankara, Turkey (at the time of his 2012 paper; previously King's College London, 2008–2011)
**Role**: Researcher / Lecturer (audio signal processing)
**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, room acoustics modeling and simulation, multichannel audio systems, psychoacoustics of spatial hearing, game audio, and microphone arrays.

## Background

Received the B.Sc. (honors) degree from the Middle East Technical University (METU), Ankara, Turkey, in 2000, the M.Sc. degree from the University of Bristol, Bristol, U.K., in 2001 (both in electrical and electronic engineering), and the Ph.D. degree in computer science from Queen's University Belfast, Belfast, U.K., in 2004. Held research positions at the University of Surrey (2004–2008) and King's College London (2008–2011). Member of the IEEE Signal Processing Society, Audio Engineering Society (AES), Turkish Acoustics Society (TAD), and the European Acoustics Association (EAA).

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "On the Design and Implementation of Higher Order Differential Microphones" (IEEE TASLP 2012) — [[sources/desena-2012-higher-order-differential|De Sena, Hacihabiboglu & Cvetkovic 2012]] — the $(\alpha, \lambda)$ directivity design framework and the complex-root differential array structure.
- Co-holder of a microphone-array patent application and co-author of AES work on circular microphone arrays for panoramic recording with psychoacoustically motivated directivity design (with De Sena and Cvetkovic, 2010).
