# Jonathan Le Roux

**Affiliation**: Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), Cambridge, MA
**Role**: Researcher (Principal Research Scientist / Speech & Audio group lead)
**Research Focus**: Speech separation and enhancement, robust automatic speech recognition, deep learning for audio, phase reconstruction.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "On the compensation between magnitude and phase in speech separation" (IEEE Signal Processing Letters 2021) — [[sources/wang-2021-magnitude-phase-compensation|Wang, Wichern & Le Roux 2021]]
- Co-proposed the phase-sensitive mask training objective and recognition-boosted speech separation (ICASSP 2015)
- Co-proposed the SI-SDR evaluation metric ("SDR – Half-Baked or Well Done?", ICASSP 2019) and permutation invariant training / deep clustering line of work
- Co-authored explicit STFT consistency constraints for spectrograms and their application to phase reconstruction (SAPA 2008)
