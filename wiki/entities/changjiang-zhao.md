# Changjiang Zhao

**Affiliation**: Inner Mongolia University, School of Computer Science
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, state space models, inplace convolutional networks.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "SICRN: advancing speech enhancement through state space model and inplace convolution techniques" (arXiv 2024) — [[sources/zhao-2024-sicrn|Zhao, He & Zhang 2024: SICRN]]. Proposed the SIC block (S4ND + 2D inplace convolution with attention fusion) and the SICRN architecture, achieving near-SOTA quality on the DNS Challenge at 0.38× the parameters and 0.14× the MACs of FullSubNet with 0 ms look-ahead.
