# Cornelis Petersen

**Affiliation**: Bassett, Adelaide, Australia (formerly School of Mechanical Engineering, The University of Adelaide)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, virtual sensing, Kalman filtering for ANC, moving virtual sensing, optimal spatial sensing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored comprehensive review of virtual sensing algorithms for ANC (Algorithms, 2008)
- Developed Kalman filtering approach to virtual sensing for ANC (Mech. Syst. Signal Process. 2008)
- Developed Kalman filtering moving virtual sensing method
- Optimal spatially fixed and moving virtual sensing algorithms for local ANC (PhD thesis, 2007)
- Active noise control at moving locations using virtual sensing
