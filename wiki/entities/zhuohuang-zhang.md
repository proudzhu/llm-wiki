# Zhuohuang Zhang

**Affiliation**: Indiana University Bloomington, IN, USA (at time of ADL-MVDR); later Microsoft, now Shenzhen Polytechnic University
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multi-channel speech separation and enhancement, MVDR beamforming, phase perception in speech quality

## Key Contributions

- First author of "ADL-MVDR: All Deep Learning MVDR Beamformer for Target Speech Separation" (ICASSP 2021) — [[sources/zhang-2021-adl-mvdr|Zhang et al. 2021]] — replaced matrix inversion and PCA in the MVDR solution with GRU networks for stable joint training and frame-level beamforming weights.
- First author of "Multi-Channel Multi-Frame ADL-MVDR for Target Speech Separation" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2021) — journal extension of the ADL-MVDR framework.
- Co-author of "Investigation of phase distortion on perceived speech quality for hearing-impaired listeners" (Interspeech 2020, with D. S. Williamson and Y. Shen).
- Co-author of "ALL-Neural Beamformer for Continuous Speech Separation" (ICASSP 2022, Microsoft).
- Demo page for ADL-MVDR: [zzhang68.github.io/adlmvdr](https://zzhang68.github.io/adlmvdr/)
