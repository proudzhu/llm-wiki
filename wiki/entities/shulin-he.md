# Shulin He

**Affiliation**: Inner Mongolia University, School of Computer Science
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, state space models, inplace convolutional networks.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "SICRN: advancing speech enhancement through state space model and inplace convolution techniques" (arXiv 2024) — [[sources/zhao-2024-sicrn|Zhao, He & Zhang 2024: SICRN]]. Contributed to the SIC block design (combining S4ND with 2D inplace convolution via an attention map) and the SICRN architecture, evaluated on the INTERSPEECH 2020 DNS Challenge dataset.
