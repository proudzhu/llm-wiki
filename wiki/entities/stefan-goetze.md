# Stefan Goetze

**Affiliation**: Department of Computer Science, The University of Sheffield, UK (at time of publication)
**Role**: Researcher / Senior Lecturer
**Research Focus**: Acoustic signal processing — speech enhancement, dereverberation, multi-microphone processing, robust ASR front-ends, psychoacoustically motivated quality/intelligibility assessment.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "MetricGAN+/-: Increasing Robustness of Noise Reduction on Unseen Data" (EUSIPCO 2022) — senior author of the de-generator extension of MetricGAN+ — [[sources/close-2022-metricgan-plus-minus|Close, Hain & Goetze 2022]]
- Co-authored "A Study on Speech Quality and Speech Intelligibility Measures for Quality Assessment of Single-Channel Dereverberation Algorithms" (IWAENC 2014) — cited as evidence that signal-level distances correlate weakly with perceptual metrics, the motivation for metric-driven SE training
- Co-authored "Front-end technologies for robust ASR in reverberant environments" (EURASIP JASP 2015) — spectral enhancement-based dereverberation and auditory modulation filterbank features
