# Kunlong Zhao

**Kunlong Zhao** (赵坤龙) is a PhD student at the School of Electronic Information, Wuhan University, China (born 2000, Henan).

**Role**: Graduate researcher
**Research Focus**: Microphone array signal processing, speech enhancement.
