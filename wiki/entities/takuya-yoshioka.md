# Takuya Yoshioka

**Affiliation**: Microsoft, Redmond, WA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, target speech/sound extraction, speech processing for communication systems.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Semantic Hearing: Programming Acoustic Scenes with Binaural Hearables" (UIST 2023) — [[sources/veluri-2023-semantic-hearing|Veluri et al. 2023]].
- Co-author of Waveformer, "Real-Time Target Sound Extraction" (IEEE ICASSP 2023).
- Co-author of SoundBeam (2022) — target sound extraction conditioned on sound-class labels and enrollment clues, and of "Personalized Speech Enhancement: New Models and Comprehensive Evaluation" (IEEE ICASSP 2022).
- Co-author of "Exploring Practical Aspects of Neural Mask-Based Beamforming for Far-Field Speech Recognition" (ICASSP 2018) — practical evaluation of neural mask-based beamforming for far-field ASR (two arrays, two acoustic models, feature-level LogMel MSE loss, online variants) — [[sources/boeddeker-2018-neural-mask-beamforming|Boeddeker, Erdogan, Yoshioka & Haeb-Umbach 2018]]
