# Dai Gao

**Affiliation**: School of Future Science and Engineering, Soochow University, Suzhou, China
**Role**: Researcher (co-first author)
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, lightweight neural architectures, state-space models.

## Key Contributions

- Co-first authored "Lightweight Speech Enhancement with State-Space Model and Depthwise Separable Convolution" (*Digital Signal Processing*, 2026) — [[sources/jiang-2026-lightweight-speech-enhancement-ssm-dsc|Jiang, Gao, Wang, Zou & Liu 2026]]. Led conceptualization, methodology, and validation; co-wrote the original draft. The paper introduces [[concepts/lights4|lightS4]] (diagonal-constrained S4), the [[concepts/auditory-inspired-spectral-compressor|AISC]] module, and a [[concepts/classifier-loss|Classifier Loss]] for speaker-aware SE.
