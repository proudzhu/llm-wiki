# Aaron Wishnick

**Affiliation**: iZotope, Inc., Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA
**Role**: Researcher / DSP engineer
**Research Focus**: Time-varying digital filters for musical applications; virtual-analog filter design; audio effects.

## Key Contributions

- Authored "Time-Varying Filters for Musical Applications" (DAFx-14, 2014) — [[sources/wishnick-2014-time-varying-filters-musical|Wishnick 2014]] — proposed the TDF-II-discretized [[concepts/state-variable-filter|state variable filter]] as a general-purpose structure for per-sample time-varying musical filtering, proved its time-varying stability for the first time, and derived closed-form coefficients for arbitrary biquad transfer functions.
