# Brian C. J. Moore

**Affiliation**: Cambridge Hearing Group, Department of Psychology, University of Cambridge, UK
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Psychoacoustics, hearing aids, auditory perception, feedback cancellation

## Key Contributions

- L3C-DeepMFC: Low-latency low-complexity deep marginal feedback cancellation for hearing aids (Interspeech 2025)
- Deep learning solution to marginal stability problems of acoustic feedback systems (JASA 2022)
- Evaluation of deep marginal feedback cancellation using speech and music (Trends in Hearing 2023)
- CAMEQ2-HF: Method for deriving initial hearing aid fittings with multi-channel compression (IJA 2010)
- NAL-NL2 prescription procedure for hearing aids (Audiology Research 2011)
