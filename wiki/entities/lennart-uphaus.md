# Lennart Uphaus

**Affiliation**: Signal Processing Group, University of Hamburg, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Low-latency neural speech enhancement and neural directional filtering for hearing devices

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Directivity-Conditioned Low-Latency Neural Filtering for Speech Enhancement in Hearing Aids" (arXiv 2026) — [[sources/uphaus-2026-directivity-low-latency|Uphaus et al. 2026]]. Proposed FiLM-OSN, a 10 ms-latency steerable neural directional filter for binaural BTE hearing aids, with an IPD preservation loss shown essential for realizing the conditioned directivity pattern.
