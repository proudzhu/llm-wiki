# Mads Østergaard

**Affiliation**: WS Audiology, Lynge, Denmark
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, own-voice cancellation, low-latency neural audio processing for hearing aids and far-field devices.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced [[concepts/own-voice-cancellation|own-voice cancellation (OVC)]] as a novel task for mitigating latency-induced distortion in streamed far-field denoising (arXiv 2026)
- Proposed the [[concepts/mamba-mingru|Mamba-MinGRU]] architecture for compute-efficient, low-latency (2 ms) own-voice cancellation
