# Haljan Lugo

**Affiliation**: Institute for Communications Technology, Technische Universität Braunschweig, Germany (Haljan Lugo Girao, M.Sc.)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, acoustic echo control, generative (diffusion) models for voice quality enhancement, hands-free communication systems.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "DiffVQE: hybrid diffusion voice quality enhancement under acoustic echo and noise" (arXiv 2026) — [[sources/lugo-2026-diffvqe|Lugo, Seidel, Mowlaee, Zhao & Fingscheidt 2026]]. Proposed the first fully reproducible diffusion-based AEC model: a single-step hybrid Cond DNN + Score DNN framework adapted from EffDiffSE that outperforms a retrained DeepVQE on most quality and intelligibility metrics at ~10–13% of its FLOPS.
