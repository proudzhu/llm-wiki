# Matti Karjalainen

**Affiliation**: Helsinki University of Technology, Laboratory of Acoustics and Audio Signal Processing, Espoo, Finland

**Role**: Professor / AES Fellow

**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, physical modeling of musical instruments, digital waveguide synthesis, warped filter design, acoustic measurement.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Frequency-Warped Signal Processing for Audio Applications" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2000) — [[sources/harma-2000-frequency-warped-signal-processing|Härmä et al. 2000]]
- Pioneered warped filter design for body modeling and sound synthesis of string instruments (NAM'96)
- Co-developed auditory filter bank designs based on Bark warping (NORSIG 1996)
- Co-authored comparison of loudspeaker equalization methods based on DSP techniques (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 1999)
