# Delf Sachau

**Delf Sachau** is a Professor of Mechatronics at the Helmut Schmidt University in Hamburg, Germany.

## Research Focus

Professor Sachau's research focuses on **Active Vibration Control** and **Active Noise Control** in structural systems. He specializes in:
- Adaptive control of smart structures.
- Active damping of vibrations in automotive and aerospace components.
- Co-supervising research on hybrid ANC systems for headphones with **[[udo-zolzer|Udo Zölzer]]** and **[[piero-iared-rivera-benois|Piero Iared Rivera Benois]]**.
