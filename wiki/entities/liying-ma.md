# Liying Ma

**Affiliation**: Dept. of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Concordia University, Montreal, H3G 1M8, Canada
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Adaptive signal processing for ANC; online path modeling; narrowband ANC with IIR notch filters; supporting-filter and AWGN scheduling strategies.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author, "A robust FFANC system with simultaneous OSPM and OFBPM" (*Signal Processing*, 2027) — investigation and writing review.
- Co-author on multiple earlier narrowband ANC systems with simultaneous OSPM/OFBPM (ICSV 2019; APCCAS 2019; IEEE/ACM TASLP 2024 hybrid ANC paper).
- Co-author, "An efficient filter bank structure for adaptive notch filtering and applications" (*IEEE/ACM TASLP*, 2021).
