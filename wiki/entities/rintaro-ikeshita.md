# Rintaro Ikeshita

**Affiliation**: NTT Corporation, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Blind source separation, independent vector extraction, dereverberation, speech enhancement

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Switching IVA and Its Extension to Blind and Spatially Guided Convolutional Beamforming" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2022) — switching-mechanism extensions of IVA/CIVA (swIVA/swCIVA) for blind separation and dereverberation with few microphones — [[sources/nakatani-2022-switching-iva|Nakatani et al. 2022: Switching IVA]]
- Co-author of Independent Low-Rank Matrix Analysis with decorrelation learning (IEEE WASPAA 2019) — one of the computationally efficient CIVA variants benchmarked in the switching framework.
- Co-author of "Blind signal dereverberation based on mixture of weighted prediction error models" (IEEE Signal Processing Letters 2021) — the swWPE algorithm that the switching CIVA framework builds upon.
- Co-author of "Blind and Spatially-Regularized Online Joint Optimization of Source Separation, Dereverberation, and Noise Reduction" (IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech, and Language Processing, vol. 32, 2024) — first blind online joint optimization of source separation, dereverberation, and noise reduction under a single ML criterion (online-WPE×IVE), extended with scale-regularized spatial alignment (online-WPE×SRIVE) achieving 0% permutation error at 8 ms algorithmic delay — [[sources/ueda-2024-online-joint-optimization|Ueda, Nakatani, Ikeshita, Kinoshita, Araki & Makino 2024]]
