# Yiqiang Cai

## Overview

Yiqiang Cai is a PhD student at the **School of Advanced Technology**, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) in Suzhou, China. His research focuses on developing deep learning algorithms and architectures for audio signal processing, particularly low-complexity models for acoustic scene classification.

## Research Areas

- Audio Signal Processing
- Acoustic Scene Classification (ASC)
- Neural Network Compression & Efficient Architectures
- Domain Generalization / Domain Adaptation

## Notable Work

- [[sources/cai-2024-tf-sepnet|Cai, Zhang & Li 2024: TF-SepNet]] — First author, proposing parallel 1D kernels to process time and frequency features separately, reducing MACs by 59% and parameters by 38-39%.
