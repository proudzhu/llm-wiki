# Yutaka Maeda

**Affiliation**: Department of Electrical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Kansai University, Suita, Osaka, Japan
**Role**: Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Simultaneous perturbation learning rules for neural networks, neuro-control, and their applications to active noise control.

## Key Contributions

- Independently proposed the simultaneous perturbation method as a learning rule for neural networks (1993–1997), demonstrating its feasibility in control problems and analog VLSI implementation — work parallel to Spall's SPSA and Cauwenberghs' stochastic error descent
- Proposed the time-difference simultaneous perturbation method, reducing the objective-function measurements per update from two to one (Electronics Letters, 1996)
- First author of "An Active Noise Control Without Estimation of Secondary-Path — ANC Using Simultaneous Perturbation —" (ACTIVE 99) — [[sources/maeda-1999-anc-simultaneous-perturbation|Maeda & Yoshida 1999]], applying perturbation-based stochastic gradient descent to ANC so that no secondary-path estimate is needed
