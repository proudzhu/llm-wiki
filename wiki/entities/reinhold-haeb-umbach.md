# Reinhold Haeb-Umbach

**Affiliation**: Paderborn University, Germany (Communications Engineering / Machine Learning research groups)
**Role**: Professor / Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, microphone array signal processing, hybrid model-based and data-driven methods, robust ASR front-ends

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Microphone Array Signal Processing and Deep Learning for Speech Enhancement: Combining model-based and data-driven approaches to parameter estimation and filtering" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2024) — [[sources/haeb-umbach-2024-microphone-array-deep-learning|Haeb-Umbach et al. 2024]]; the article's three-class taxonomy of hybrid enhancement methods and its "model as regularizer" argument.
- Co-author of neural-network-based spectral mask estimation for acoustic beamforming (Heymann, Drude & Haeb-Umbach, ICASSP 2016) — an early Class-1 hybrid (DNN masks driving MVDR beamforming).
- Co-author of the CHiME-5 dinner-party front-end processing paper introducing guided source separation (Boeddeker et al. 2018).
- Co-author of "Speech Processing for Digital Home Assistance: Combining Signal Processing with Deep-Learning Techniques" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2019).
