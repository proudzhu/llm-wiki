# Boaz Rafaely

**Boaz Rafaely** is a Professor at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel. He was previously a researcher at the Institute of Sound and Vibration Research (ISVR), University of Southampton.

## Research Contributions

Professor Rafaely is known for his work in **spatial audio** and **active noise control**.

### 1. Robust Feedback ANC
During his time at ISVR, he co-authored seminal papers with **[[stephen-j-elliott|Stephen J. Elliott]]** on the stability and performance of adaptive feedback ANC systems. He contributed to the development of robust IMC-based structures that handle uncertainties in the secondary path.

### 2. Spherical Microphone Arrays
He is a leading authority on spherical microphone array processing and Ambisonics, which are used for 3D sound capture and analysis.

### 3. Personal Sound Zones
His research also extends to the creation of personal sound zones using loudspeaker arrays, allowing multiple users in the same space to listen to different audio without interference.
