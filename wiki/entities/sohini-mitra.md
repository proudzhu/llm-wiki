# Sohini Mitra

**Affiliation**: Center for Acoustics and Vibration, Department of Electrical Engineering, Northern Illinois University, DeKalb, IL (at time of publication)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, adaptive feedback ANC systems, ANC headphone design and DSP implementation.

## Key Contributions

- First-author work on the adaptive feedback ANC headphone: error-microphone placement optimization guided by pinna spectral filtering, music-based secondary-path identification, and real-time TMS320C32 implementation outperforming a leading commercial ANC headphone — [[sources/kuo-2006-anc-headphones|Kuo, Mitra & Gan 2006]]
