# Cheng Yu

**Affiliation**: Research Center for Information Technology Innovation, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning for speech enhancement; metric-driven training.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "MetricGAN+: An Improved Version of MetricGAN for Speech Enhancement" (Interspeech 2021) — three domain-knowledge training techniques (noisy-speech discriminator targets, larger replay buffer, learnable sigmoid mask) lifting MetricGAN to PESQ 3.15 on VoiceBank+DEMAND — [[sources/fu-2021-metricgan-plus|Fu et al. 2021]]
- Co-authored "Boosting Objective Scores of a Speech Enhancement Model by MetricGAN Post-processing" (APSIPA ASC 2020) — [[sources/fu-2020-metricgan-post-processing|Fu et al. 2020]]
