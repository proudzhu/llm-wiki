# Mike Goodwin

**Affiliation**: Amazon Web Services, Palo Alto, CA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, machine learning for audio, low-complexity neural speech and audio processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Framewise WaveGAN: High Speed Adversarial Vocoder in Time Domain with Very Low Computational Complexity" (ICASSP 2023, pp. 5596–5600) — the 1.2-GFLOPS framewise GAN vocoder that made adversarial time-domain synthesis practical on CPUs — [[sources/mustafa-2023-framewise-wavegan|Mustafa et al. 2023]]
- Co-authored "NoLACE: Improving Low-Complexity Speech Codec Enhancement Through Adaptive Temporal Shaping" (ICASSP 2024) — neural Opus codec enhancement, with Büthe, Valin, Helwani (referenced from [[entities/ahmed-mustafa|Ahmed Mustafa]])
- Co-author of noise-robust DSP-assisted neural pitch estimation with very low complexity (ICASSP 2024, with Subramani, Valin, Smaragdis) — referenced from [[entities/jan-buthe|Jan Büthe]]
