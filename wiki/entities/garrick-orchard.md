# Garrick Orchard

**Affiliation**: Neuromorphic Computing Lab, Intel Labs (work done during Intel employment)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Neuromorphic computing, spiking neural networks, event-based vision, SNN learning algorithms (SLAYER).

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "The Intel Neuromorphic DNS Challenge" (arXiv 2023) — [[sources/timcheck-2023-intel-neuromorphic-dns-challenge|Timcheck et al. 2023]].
- Co-originator of SLAYER (Shrestha & Orchard, 2018), a surrogate-gradient backpropagation framework for SNNs that underlies the Lava-dl training tool used in the N-DNS Challenge baseline.
- Contributor to event-based vision and neuromorphic algorithm research, including DVS Gesture recognition work cited within the N-DNS Challenge paper.
