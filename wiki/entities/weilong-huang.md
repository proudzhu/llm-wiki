# Weilong Huang

**Affiliation**: International Audio Laboratories Erlangen, Friedrich-Alexander University Erlangen-Nuremberg (FAU), Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Neural directional filtering, spatial audio, deep learning for audio signal processing.
