# Andrew J. Fleming

**Andrew J. Fleming** is a Professor of Precision Mechatronics at the University of Newcastle, Australia.

## Research Focus

Professor Fleming specializes in the design, modeling, and control of precision mechatronic systems, particularly those involving **nanopositioning** and **active vibration control**.

### 1. Active Vibration Control
He has co-authored significant work on using **[[../concepts/model-predictive-control|Model Predictive Control]]** and other advanced control strategies to manage structural vibrations. His research often involves handling the physical constraints (saturation) of piezoelectric actuators, which are common in precision systems.

### 2. Nanopositioning
He is a world expert on high-speed nanopositioning for applications like Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM), where high-bandwidth feedback control is required to achieve sub-nanometer precision.
