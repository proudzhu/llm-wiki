# Hou-I. Liu

**Affiliation**: Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Hsinchu, Taiwan, ROC
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Lightweight deep learning, efficient neural network architectures, computer vision, multimedia computing.

## Key Contributions
