# Florian Strub

**Affiliation**: Univ. Lille, CNRS, Centrale Lille, Inria, UMR 9189 CRIStAL, France (at time of publication)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Visual reasoning, multimodal learning, deep reinforcement learning, and neural network conditioning.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer" (AAAI 2018) — [[sources/perez-2018-film-visual-reasoning|Perez, Strub, de Vries, Dumoulin & Courville 2018]], which introduced [[concepts/film-layer|Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM)]] as a general conditioning layer and achieved state-of-the-art on CLEVR visual reasoning.
