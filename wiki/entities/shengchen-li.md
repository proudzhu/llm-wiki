# Shengchen Li

## Overview

Dr. Shengchen Li is an Associate Professor in the **Department of Intelligent Science** at the **School of Advanced Technology**, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) in Suzhou, China. He leads a research group focused on machine listening, computer music, and acoustic signal processing. Dr. Li is a Senior Member of the IEEE and a member of the Speech Dialogue and Auditory Processing Task Force within the China Computer Federation.

## Research Areas

- Machine Listening & Acoustic Signal Processing
- Auditory Intelligence & Domain Adaptation
- Computer Music & Piano Performance Evaluation
- Deep Learning Algorithms Hardware Implementation

## Notable Work

- [[sources/cai-2024-tf-sepnet|Cai, Zhang & Li 2024: TF-SepNet]] — Corresponding author/Supervisor, proposing parallel 1D kernels to process time and frequency features separately.
