# Qirui Huang

**Affiliation**: School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Active noise control, generative fixed-filter ANC, deep learning for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored Delayless generative fixed-filter ANC based on deep learning and Bayesian filter (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2024)
- Co-authored GFANC-Kalman: Generative fixed-filter ANC with CNN-Kalman filtering (IEEE SPL 2024)
- Co-authored Transformer-based End-to-End Control-Filter Generation (E2E-CFG) for ANC (arXiv 2026)
