# Jyri Huopaniemi

**Affiliation**: Nokia Research Center, Speech and Audio Systems Laboratory, Helsinki, Finland

**Role**: Researcher / AES Member

**Research Focus**: Binaural technology, 3-D sound, head-related transfer function (HRTF) filter design, virtual acoustics, multimedia signal processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Frequency-Warped Signal Processing for Audio Applications" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2000) — [[sources/harma-2000-frequency-warped-signal-processing|Härmä et al. 2000]]
- Investigated warped filters for binaural and crosstalk-canceled binaural filter design (AES conventions 1997, 1999)
- Co-authored objective and subjective evaluation of HRTF filter design methods (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 1999)
- Published "Virtual Acoustics and 3-D Sound in Multimedia Signal Processing" (Ph.D. thesis, Helsinki University of Technology, 1999)
