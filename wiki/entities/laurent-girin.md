# Laurent Girin

**Affiliation**: Univ. Grenoble Alpes, Grenoble-INP, GIPSA-lab, Saint-Martin-d'Hères, France
**Role**: Professor / Researcher
**Research Focus**: Audio and speech signal processing; variational autoencoders and dynamical VAEs for speech/audio modeling; sound source localization.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "A survey of sound source localization with deep learning methods" (JASA 2022) — [[sources/grumiaux-2022-ssl-deep-learning-survey|Grumiaux et al. 2022]].
- Author of foundational work on dynamical variational autoencoders (Girin et al. 2021, cited in the survey's perspectives on combining deep generative models with conventional spatial-processing models).
