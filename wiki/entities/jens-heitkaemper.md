# Jens Heitkaemper

**Affiliation**: Google LLC
**Role**: Research Scientist / Inventor
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, bone conduction signal processing, automatic speech recognition.

## Key Contributions

- BCS-guided speech enhancement for earbud voice assistants using Conformer-based fusion of air-conducted and bone-conducted signals (US Patent US20260073929A1, 2026)
- First-authored "Bone Conducted Signal Guided Speech Enhancement for Voice Assistant on Earbuds" (ICASSP 2025) — the publication of the patent system, adding BCS bandwidth reduction to 500 Hz (6.25% of original), VAD-gated handling of distorted BCS, and WER evaluation against a multi-channel enhancer — [[sources/heitkaemper-2025-bcs-speech-enhancement-earbuds|Heitkaemper et al. 2025]]
