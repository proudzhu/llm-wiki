# lisyarus

**lisyarus** (Alexey) is a software engineer, blogger, and independent game developer known for deep technical explorations of computer graphics, mathematics, and systems programming.

## Overview

He maintains a technical blog at [lisyarus.github.io](https://lisyarus.github.io/), where he writes extensively about topics such as:
- **Geometry and Physics Simulation**: Implementing robust algorithms for collision detection, convex hulls, and splines.
- **Computer Graphics**: Modern OpenGL/Vulkan techniques, rendering pipelines, and noise generation.
- **Floating-point Math**: Analyzing the practical behavior of [[concepts/ieee-754|IEEE 754]] and common pitfalls in numerical programming.
- **Game Engine Design**: Documenting the development of his own custom engines and tools.
