# Feng Dang

**Affiliation**: Key Laboratory of Speech Acoustics & Content Understanding, Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, dual-path transformer architectures, complex-domain time-frequency modeling.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "DPT-FSNet: Dual-Path Transformer Based Full-Band and Sub-Band Fusion Network for Speech Enhancement" (ICASSP 2022) — [[sources/dang-2022-dpt-fsnet|Dang, Chen & Zhang 2022]]: transplanted the dual-path transformer to the STFT domain with intra/sub-band and inter/full-band paths; SOTA on VoiceBank+DEMAND (PESQ 3.33) and DNS at 0.88 M params.
