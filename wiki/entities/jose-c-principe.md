# José C. Príncipe

## Overview

Professor at the **Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering**, University of Florida, Gainesville, FL. Pioneer of **Information Theoretic Learning (ITL)** and **correntropy** theory.

## Research Areas

- Information Theoretic Learning (ITL)
- [[concepts/correntropy|Correntropy]] — Introduced the concept and its application to machine learning
- Neural networks and adaptive signal processing
- Brain-machine interfaces

## Notable Contributions

- Introduced correntropy as a nonlinear similarity measure for signal processing
- Developed the theoretical foundation connecting correntropy to Renyi's entropy
- Supervised and collaborated on [[concepts/generalized-correntropy|Generalized Correntropy]] work
