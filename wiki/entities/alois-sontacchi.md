# Alois Sontacchi

**Affiliation**: Institute of Electronic Music and Acoustics, University of Music and Performing Arts Graz, Austria
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, virtual sensing for ANC, neural observation filters, secondary path interpolation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored CNN-based neural observation filter for online RMT estimation (Forum Acusticum/Euronoise 2025)
- Co-authored Obs-TasNet for online estimation of virtual sensing observation filters (ICASSP 2026)
- Co-authored DTW-based secondary path interpolation for local ANC with moving listeners (IEEE OJSP 2026)
