# Silviu Ciochină

**Affiliation**: University Politehnica of Bucharest, Romania
**Role**: Researcher, Professor
**Research Focus**: Adaptive filtering, system identification, echo cancellation, variable step-size and regularization control.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Recursive Least-Squares Algorithms for the Identification of Low-Rank Systems" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2019) — [[sources/elisei-iliescu-2019-low-rank-rls|Elisei-Iliescu et al. 2019]] — RLS-NKP low-rank adaptive filters with variable regularization and DCD-based solving.
- Earlier work on joint optimization of normalized step-size and regularization parameters (Ciochină et al. 2015), used as the adaptive-algorithm basis of the subband NLMS echo canceller in [[sources/seidel-2024-bark-scale-nn-residual-suppression|Seidel et al. 2024]].
