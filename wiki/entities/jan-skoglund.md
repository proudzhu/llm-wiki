# Jan Skoglund

**Affiliation**: Google LLC, San Francisco, CA, USA
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Neural speech and audio coding, low-bitrate neural vocoders, speech quality evaluation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "LPCNet: Improving Neural Speech Synthesis Through Linear Prediction" (ICASSP 2019, pp. 5891–5895) — the [[concepts/lpcnet|LPCNet]] hybrid vocoder that combines linear prediction with WaveRNN for speaker-independent synthesis under 3 GFLOPS — [[sources/valin-2018-lpcnet|Valin & Skoglund 2018]]
- Co-authored "Wavenet based low rate speech coding" (ICASSP 2018, pp. 676–680) — the WaveNet-based very-low-bitrate coder whose quantized conditioning features motivate LPCNet's feature set
- Co-authored "A Real-Time Wideband Neural Vocoder at 1.6 kb/s Using LPCNet" (INTERSPEECH 2019) — the low-bitrate coding application of LPCNet, outperforming MELP at 1.6 kb/s
- Co-authored "Improving Opus Low Bit Rate Quality with Neural Speech Synthesis" (INTERSPEECH 2020) — neural-synthesis enhancement of the Opus codec at low bitrates
- Co-authored "Neural Speech and Audio Coding" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine, 2025, with Minje Kim) — a survey framing LPCNet among the hybrid model-based/data-driven codec designs
