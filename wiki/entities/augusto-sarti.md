# Augusto Sarti

**Affiliation**: Dipartimento di Elettronica, Informazione e Bioingegneria (DEIB), Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
**Role**: Professor (Senior Member, IEEE)
**Research Focus**: Audio signal processing, sound analysis and synthesis, room acoustics, networked music performance, distributed audio processing.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Hybrid Packet Loss Concealment for Real-Time Networked Music Applications" (IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing 2024) — senior author of **PARCnet**, the linear-autoregressive + neural-residual hybrid PLC method for 32 kHz networked music signals — [[sources/mezza-2024-hybrid-plc|Mezza et al. 2024]]
- Co-author of "An overview on networked music performance technologies" (IEEE Access 2016) — the survey that frames the latency/fidelity/bandwidth constraints of NMP systems the PLC work addresses
