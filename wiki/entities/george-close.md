# George Close

**Affiliation**: Department of Computer Science, The University of Sheffield, UK
**Role**: Researcher (Centre for Doctoral Training in Speech and Language Technologies, UKRI-funded)
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, metric-driven training, robustness of noise reduction to unseen data.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "MetricGAN+/-: Increasing Robustness of Noise Reduction on Unseen Data" (EUSIPCO 2022) — introduces the de-generator, a third network trained toward a sub-perfect metric score so the MetricGAN+ discriminator observes a wider score range; PESQ 3.05 → 3.22 on VoiceBank+DEMAND and better CHiME-3 generalization — [[sources/close-2022-metricgan-plus-minus|Close, Hain & Goetze 2022]]
