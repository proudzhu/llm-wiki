# Sergio L. Netto

**Affiliation**: Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering (DEL/Poli) and Program of Electrical Engineering (PEE/COPPE), Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), Brazil
**Role**: Associate Professor (since 1997 at DEL/Poli, since 1998 at PEE/COPPE)
**Research Focus**: Adaptive signal processing, digital filter design, speech processing (synthesis and coding).

## Biography

Born in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Received the B.S. degree (cum laude) from UFRJ in 1991, the M.S. degree from COPPE/UFRJ in 1992, and the Ph.D. degree from the University of Victoria, BC, Canada, in 1996, all in electrical engineering. Co-author (with P. S. R. Diniz and E. A. B. da Silva) of *Digital Signal Processing: System Analysis and Design* (Cambridge University Press, 2002).
