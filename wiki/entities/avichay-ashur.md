# Avichay Ashur

**Affiliation**: Andrew and Erna Viterbi Faculty of Electrical & Computer Engineering, Technion — Israel Institute of Technology, Haifa, Israel
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Acoustic howling suppression, deep learning for speech enhancement, fine-tuning strategies for joint noise reduction and feedback suppression.

## Key Contributions

- Acoustic howling suppression enhancement by fine-tuning deep speech enhancement networks (Preprint 2026) — adapts a pretrained DEMUCS-based Denoiser for AHS by mixing offline-generated howling samples with original noise-reduction data; identifies 60-40 mixing ratio as the optimal trade-off, achieving state-of-the-art PESQ stability across gain levels.
