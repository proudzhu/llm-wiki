# Gaël Richard

**Affiliation**: Télécom Paris (Institut Polytechnique de Paris), France
**Role**: Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Audio and speech signal processing, music information retrieval (MIR), source separation, audio coding, deep generative models for audio.

## Key Contributions

- Lead/corresponding author of "Audio Signal Processing in the 21st Century" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2023) — the TC-AASP 25-year retrospective — [[sources/richard-2023-audio-signal-processing-21st-century|Richard et al. 2023]]
- Co-authored coding-based informed source separation via nonnegative tensor factorization (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2013, cited as [3] in the retrospective)
- Co-authored late-reverberation synthesis linking radiance transfer to feedback delay networks (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2015, cited as [8])
