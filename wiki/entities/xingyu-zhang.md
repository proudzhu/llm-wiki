# Xingyu Zhang

**Affiliation**: Defense Innovation Institute, Academy of Military Sciences (AMS), Beijing, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Deep learning for speech enhancement and recognition, bone-conducted speech processing, multisensory (audio/visual/EMG) speech interfaces.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "DBMIF: a deep balanced multimodal iterative fusion framework for air- and bone-conduction speech enhancement" (Applied Intelligence 2026) — [[sources/wu-2026-dbmif-multimodal-fusion|Wu et al. 2026]]
- Co-author of AveSpeech, a multimodal speech-recognition dataset integrating audio, visual and electromyographic signals (IEEE Trans. Human-Machine Systems 2025)
- Earlier work on bone-conducted speech spectral restoration with attention-based contextual modelling (IEICE Trans. Fundamentals 2019)
