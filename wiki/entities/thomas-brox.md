# Thomas Brox

**Affiliation**: Computer Science Department, University of Freiburg, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Computer vision, optical flow, unsupervised and self-supervised feature learning, video and image segmentation.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation" (MICCAI 2015), introducing the U-shaped encoder–decoder with skip connections that became the default biomedical segmentation backbone — [[sources/ronneberger-2015-u-net|Ronneberger 2015]]
