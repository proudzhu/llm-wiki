# Han Cai

**Affiliation**: MIT (MIT HAN Lab)
**Role**: Researcher / PhD candidate
**Research Focus**: Efficient deep learning, neural architecture search (Once-for-All), hardware-aware model design, TinyML on microcontrollers.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "MCUNetV2: Memory-Efficient Patch-based Inference for Tiny Deep Learning" (arXiv 2021) — [[sources/lin-2021-mcunetv2\|Lin et al. 2021: MCUNetV2]]; co-developed patch-based inference scheduling, receptive field redistribution, and the joint architecture + inference-scheduling NAS.
- Lead author of "Once-for-All: Train One Network and Specialize it for Efficient Deployment" (ICLR 2020) — introduced a super-network that can be specialized to many deployment scenarios without retraining.
- Co-author of "ProxylessNAS: Direct Neural Architecture Search on Target Task and Hardware" (ICLR 2019) — hardware-aware NAS with customized latency/energy targets.
