# Shoji Makino

**Shoji Makino** is a professor at the Graduate School of Information, Production and Systems, Waseda University, Japan.

## Research Areas

- Blind source separation
- Speech enhancement
- Acoustic signal processing
- Microphone array processing

## Notable Contributions

- Pioneering work on blind speech separation using time-frequency masking
- Time-frequency-bin-wise linear combination of beamformers
- SCM reconstruction for multi-source speech enhancement (Liu et al. 2026)
- SR-SwIVA-ISS: Fast optimization for spatially regularized switching IVA (Dong et al. 2026)
