# Biing-Hwang (Fred) Juang

**Affiliation**: Center for Signal and Image Processing, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, GA, USA (formerly Bell Laboratories)
**Role**: Senior Researcher / Professor
**Research Focus**: Speech processing, acoustic echo cancellation, speech recognition, and signal processing for telecommunications.

## Key Contributions

- Co-developed (with [[entities/ted-wada|Ted S. Wada]]) the robust AEC with error recovery nonlinearity (ERN) and batch adaptation (Proc. IEEE WASPAA 2009), the foundation cited as [1] in [[sources/wung-2011-residual-echo-suppression-system|Wung et al. 2011]].
- Senior author of "A system approach to residual echo suppression in robust hands-free teleconferencing" (Proc. IEEE ICASSP 2011) — [[sources/wung-2011-residual-echo-suppression-system|Wung et al. 2011]]
