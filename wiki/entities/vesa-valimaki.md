# Vesa Välimäki

**Affiliation**: Helsinki University of Technology, Laboratory of Acoustics and Audio Signal Processing, Espoo, Finland; later Aalto University

**Role**: Researcher / AES Member

**Research Focus**: Digital audio effects, fractional delay filters, digital waveguide synthesis, interpolated waveguide mesh, sound synthesis.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Frequency-Warped Signal Processing for Audio Applications" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2000) — [[sources/harma-2000-frequency-warped-signal-processing|Härmä et al. 2000]]
- Co-developed the interpolated digital waveguide mesh for reduced dispersion (ICASSP'97; IEEE Trans. Speech Audio Process., 2000)
- Applied frequency warping to dispersion error reduction in triangular and interpolated digital waveguide meshes (IEEE Signal Process. Lett., 1999; ICASSP'99)
- Co-authored "Splitting the Unit Delay — Tools for Fractional Delay Filter Design" (IEEE Signal Process. Mag., 1996), a foundational reference for fractional delay techniques
