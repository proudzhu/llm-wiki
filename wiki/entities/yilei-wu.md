# Yilei Wu

**Affiliation**: Defense Innovation Institute, Academy of Military Sciences (AMS), Beijing, China
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multimodal (air- and bone-conducted) speech enhancement, GAN-based generative speech models, subband signal processing, cross-modal fusion architectures.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "DBMIF: a deep balanced multimodal iterative fusion framework for air- and bone-conduction speech enhancement" (Applied Intelligence 2026) — three-branch iterative AC/BC fusion with DIAF, CBGI and a parameter-shared DBI equilibrium bottleneck; SI-SDR 12.42 dB / PESQ 2.70 and the lowest downstream ASR CER among nine compared systems — [[sources/wu-2026-dbmif-multimodal-fusion|Wu et al. 2026]]
