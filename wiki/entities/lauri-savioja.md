# Lauri Savioja

**Affiliation**: Helsinki University of Technology, Telecommunications Software and Multimedia Laboratory, Espoo, Finland

**Role**: Researcher / AES Member

**Research Focus**: Room acoustics simulation, digital waveguide mesh methods, finite-difference time-domain modeling, dispersion error reduction.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Frequency-Warped Signal Processing for Audio Applications" (J. Audio Eng. Soc., 2000) — [[sources/harma-2000-frequency-warped-signal-processing|Härmä et al. 2000]]
- Extended the digital waveguide mesh to 3-D room acoustics simulation (ICMC'94)
- Developed the interpolated digital waveguide mesh to reduce dispersion error (ICASSP'97; IEEE Trans. Speech Audio Process., 2000)
- Applied frequency warping to reduce dispersion errors in digital waveguide meshes (IEEE Signal Process. Lett., 1999; ICASSP'99)
