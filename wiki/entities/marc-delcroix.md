# Marc Delcroix

**Affiliation**: NTT Communication Science Laboratories, Kyoto, Japan
**Role**: Senior Research Scientist
**Research Focus**: Target speech extraction, robust ASR, speech enhancement, multi-modal processing

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Neural Target Speech Extraction: An Overview" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2023) — [[sources/zmolikova-2023-neural-target-speech-extraction-overview|Zmolikova et al. 2023]].
- Single-channel target speaker extraction and recognition with SpeakerBeam (ICASSP 2018, [25])
- End-to-end SpeakerBeam for single-channel target speech recognition (Interspeech 2019, [45])
- Multimodal SpeakerBeam: single-channel target speech extraction with audio-visual speaker clues (Interspeech 2019, [33])
- ConceptBeam: concept-driven target speech extraction (ACM Multimedia 2022, [60])
