# Kaiming He (何恺明)

**Affiliation**: Associate Professor (tenured), Department of EECS, MIT; Distinguished Scientist, Google DeepMind (part-time)
**Email**: kaiming@mit.edu
**Homepage**: [people.csail.mit.edu/kaiming](http://people.csail.mit.edu/kaiming/)
**Research areas**: Computer vision, deep learning

## Biography

- **B.S.** (2007): Tsinghua University
- **Ph.D.** (2011): The Chinese University of Hong Kong
- **2011–2016**: Researcher, Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA)
- **2016–2024**: Research Scientist, Facebook AI Research (FAIR)
- **2024–present**: Associate Professor, MIT EECS

## Key Contributions

Kaiming He is best known for:

- **Deep Residual Networks (ResNets)** — the most-cited paper of the 21st century per a Nature article; residual connections are now ubiquitous in modern deep learning (Transformers/ChatGPT, AlphaGo Zero, AlphaFold, virtually all GenAI models). CVPR 2016 Best Paper.
- **Faster R-CNN** (with Ren, Girshick, Sun, 2015) — established the core paradigm of modern [[concepts/object-detection|object detection]]; won the NeurIPS 2025 Test of Time Award.
- **Mask R-CNN** (2017) — extended detection to instance segmentation; ICCV 2017 Best Paper.
- **Self-supervised learning**: MoCo, MAE.

His publications have over 700,000 citations (as of May 2025).
