# Yuki Watanabe

**Affiliation**: NTT Inc., Tokyo, Japan
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Speech intelligibility and psychoacoustics for wearable audio devices; low-complexity signal processing for open-ear headphones.

## Key Contributions

- First author of "Low-frequency harmonic control for speech intelligibility in open-ear headphones" (ICASSP 2026) — [[sources/watanabe-2026-low-frequency-harmonic-control|Watanabe et al. 2026]], a low-complexity post-filter (LFHC) that suppresses the fundamental and emphasizes the 2nd/3rd harmonics to improve intelligibility of open-ear playback in noise.
- Co-author of "Effects of acoustic transparency of wearable audio devices on Audio AR" (CHI 2025) — studied how the high acoustic transparency of open-ear-type wearable devices affects augmented-reality audio.
