# Kyle L. Kashima

**Affiliation**: Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics (CCRMA), Department of Music, Stanford University
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Spectral analysis for polyphonic music transcription; FFT-based filter-bank design.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored *The Bounded-Q Frequency Transform* (STAN-M-28, 1985) — [[sources/kashima-1985-bounded-q-frequency-transform|Kashima & Mont-Reynaud 1985]]. Introduced the [[concepts/bounded-q-transform|Bounded-Q Transform (BQT)]], an FFT-based piecewise-linear approximation to the constant-Q filter bank developed as the front end of the CCRMA intelligent music-transcription system.
