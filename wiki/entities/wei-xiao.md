# Wei Xiao

**Affiliation**: Huawei European Research Center, Munich, Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Multi-channel noise reduction for mobile-phone hands-free communication.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Multi-channel noise reduction for hands-free voice communication on mobile phones" (ICASSP 2017) — [[sources/jin-2017-multichannel-noise-reduction-mobile|Jin et al. 2017]] — proposed an adaptive-coherence noise estimation method with adaptive split-frequency selection and globally MMSE-optimal multi-channel variance estimation, validated on a 3-microphone Huawei Mate 8.
