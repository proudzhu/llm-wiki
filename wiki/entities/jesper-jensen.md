# Jesper Jensen

**Affiliation**: Eriksholm Research Centre, Snekkersten, Denmark; Department of Electronic Systems, Aalborg University, Aalborg, Denmark
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Hearing aid signal processing, speech enhancement, beamforming, speech intelligibility prediction, virtual microphone techniques.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored low-complexity virtual microphone synthesis for hearing aid beamforming (ICASSP 2026)
- Co-authored Hybrid Variable Span Filter (HVSF) for interpretable multi-channel speech enhancement (ICASSP 2026)
- Developed speech intelligibility prediction algorithms (Jensen & Taal 2016)
- Co-authored [[sources/apostolidis-2026-listen-first-output-based-multi-microphone|Apostolidis et al. 2026: Listen first — output-based multi-microphone speech enhancement]] — contributed to the [[concepts/output-based-speech-enhancement|output-based SE paradigm]] using GP-selected [[concepts/mpdr-beamformer|MPDR]] beamforming for hearing aids (arXiv 2026)
