# Joon-Hyuk Chang

**Affiliation**: School of Electronic Engineering, Hanyang University, Seoul, Korea
**Role**: Associate Professor at Hanyang University (at the time of the 2016 paper); IEEE Senior Member
**Research Focus**: Speech coding, speech enhancement, speech recognition, audio coding, and adaptive signal processing.

**Background**: B.S. in electronics engineering, Kyungpook National University (1998); M.S. and Ph.D. in electrical engineering, Seoul National University (2000, 2004). Chief Engineer at Netdus Corp. (2000–2005); postdoctoral researcher at the University of California, Santa Barbara (2004–2005, adaptive signal processing and audio coding); Research Scientist at Korea Institute of Science and Technology (2005, speech recognition); Assistant Professor at Inha University (2005–2011); Hanyang University since 2011.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Packet Loss Concealment Based on Deep Neural Networks for Digital Speech Transmission" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2016) — supervised the first DNN-regression approach to packet loss concealment — [[sources/lee-2016-dnn-plc|Lee & Chang 2016]]
- Co-author of "DNN-Based Geometry-Invariant DOA Estimation With Microphone Positional Encoding and Complexity Gradual Training" (IEEE TASLPRO 2025) — introduced Microphone Positional Encoding (MPE), GI-DOAEnet, and Complexity Gradual Training (CGT) for geometry-invariant DOA estimation — [[sources/baek-2025-geometry-invariant-doa-estimation|Baek, Chang & Cohen 2025]]
