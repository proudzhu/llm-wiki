**Affiliation**: International Audio Laboratories Erlangen, Friedrich-Alexander University Erlangen-Nuremberg (FAU), Germany
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Spatial audio, acoustic signal processing, directional audio coding, low-complexity speech enhancement.

- μNet: ultra-low-memory and low-complexity speech enhancement for embedded DSPs (arXiv 2026)
