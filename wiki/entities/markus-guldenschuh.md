# Markus Guldenschuh

**Affiliation**: Institute of Electronic Music and Acoustics (IEM), Graz University of Technology / University of Music and Performing Arts, Graz, Austria
**Role**: Researcher (Ph.D. candidate at time of 2014 paper)
**Research Focus**: Active noise control headphones, secondary-path modeling and variability, prediction filter design, assessment of ANC headphones

## Biography

Received the Dipl.-Ing. degree (M.Sc. equivalent) in Electrical Engineering from Graz University of Technology, Austria, in 2009. Since 2009 with the Institute of Electronic Music and Acoustics in Graz; pursued the Ph.D. in acoustics and audio engineering at the University of Music and Performing Arts, Austria. Visiting scholar (Nov 2012 – Apr 2013) in the Dynamic Systems and Control Group of Prof. de Callafon at the University of California, San Diego.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Detection of secondary-path irregularities in active noise control headphones" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2014) — [[sources/guldenschuh-2014-secondary-path-irregularities|Guldenschuh & de Callafon 2014]]
- Earlier work on ANC headphone assessment and secondary-path models (ICCE-Berlin 2012; ICSC 2013), establishing the leak-induced low-frequency secondary-path variations underlying the 2014 paper
- "Prediction filter design for active noise cancellation headphones" (IET Signal Processing 2013, with R. Höldrich)
