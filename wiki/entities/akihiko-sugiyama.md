# Akihiko Sugiyama

**Affiliation**: NEC Corporation, Kawasaki, Japan (Multimedia Signal Processing, C&C Media Research Laboratories — per the 1999 paper); also affiliated with academic institutions
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Audio coding, audio signal processing, adaptive filter applications, history of digital audio devices and consumer audio technology; co-developed a robust adaptive microphone-array beamformer.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Audio Signal Processing in the 21st Century" (IEEE Signal Processing Magazine 2023) — TC-AASP 25-year retrospective; led the audio-coding subsection tracing the MPEG family (MPEG-1 → MPEG-H) — [[sources/richard-2023-audio-signal-processing-21st-century|Richard et al. 2023]]
- Authored "The origin of digital information devices: the silicon audio and its family" (APSIPA Transactions on Signal and Information Processing 2018, cited as [4] in the retrospective) — documenting the 1994 Silicon Audio prototype as the precursor to Apple's iPod
- Co-author of "A robust adaptive beamformer for microphone arrays with a blocking matrix using constrained adaptive filters" (IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, vol. 47, no. 10, pp. 2677–2684, 1999) — second author on the [[concepts/coefficient-constrained-adaptive-filter|coefficient-constrained]] [[concepts/adaptive-blocking-matrix|adaptive blocking matrix]] for a [[concepts/gsc-beamformer|GSC]], robust to 20° of [[concepts/steering-vector-error|target-direction error]] with no loss of interference-reduction degrees of freedom — [[sources/hoshuyama-1999-robust-adaptive-beamformer-ccaf|Hoshuyama, Sugiyama & Hirano 1999]]
