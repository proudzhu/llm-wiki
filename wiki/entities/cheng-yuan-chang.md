# Cheng-Yuan Chang

**Affiliation**: Department of Electrical Engineering, Chung Yuan Christian University (CYCU), Taoyuan, Taiwan — Distinguished Professor (since 2021); Department Chairman 2013–2019, Dean 2022–2024; Visiting Professor at Northern Illinois University in 2012
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Signal processing applications and active noise control system design; holds multiple international patents; recipient of the 2018 Future Tech Award from the National Science and Technology Council, Taiwan.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "Integrated Active Noise Control and Hear-Through for Speech Clarity Under Low-Frequency Ambient Noise" (IEEE Trans. Consumer Electronics 2026) — [[sources/huang-2026-integrated-anc-hear-through|Huang, Chang & Kuo 2026]]
- Co-author of "Time-Shift Modeling-Based Hear-Through System for In-Ear Headphones" (IEEE Trans. Consumer Electronics, 2022)
- Co-author of "A Complete Design of Smart Pad That Reduces Snoring" (IEEE Trans. Consumer Electronics, 2023)
