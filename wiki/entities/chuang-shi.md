# Chuang Shi

**Affiliation**: School of Information and Communication Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), Chengdu, China
**Role**: Researcher

## Research Focus

Active noise control, virtual sensing techniques, multi-channel feedforward control systems, ANC casings.

## Key Contributions

- **RP-VS Method**: Proposed the Relative Path based Virtual Sensing (RP-VS) method which unifies AF-VS and RM-VS approaches (MSSP 2020)
- **ANC Casing**: Designed and implemented an ANC casing with (1,4,4) multi-channel feedforward control on a real-time DSP platform (MSSP 2020)
