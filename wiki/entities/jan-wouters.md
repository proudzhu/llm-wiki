# Jan Wouters

**Affiliation**: KU Leuven, Division of Experimental Otorhinolaryngology (ExpORL), Leuven, Belgium
**Role**: Full Professor (Neurosciences Department, since 2001; professor since 1993)
**Research Focus**: Audiology and the auditory system, signal processing for cochlear implants and hearing aids.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Integrated Active Noise Control and Noise Reduction in Hearing Aids" (IEEE Trans. Audio, Speech, Lang. Process. 2010) — the FxMWF scheme integrating NR and ANC in open-fitting hearing aids, developed with the KU Leuven ESAT-SCD group — [[sources/serizel-2010-integrated-anc-nr-hearing-aids|Serizel, Moonen, Wouters & Jensen 2010]]
- Author of about 145 articles in international peer-reviewed journals on audiology, cochlear implants, and hearing-aid signal processing (per the 2010 paper's biography)
- Heads the Laboratory for Experimental ORL and the audiology program at KU Leuven; board member of the International Collegium for Rehabilitative Audiology (ICRA) and member of the International Collegium for ORL (CORLAS)
