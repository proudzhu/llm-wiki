# Henning Schepker

**Affiliation**: Starkey Hearing Technologies, Eden Prairie, MN (also affiliated with University of Oldenburg / Signal Processing Group)
**Role**: Researcher
**Research Focus**: Acoustic feedback cancellation in hearing aids, including adaptive filtering, shadow filter approaches, affine combination of filters, null-steering beamformer-based feedback cancellation for multi-microphone hearing aids, and stability-controlled hybrid AFC schemes.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author on IMU-based step-size control for AFC (ICASSP 2026)
- Null-steering beamformer-based feedback cancellation for multi-microphone hearing aids (IEEE/ACM TASLP, 2019)
- Stability-controlled hybrid adaptive feedback cancellation scheme (JASA, 2018)
- Affine combination of filters for improved AFC (ICASSP 2016)
