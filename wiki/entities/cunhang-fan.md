# Cunhang Fan

**Affiliation**: Professor, School of Computer Science and Technology, Anhui University (Anhui Province Key Laboratory of Multimodal Cognitive Computation), Hefei, China
**Role**: Professor
**Research Focus**: Speech enhancement, noise-robust speech recognition, brain-computer interface, multimodal speech processing

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "SSE-Net: Toward Low-Power-Consumption Spiking Neural Network for Monaural Speech Enhancement" (IEEE/ACM TASLP 2026) — [[sources/liu-2026-sse-net|Liu et al. 2026]]
- Lead author of BSDB-Net: band-split dual-branch network with selective state space mechanism for monaural speech enhancement (AAAI 2025)
- Research on noise-robust end-to-end speech recognition, speech separation, and auditory attention decoding (with Jianhua Tao, Jiangyan Yi, Zhao Lv, Chengshi Zheng)
- Co-authored "LiSenNet: Lightweight Sub-band and Dual-Path Modeling for Real-Time Speech Enhancement" (ICASSP 2025) — sub-band conv + dual-path recurrent SE model at 37k params / 56M MACs/s, PESQ 3.07 on VoiceBank+DEMAND — [[sources/yan-2025-lisennet|Yan et al. 2025]]
