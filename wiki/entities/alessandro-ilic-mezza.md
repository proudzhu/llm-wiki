# Alessandro Ilic Mezza

**Affiliation**: Dipartimento di Elettronica, Informazione e Bioingegneria (DEIB), Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy
**Role**: Researcher (Graduate Student Member, IEEE)
**Research Focus**: Real-time audio signal processing, deep learning for audio, packet loss concealment for networked music performance, hybrid DSP/neural architectures.

## Key Contributions

- Lead author of "Hybrid Packet Loss Concealment for Real-Time Networked Music Applications" (IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing 2024) — proposes **PARCnet**, a parallel linear-AR + feed-forward residual-network PLC system (416 k params, real-time at 32 kHz on CPU) that wins a MUSHRA test by 21.7 points over AR(128) — [[sources/mezza-2024-hybrid-plc|Mezza et al. 2024]]
- Co-author of "A deep learning approach for low-latency packet loss concealment of audio signals in networked music performance applications" (Conf. Open Innovations Assoc. 2020) — the NMP-PLC neural baseline that later work (including PARCnet) benchmarks against
- Doctoral research at Politecnico di Milano on deep generative and predictive models for low-latency audio communications.
