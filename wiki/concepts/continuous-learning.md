# Continuous Learning (LLM)

In the context of large language model research, **continuous learning** refers to a model's ability to keep learning over extended periods of time (analogous to how humans learn continuously), rather than only through one-shot intensive training. The term is used here as framed by [[entities/wenfeng-liang|Liang Wenfeng]] in his 2026 investor exchange meeting, where he identifies it as the **next bottleneck** on the path to AGI — the step after Agents in his [[concepts/agi-roadmap-staircase|AGI staircase]].

## Definition (Per Liang Wenfeng)

> "怎么让模型可以持续地学习，⽽不是说你要给它⼀个很强的训练，它应该能够像⼈⼀样做⼀个⽐较⻓时间的持续学习"

How to enable a model to learn continuously — rather than giving it one intensive training round, it should be able to do longer-term continuous learning like a human.

## Why It Matters

- **Post-Agent bottleneck** — agents will solve all problems solvable by agents, then plateau. Continuous learning is the next obstacle.
- **Path to self-iteration** — only after continuous learning is solved can a model develop its own next version (the "singularity" step in the AGI staircase).
- **Currently unsolved globally** — Liang: "the whole world is still in the exploration stage, no one has found a good method yet."

## Distinctive Framing

Liang's distinctive contribution to the framing:

1. **It is a problem, not a technique** — "Continuous learning may not be one technique; it is a problem. How to solve this problem may involve many techniques — not one thing, but many things."
2. **It is both an algorithm and an engineering problem** — many methods exist, many candidate ideas look promising, but none has yet worked through.
3. **It does not consume heavy resources** — "CoT does not consume resources. Continuous learning research needs ideas, not cards or people — it's not a project that needs many people working on it constantly."
4. **Research method is "摸奖" (lottery / "feeling for prizes")** — anyone can attempt it; the barrier is low; what comes out is uncertain. DeepSeek's distinctive posture is treating this as important and spending time discussing / thinking about it, even though no resources need to be allocated.

## Relationship to Other Frontiers

- **Recursive self-improvement** — the broader research direction; continuous learning is the prerequisite. Liang treats the two as the same problem rather than separate techniques.
- **Coding agent** — DeepSeek's current highest-priority vertical (2026); related but distinct from continuous learning as a research target.
- **Model self-improvement** — once continuous learning is solved, the model can improve itself; this is the "singularity" step in the staircase.
