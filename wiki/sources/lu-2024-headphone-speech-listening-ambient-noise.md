# Lu et al. (2024): Headphone Speech Listening Based on Ambient Noise

> **Patent**: [US20240005903A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240005903A1/en) ([8IGY7CNW](zotero://select/items/0_8IGY7CNW))
> **Assignee**: Apple Inc.

## Overview

This patent describes a system and method for enhancing speech listening in headphones by dynamically adjusting the **[[wiki/concepts/transparency-mode|Transparency Mode]]** based on ambient noise levels and the presence of speech signals.

## Key Innovations

1. **Ambient Noise Dependent Transparency**: The system automatically scales the pass-through of external sound based on the spectral characteristics and energy level of the ambient noise.
2. **Speech Prioritization**: Specifically targets the frequency bands corresponding to human speech to ensure clarity while still suppressing distracting background noise (e.g., wind or engine roar).
3. **Threshold-Based Activation**: Uses configurable thresholds to trigger transitions between high-isolation ANC and high-awareness transparency, preventing sudden or jarring audio changes for the user.

## Significance

This patent represents a shift from "static" transparency modes to "context-aware" awareness systems, where the headphone acts as an active filter for the environment, prioritizing human interaction over ambient noise reduction.
